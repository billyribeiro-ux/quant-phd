Rungs 1 and 2

Rung 1 · Worked example

Consider an operating company with a 5-year explicit forecast. Operating earnings (\(\text{EBIT}\)) grow from $120.0M to $180.0M, with a corporate marginal tax rate of \(\tau_c = 25\%\). Depreciation grows from $25.0M to $35.0M, while capital expenditures grow from $40.0M to $50.0M and working capital requirements expand by $10.0M to $16.0M annually.

In year 1, \(\text{NOPAT}_1 = 120 \times (1 - 0.25) = 90.0\) million dollars. Free cash flow to the firm is

\[ \text{FCFF}_1 = 90.0 + 25.0 - 40.0 - 10.0 = 65.0\text{ million dollars.} \]

Across the 5-year horizon, explicit cash flows are \([65.00, 75.25, 83.50, 92.75, 104.00]\) million dollars. Discounting at \(\text{WACC} = 9.0\%\) yields an explicit present value of $320.75M. Assuming a terminal sustainable growth rate of \(g = 2.50\%\), terminal value at horizon \(H = 5\) is

\[ TV_5 = \frac{104.00 \times (1 + 0.025)}{0.090 - 0.025} = \frac{106.60}{0.065} = 1640.00\text{ million dollars.} \]

Discounted to \(t=0\), \(\text{PV}(TV_5) = 1640.00 / (1.09)^5 = 1065.89\) million dollars, representing 76.87% of total enterprise value (\(EV = 320.75 + 1065.89 = 1386.63\) million dollars). Deducting net debt of $270.0M ($350.0M total debt less $80.0M cash), minority interest of $25.0M, preferred stock of $15.0M, and adding non-operating assets of $30.0M yields common equity value of $1106.63M, or $22.13 per share across 50.0 million shares.
