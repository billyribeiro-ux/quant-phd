Lab 1 · Worked example: Corporate Hedging Program & CFaR Simulation

Walks through the complete multi-currency hedging pipeline, demonstrating transaction exposure aggregation, portfolio cash flow variance calculation, 95% CFaR estimation, and cross-border valuation equivalence.

"""CF9 project lab 1 (rung 1, worked example): Corporate Hedging Program & CFaR Simulation.

SIMULATED: Meridian Global Industries multi-currency exposure and hedging pipeline.
Demonstrates the full corporate hedging workflow:
1. Extraction of net transaction exposure across EUR, JPY, and GBP.
2. Translation to USD base currency and portfolio covariance aggregation.
3. Minimum-variance hedge ratio determination and residual risk calculation.
4. Analytical 95% Cash-Flow-at-Risk (CFaR) and shortfall probability below capex hurdle.
5. ASC 815 / IFRS 9 retrospective dollar-offset hedge accounting compliance.
6. Cross-border capital budgeting valuation equivalence.
Standard library only; deterministic execution.
"""

from __future__ import annotations

import math
from statistics import NormalDist


def main() -> None:
    print("CF9 Project Lab 1: Corporate Hedging Program (Worked Example)")
    print("===========================================================")

    # 1. Foreign Currency Exposures (Millions)
    receivables = {"EUR": 30.0, "JPY": 3500.0, "GBP": 18.0}
    payables = {"EUR": 10.0, "JPY": 1200.0, "GBP": 6.0}
    spot_rates = {"EUR": 1.1000, "JPY": 0.007000, "GBP": 1.2800}

    currencies = ["EUR", "JPY", "GBP"]
    net_fc = {c: receivables[c] - payables[c] for c in currencies}
    usd_exp = [net_fc[c] * spot_rates[c] for c in currencies]

    print("1. Net Contractual Transaction Exposure:")
    for i, c in enumerate(currencies):
        print(
            f"   {c}: Receivables = {receivables[c]:,.1f}M, Payables = {payables[c]:,.1f}M -> "
            f"Net = {net_fc[c]:,.1f}M (USD ${usd_exp[i]:,.2f}M)"
        )
    total_gross_usd = sum(usd_exp)
    domestic_costs = 5.0
    expected_cf = total_gross_usd - domestic_costs
    print(f"   Total Gross Foreign Cash Flow:   ${total_gross_usd:,.2f}M")
    print(f"   Domestic USD Operating Costs:    ${domestic_costs:,.2f}M")
    print(f"   Net Expected Operating Cash Flow:${expected_cf:,.2f}M")

    # 2. Covariance Matrix (Annualized FX returns)
    vols = [0.10, 0.12, 0.09]
    corr = [
        [1.0, 0.35, 0.65],
        [0.35, 1.0, 0.30],
        [0.65, 0.30, 1.0],
    ]
    cov_matrix = [[corr[i][j] * vols[i] * vols[j] for j in range(3)] for i in range(3)]

    # Unhedged portfolio variance: Var(CF) = w^T * Sigma * w
    var_unhedged = sum(usd_exp[i] * usd_exp[j] * cov_matrix[i][j] for i in range(3) for j in range(3))
    std_unhedged = math.sqrt(var_unhedged)

    print("\n2. Portfolio Cash Flow Risk (Unhedged):")
    print(f"   Cash Flow Variance:              {var_unhedged:.4f} ($M^2)")
    print(f"   Cash Flow Standard Deviation:    ${std_unhedged:.2f}M")

    # 3. Minimum-Variance Hedging Program
    # Forward cover on EUR (85%), JPY (80%), GBP (90%)
    hedge_ratios = [0.85, 0.80, 0.90]
    residual_weights = [usd_exp[i] * (1.0 - hedge_ratios[i]) for i in range(3)]
    var_hedged = sum(residual_weights[i] * residual_weights[j] * cov_matrix[i][j] for i in range(3) for j in range(3))
    std_hedged = math.sqrt(var_hedged)
    hedge_eff = 1.0 - (var_hedged / var_unhedged)

    print("\n3. Hedged Portfolio Risk (Minimum-Variance Cover):")
    print(f"   Hedged Cash Flow Variance:       {var_hedged:.4f} ($M^2)")
    print(f"   Hedged Cash Flow Std Dev:        ${std_hedged:.2f}M")
    print(f"   Hedge Effectiveness (R^2):       {hedge_eff:.2%}")

    # 4. Cash-Flow-at-Risk (CFaR 95%) and Shortfall Below Hurdle K
    hurdle_k = 42.0  # Debt service $22M + critical capex $20M
    dist = NormalDist()
    z_95 = dist.inv_cdf(0.95)

    cfar_unhedged = z_95 * std_unhedged
    worst_cf_unhedged = expected_cf - cfar_unhedged
    prob_shortfall_unhedged = dist.cdf((hurdle_k - expected_cf) / std_unhedged)

    cfar_hedged = z_95 * std_hedged
    worst_cf_hedged = expected_cf - cfar_hedged
    prob_shortfall_hedged = dist.cdf((hurdle_k - expected_cf) / std_hedged)

    print("\n4. Cash-Flow-at-Risk Analysis (95% Confidence, Hurdle = $42.00M):")
    print(f"   Unhedged 95% CFaR Shortfall:     ${cfar_unhedged:.2f}M")
    worst_str_un = f"${worst_cf_unhedged:.2f}M"
    print(f"   Unhedged Worst-Case 5% Cash Flow:{worst_str_un} (Shortfall Risk = {prob_shortfall_unhedged:.2%})")
    worst_str_hd = f"${worst_cf_hedged:.2f}M"
    print(f"   Hedged 95% CFaR Shortfall:       ${cfar_hedged:.2f}M")
    print(f"   Hedged Worst-Case 5% Cash Flow:  {worst_str_hd} (Shortfall Risk = {prob_shortfall_hedged:.4%})")

    # 5. Hedge Accounting Compliance (ASC 815 / IFRS 9)
    delta_deriv = 9.80  # Derivative fair value gain
    delta_item = -10.50  # Hedged item foreign revenue drop
    offset_ratio = abs(delta_deriv) / abs(delta_item)
    qualified = 0.80 <= offset_ratio <= 1.25

    print("\n5. Hedge Accounting Qualification (80%-125% Corridor):")
    print(f"   Derivative Delta:                +${delta_deriv:.2f}M")
    print(f"   Hedged Item Delta:               ${delta_item:.2f}M")
    print(f"   Retrospective Dollar-Offset:     {offset_ratio:.2%}")
    print(f"   Cash Flow Hedge Qualified:       {qualified}")

    # 6. Cross-Border Project Valuation Parity
    initial_eur = 50.0
    cfs_eur = [14.0, 18.0, 22.0, 20.0]
    r_usd = 0.05
    r_eur = 0.03
    wacc_usd = 0.10
    wacc_eur = ((1.0 + wacc_usd) * (1.0 + r_eur) / (1.0 + r_usd)) - 1.0

    pv_eur = sum(cf / ((1.0 + wacc_eur) ** (t + 1)) for t, cf in enumerate(cfs_eur))
    npv_foreign_usd = (-initial_eur + pv_eur) * spot_rates["EUR"]

    forward_rates = [spot_rates["EUR"] * (((1.0 + r_usd) / (1.0 + r_eur)) ** (t + 1)) for t in range(4)]
    cfs_usd = [cf * fwd for cf, fwd in zip(cfs_eur, forward_rates, strict=True)]
    pv_usd = sum(cf / ((1.0 + wacc_usd) ** (t + 1)) for t, cf in enumerate(cfs_usd))
    npv_home_usd = (-initial_eur * spot_rates["EUR"]) + pv_usd

    print("\n6. Cross-Border Capital Budgeting Valuation:")
    print(f"   Foreign Currency Method NPV:     ${npv_foreign_usd:,.2f}M")
    print(f"   Home Currency Method NPV:        ${npv_home_usd:,.2f}M")
    print(f"   Parity Equivalence Confirmed:    {abs(npv_foreign_usd - npv_home_usd) < 1e-4}")


main()

Lab 3 · Independent project: Seed 42 benchmark evaluation

Generates deterministic benchmark numbers for seed 42, establishing the exact portfolio variance, Cash-Flow-at-Risk, and project valuation targets that the student's risk committee memo must evaluate.

"""CF9 project lab 3 (rung 3 check): Reference numbers for independent memo evaluation.

SIMULATED: Meridian Global Industries generated deterministically from seed 42.
Standard library only; deterministic execution.
Computes and prints the exact baseline table, portfolio variance, CFaR,
and cross-border project valuation that the student's project memo must evaluate.
"""

from __future__ import annotations

import math
import random
from statistics import NormalDist
from typing import Any

CURRENCIES = ["EUR", "JPY", "GBP"]


def make_instance(seed: int) -> dict[str, Any]:
    """Generate a deterministic, valid corporate hedging instance for the given seed."""
    rng = random.Random(seed)

    eur_rec = round(rng.uniform(22.0, 35.0), 2)
    eur_pay = round(rng.uniform(8.0, 14.0), 2)
    jpy_rec = round(rng.uniform(2500.0, 4200.0), 1)
    jpy_pay = round(rng.uniform(800.0, 1600.0), 1)
    gbp_rec = round(rng.uniform(14.0, 24.0), 2)
    gbp_pay = round(rng.uniform(4.0, 9.0), 2)

    s_eur = round(rng.uniform(1.05, 1.15), 4)
    s_jpy = round(rng.uniform(0.0065, 0.0075), 6)
    s_gbp = round(rng.uniform(1.24, 1.34), 4)

    vol_eur = round(rng.uniform(0.09, 0.12), 4)
    vol_jpy = round(rng.uniform(0.10, 0.14), 4)
    vol_gbp = round(rng.uniform(0.08, 0.11), 4)
    vols = [vol_eur, vol_jpy, vol_gbp]

    rho_ej = round(rng.uniform(0.25, 0.45), 4)
    rho_eg = round(rng.uniform(0.55, 0.75), 4)
    rho_jg = round(rng.uniform(0.20, 0.40), 4)

    corr_matrix = [
        [1.0, rho_ej, rho_eg],
        [rho_ej, 1.0, rho_jg],
        [rho_eg, rho_jg, 1.0],
    ]

    cov_matrix: list[list[float]] = []
    for i in range(3):
        row: list[float] = []
        for j in range(3):
            cov_ij = corr_matrix[i][j] * vols[i] * vols[j]
            row.append(round(cov_ij, 8))
        cov_matrix.append(row)

    hurdle = round(rng.uniform(42.0, 55.0), 2)
    domestic_costs_usd = round(rng.uniform(15.0, 25.0), 2)

    delta_exposure = round(rng.uniform(-12.0, -8.0), 2)
    ratio = round(rng.uniform(0.75, 1.30), 4)
    delta_derivative = round(abs(delta_exposure) * ratio, 2)

    initial_outlay_eur = round(rng.uniform(40.0, 60.0), 2)
    cf_eur_1 = round(rng.uniform(12.0, 18.0), 2)
    cf_eur_2 = round(rng.uniform(16.0, 24.0), 2)
    cf_eur_3 = round(rng.uniform(20.0, 28.0), 2)
    cf_eur_4 = round(rng.uniform(18.0, 25.0), 2)
    project_cfs_eur = [cf_eur_1, cf_eur_2, cf_eur_3, cf_eur_4]

    r_usd = round(rng.uniform(0.045, 0.055), 4)
    r_eur = round(rng.uniform(0.025, 0.035), 4)
    wacc_usd = round(rng.uniform(0.095, 0.115), 4)

    return {
        "seed": seed,
        "currencies": list(CURRENCIES),
        "receivables": [eur_rec, jpy_rec, gbp_rec],
        "payables": [eur_pay, jpy_pay, gbp_pay],
        "spot_rates": [s_eur, s_jpy, s_gbp],
        "fx_volatilities": [vol_eur, vol_jpy, vol_gbp],
        "correlation_matrix": corr_matrix,
        "covariance_matrix": cov_matrix,
        "hurdle_cash_flow": hurdle,
        "domestic_costs_usd": domestic_costs_usd,
        "delta_exposure": delta_exposure,
        "delta_derivative": delta_derivative,
        "initial_outlay_eur": initial_outlay_eur,
        "project_cfs_eur": project_cfs_eur,
        "r_usd": r_usd,
        "r_eur": r_eur,
        "wacc_usd": wacc_usd,
    }


def compute_net_transaction_exposure(receivables: dict[str, float], payables: dict[str, float]) -> dict[str, float]:
    """Calculate net contractual foreign currency exposure."""
    return {c: receivables[c] - payables[c] for c in receivables}


def compute_usd_exposure(net_fc_exposure: dict[str, float], spot_rates: dict[str, float]) -> dict[str, float]:
    """Translate foreign currency net exposures to domestic USD base currency."""
    return {c: net_fc_exposure[c] * spot_rates[c] for c in net_fc_exposure}


def portfolio_cash_flow_variance(weights: list[float], cov_matrix: list[list[float]]) -> float:
    """Calculate multi-currency portfolio cash flow variance: Var(CF) = w^T * Sigma * w."""
    n = len(weights)
    return sum(weights[i] * weights[j] * cov_matrix[i][j] for i in range(n) for j in range(n))


def evaluate_hedge_effectiveness(var_unhedged: float, var_hedged: float) -> float:
    """Calculate variance reduction percentage."""
    return 1.0 - (var_hedged / var_unhedged)


def compute_cfar(
    mean_cf: float,
    std_cf: float,
    alpha: float = 0.05,
    hurdle: float | None = None,
) -> dict[str, float]:
    """Compute analytical Cash-Flow-at-Risk under normality."""
    dist = NormalDist()
    z_alpha = dist.inv_cdf(1.0 - alpha)
    cfar_amount = z_alpha * std_cf
    worst_case_cf = mean_cf - cfar_amount
    prob_shortfall = 0.0
    if hurdle is not None:
        z_hurdle = (hurdle - mean_cf) / std_cf
        prob_shortfall = dist.cdf(z_hurdle)
    return {
        "mean_cf": mean_cf,
        "std_cf": std_cf,
        "cfar": cfar_amount,
        "worst_case_cf": worst_case_cf,
        "prob_shortfall": prob_shortfall,
    }


def hedge_accounting_test(delta_derivative: float, delta_exposure: float) -> dict[str, Any]:
    """Evaluate retrospective dollar-offset ratio for hedge accounting."""
    ratio = abs(delta_derivative) / abs(delta_exposure)
    qualified = 0.80 <= ratio <= 1.25
    return {
        "dollar_offset_ratio": ratio,
        "is_qualified": qualified,
    }


def cross_border_npv(
    initial_outlay_foreign: float,
    foreign_cfs: list[float],
    spot_fx: float,
    wacc_home: float,
    r_home: float,
    r_foreign: float,
    method: str = "foreign",
) -> float:
    """Calculate cross-border capital budgeting NPV in domestic currency."""
    if method == "foreign":
        wacc_foreign = ((1.0 + wacc_home) * (1.0 + r_foreign) / (1.0 + r_home)) - 1.0
        pv_foreign = sum(cf / ((1.0 + wacc_foreign) ** (t + 1)) for t, cf in enumerate(foreign_cfs))
        npv_foreign = -initial_outlay_foreign + pv_foreign
        return npv_foreign * spot_fx

    forward_rates = [spot_fx * (((1.0 + r_home) / (1.0 + r_foreign)) ** (t + 1)) for t in range(len(foreign_cfs))]
    home_cfs = [cf * fwd for cf, fwd in zip(foreign_cfs, forward_rates, strict=True)]
    pv_home = sum(cf / ((1.0 + wacc_home) ** (t + 1)) for t, cf in enumerate(home_cfs))
    return (-initial_outlay_foreign * spot_fx) + pv_home


def main() -> None:
    print("CF9 Project Lab 3: Seed 42 Reference Benchmark")
    print("==============================================")

    seed = 42
    inst = make_instance(seed)

    currencies = inst["currencies"]
    rec = dict(zip(currencies, inst["receivables"], strict=True))
    pay = dict(zip(currencies, inst["payables"], strict=True))
    spots = dict(zip(currencies, inst["spot_rates"], strict=True))
    cov_matrix = inst["covariance_matrix"]

    # 1. Net exposures
    net_fc = compute_net_transaction_exposure(rec, pay)
    usd_exp = compute_usd_exposure(net_fc, spots)
    exp_vector = [usd_exp[c] for c in currencies]

    print("1. Net Exposures (Seed 42):")
    for c in currencies:
        print(
            f"   {c}: Rec = {rec[c]:,.2f}M, Pay = {pay[c]:,.2f}M -> "
            f"Net = {net_fc[c]:,.2f}M (Spot = {spots[c]:.6f}, USD = ${usd_exp[c]:,.2f}M)"
        )

    gross_usd = sum(exp_vector)
    domestic_costs = inst["domestic_costs_usd"]
    expected_cf = gross_usd - domestic_costs
    print(f"   Total Gross Foreign Cash Flow:   ${gross_usd:,.2f}M")
    print(f"   Domestic Operating Costs:        ${domestic_costs:,.2f}M")
    print(f"   Expected Net Cash Flow:          ${expected_cf:,.2f}M")

    var_unhedged = portfolio_cash_flow_variance(exp_vector, cov_matrix)

    print(f"   Cash Flow Variance:              {var_unhedged:.4f}")
    print(f"   Cash Flow Volatility:            ${std_unhedged:.2f}M")

    # 3. Minimum-variance hedge (80% across all currencies)
    hedged_weights = [x * 0.20 for x in exp_vector]
    var_hedged = portfolio_cash_flow_variance(hedged_weights, cov_matrix)
    std_hedged = math.sqrt(var_hedged)
    eff = evaluate_hedge_effectiveness(var_unhedged, var_hedged)

    print("\n3. Hedged Portfolio Risk (80% Cover Benchmark):")

Rung 3 · Specification

Context
The Chief Financial Officer and Risk Committee of Meridian Global Industries require an algorithmic hedging simulation engine (reference.py) and an executive risk committee memorandum (memo.md). The multinational firm generates substantial operating revenues in EUR, JPY, and GBP with reporting currency in USD. The firm faces contractual transaction exposures, economic operating risks, and debt service obligations. The treasury requires an automated quantitative system to measure net exposure, compute minimum-variance hedge ratios, calculate portfolio Cash-Flow-at-Risk (CFaR), evaluate retrospective hedge accounting compliance under ASC 815 and IFRS 9, and appraise international capital expenditure projects.
Inputs
The estimation engine ingests a multi-currency corporate profile generated by make_instance(seed: int):
  1. Foreign cash flows: Dictionaries of committed quarterly receivables (receivables) and committed quarterly payables (payables) for EUR, JPY, and GBP.
  2. Market parameters: Spot exchange rates in USD per foreign currency unit (spot_rates), annualized currency volatilities, and a \(3 \times 3\) positive definite covariance matrix (covariance_matrix).
  3. Operational hurdles: Critical hurdle cash flow (hurdle_cash_flow) for debt service and essential capital expenditures, and domestic USD operating costs.
  4. Hedge accounting shocks: Fair value changes for derivatives (delta_derivative) and underlying hedged items (delta_exposure).
  5. Capital budgeting parameters: Initial foreign outlay, 4-year foreign cash flows, domestic WACC, and interest rates for home and foreign economies.
Required outputs
Two core deliverables:
  • reference.py: Fully typed standard-library Python module implementing eight required functions: compute_net_transaction_exposure, compute_usd_exposure, minimum_variance_hedge_ratio, portfolio_cash_flow_variance, compute_cfar, evaluate_hedge_effectiveness, hedge_accounting_test, and cross_border_npv.
  • memo.md: Executive Risk Committee memorandum (2–3 pages) detailing the firm's unhedged risk profile, optimal hedging recommendations across instruments, Cash-Flow-at-Risk shortfall probabilities, hedge accounting documentation, and cross-border investment appraisal.
Constraints
  • Standard library only: no external dependencies. Use math and statistics.NormalDist.
  • Portfolio variance must strictly incorporate all off-diagonal cross-currency covariance terms: \(\Var(CF) = w^T \Sigma w\).
  • Cash-Flow-at-Risk must compute the maximum dollar shortfall at confidence level \(1 - \alpha\): \(CFaR = z_{1-\alpha} \times \sigma\), with worst-case cash flow \(\mu - CFaR\).
  • Hedge accounting test must enforce the exact 80% to 125% [0.80, 1.25] retrospective effectiveness corridor under ASC 815 / IFRS 9.
  • Cross-border project valuation must demonstrate exact parity equivalence between the Foreign-Currency and Home-Currency DCF methods.
  • All invalid inputs (e.g. mismatched currency keys, negative cash flows, non-positive spot rates, non-positive futures variance, dimension mismatches, alpha outside \((0, 0.5)\)) must raise ValueError.
Grading
Grading: both autograder and rubric. The autograder executes the automated test suite across 50 pseudo-random instance seeds to verify exact numerical accuracy, covariance aggregation, and error handling. The rubric evaluates the executive risk committee memorandum across five analytical criteria.

Exemplar benchmarks

Exemplar A · Exemplary (Level 4 across all criteria)

Executive Summary: Meridian Global Industries generates annual foreign cash flows of €22.16M, ¥1,988.9M, and £13.98M, translating to $56.27M in gross revenues and $39.07M in expected net cash flow after $17.20M in domestic operating expenses. Under an unhedged policy, quarterly cash flow variance is 18.0975 (\(\sigma = \$4.25\text{M}\)), creating an unhedged 95% Cash-Flow-at-Risk (CFaR) of $7.00M and a worst-case cash flow of $32.07M. Given our critical debt service and essential capital expenditure hurdle of $49.08M, the unhedged firm faces an acute shortfall probability of 99.07%, severely threatening debt covenants and forcing costly external capital raising (Froot, Scharfstein & Stein 1993).

Hedging Strategy & Effectiveness: We recommend an 80% layered forward cover program across EUR, JPY, and GBP. This program compresses cash flow variance by 96.00% to 0.7239 (\(\sigma = \$0.85\text{M}\)), lowering the 95% CFaR to $1.40M. All derivative transactions satisfy ASC 815 and IFRS 9 criteria for cash flow hedge accounting, exhibiting a retrospective dollar-offset ratio of 119.50% (within the mandatory 80%–125% corridor). Translation gains and losses are deferred in Other Comprehensive Income (OCI), shielding reported operating net income from artificial volatility. Furthermore, appraisal of the European manufacturing expansion confirms a positive net present value of $31.47M under both Foreign-Currency and Home-Currency DCF valuation approaches, remaining robust to a +200 bps country risk spread.

Treasury Policy & Governance: The committee strictly prohibits "selective hedging" (discretionary market timing). Treasury execution will be governed by defined benchmark bands (70%–90% coverage), pre-approved ISDA master agreements with Tier-1 banking counterparties, and independent monthly compliance reporting to the Board Audit & Risk Committee.

Exemplar B · Developing (Level 2 across all criteria)

Summary: The firm has foreign sales in Europe, Japan, and Britain. The unhedged risk is high because exchange rates fluctuate. We should enter into currency forwards to lock in exchange rates and protect our cash flow from dropping.

Calculations: The net exposures are €22.16M, ¥1,988.9M, and £13.98M. If we hedge 80%, our risk is lower. The dollar-offset ratio is 119.5%, which is between 80% and 125%, so we get hedge accounting. The capital budgeting project in Germany has a positive NPV of $31.47M.

Limitations: The memo lacks a mathematical formulation of multi-currency covariance, fails to derive the minimum-variance hedge ratio, provides superficial treatment of CFaR without linking to covenant breach probabilities, and does not establish clear governance rules against speculative selective hedging.
