Change of Measure

Continuous-time derivative pricing rests on equivalent martingale measures. When the underlying probability measure changes, the drift of an Itô diffusion shifts while its quadratic variation remains invariant. This module develops the mathematical machinery of measure transformations: establishing Girsanov's theorem, identifying the market price of risk, proving the martingale representation theorem and market completeness, constructing the generalized numeraire framework of Geman, El Karoui & Rochet (1995), formulating the \(T\)-forward measure to decouple stochastic rates from payoffs, solving Margrabe's exchange option in closed form, and deriving covariance quanto adjustments for cross-currency contracts.

  • Prerequisites: SC2 Itô Calculus & SDEs. Prerequisite closure includes SC2, SC1, M6, M5, M4, M2, M1 and P0.1.
  • Unlocks: SC4 PDEs & Feynman–Kac, SC5 Jump Processes, SC6 Stochastic Control, CR1 Credit Risk Modeling, STUDIO-SC.
  • Project: Numeraire toolkit and derivations workbook, graded by autograder and rubric
  • Data: none. Pure continuous-time theory and numerical validation engine using exact closed forms and simulated paths labeled SIMULATED.

56 h lessons · 24 h project (30% of the 80 h module)

Objectives

By the end of SC3 you can:

  1. State and prove Girsanov's theorem for continuous-time Brownian martingales, verifying Novikov's condition for Doléans-Dade exponential densities (Lesson 1).
  2. Derive the market price of risk for single-asset and multi-asset diffusion markets, establishing the existence of equivalent martingale measures that eliminate excess drift (Lesson 2).
  3. Formulate the martingale representation theorem in Brownian filtrations, and construct replicating self-financing portfolios that establish complete market replication (Lesson 3).
  4. Apply the change of numeraire theorem of Geman, El Karoui & Rochet (1995) to simplify complex option payoffs, decomposing the Black-Scholes formula into dual-measure probabilities (Lesson 4).
  5. Construct the \(T\)-forward measure using zero-coupon bond numeraires, and price European options under stochastic interest rate models by factoring out stochastic discounting (Lesson 5).
  6. Derive the closed-form Margrabe exchange option formula, and prove its homogeneity of degree 1 and put-call exchange symmetry (Lesson 6).
  7. Calculate exact quanto drift adjustments for cross-currency derivatives, and implement Girsanov importance sampling for rare-event deep out-of-the-money option pricing (Lesson 7).

Placement diagnostic

Nine questions. Seven evaluate prerequisites from SC2, SC1, M6, M4 and M2. Two are skill checks, marked as such, testing the technical derivation and numeraire skills taught in rungs 1 and 2 of the project ladder. The mastery bar is 80 percent (requiring at least eight correct answers out of nine). Skipping rungs 1 and 2 requires meeting the 80 percent mastery bar and answering both skill checks correctly.

  1. Under Itô's lemma, what is the differential of \(f(t, S_t) = \log S_t\) when \(dS_t = \mu S_t dt + \sigma S_t dB_t\)?

    Options

    By SC2 (Itô's lemma), \(d\log S = (1/S)dS - \tfrac{1}{2}(1/S^2)(dS)^2 = (\mu - \tfrac{1}{2}\sigma^2)dt + \sigma dB_t\).

  2. In measure theory (M6), what condition guarantees the existence of a unique Radon–Nikodym derivative \(dQ/dP\)?

    Options

    By the Radon–Nikodym theorem (M6), absolute continuity \(Q \ll P\) on a \(\sigma\)-finite space guarantees a non-negative density \(dQ/dP\).

  3. For standard Brownian motion \((B_t)_{t \ge 0}\), what is the rate of quadratic variation accumulation \(d[B, B]_t / dt\)?

    By SC1 (quadratic variation), the quadratic variation of standard Brownian motion satisfies \([B, B]_t = t\), so \(d[B, B]_t = dt\).

  4. When two probability measures \(P\) and \(Q\) satisfy \(P(A) = 0 \iff Q(A) = 0\) for all events \(A \in \mathcal{F}\), what are they called?

    Options

    Two measures are equivalent if they share the exact same null sets (M6, Radon–Nikodym theorem).

  5. By the tower property of conditional expectation (M6), what is \(\E[\E[X \mid \mathcal{F}_t] \mid \mathcal{F}_s]\) for \(s \le t\)?

    Options

    The smaller \(\sigma\)-algebra wins: conditioning on the coarse filtration \(\mathcal{F}_s\) after the finer \(\mathcal{F}_t\) leaves \(\E[X \mid \mathcal{F}_s]\).

  6. What is the expectation of the Itô stochastic integral \(\E[\int_0^H B_s dB_s]\) under the Brownian filtration?

    By SC2 (Itô integral), stochastic integrals of square-integrable adapted integrands have zero expectation.

  7. For two correlated Brownian motions with \(d\langle B_1, B_2 \rangle_t = \rho dt\), what is the cross quadratic covariation \([B_1, B_2]_H\)?

    Options

    By polarization of quadratic variation, \([B_1, B_2]_H = \rho H\).

  8. Skill check (rung 1): In the Margrabe exchange option formula for assets with volatilities \(\sigma_1, \sigma_2\) and correlation \(\rho\), what is the effective volatility \(\sigma_M\) of the relative price \(S_1 / S_2\)?

    Options

    Applying Itô's formula to \(\log(S_1/S_2) = \log S_1 - \log S_2\), the diffusion increment is \(\sigma_1 dB_1 - \sigma_2 dB_2\), whose variance rate is \(\sigma_1^2 - 2\rho\sigma_1\sigma_2 + \sigma_2^2\).

  9. Skill check (rung 2): Under the domestic risk-neutral measure \(\mathbb{Q}^d\), what is the drift of a foreign asset with foreign risk-free rate \(r_f\), volatility \(\sigma_S\), and FX volatility \(\sigma_X\) with correlation \(\rho_{SX}\)?

    Options

    To ensure that the domestic-currency value of the foreign asset \(X_t S^f_t\) grows at the domestic risk-free rate \(r_d\), the foreign asset drift under \(\mathbb{Q}^d\) must equal \(r_f - \rho_{SX}\sigma_S\sigma_X\).

The project ladder

The module concludes with the Numeraire toolkit project (24 hours). Rungs 1 and 2 develop the algebraic and numerical verification workflow tested in the placement diagnostic. Meeting the placement mastery bar (80%) and answering both skill checks permits skipping directly to Rung 3.

  1. Worked example

    Derivation and numerical verification of the Margrabe exchange option formula on a SIMULATED instance, annotated with the justification for every step.

  2. Completion problem

    Complete the missing Margrabe effective volatility and quanto drift adjustment in the verification script, graded instantly in the browser.

  3. Independent project

    Implement the full numeraire toolkit module numeraire_toolkit.py, derive both pricing equations under their respective numeraire measures, and produce a formal derivations workbook. Graded by autograder and rubric. Project specification.

  4. Stretch

    Extend the numeraire framework to three-asset spread options and cross-currency quanto exchange contracts under coupled measure changes.

Data status

No real market data is embedded in SC3. As a foundational stochastic calculus and continuous-time asset pricing module, all laboratory demonstrations, project checks, and autograder tests compute from exact closed-form analytical formulas, measure transformations, or deterministic pseudo-random paths labeled SIMULATED.

Core reading

Geman, El Karoui & Rochet (1995) provides the seminal framework for changes of numeraire in option pricing. Shreve (2004) gives the definitive modern treatment of Girsanov's theorem, martingale representation, and cross-currency quanto derivatives. Harrison & Pliska (1981) establishes the foundational continuous-time martingale approach to asset pricing.
