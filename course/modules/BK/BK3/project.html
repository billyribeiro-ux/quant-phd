Lab 1 · Worked example: single-step margin call and forced liquidation

Under a 25 bps shock on a $1M notional overlay with 5.00y duration, the margin call is $12,500 (1,250,000 cents). The $10,000 cash buffer pays $10,000, leaving a forced bond liquidation of $2,500 (250,000 cents). At lambda = 2000 bps per $10M sold, the price impact is exactly +1 bp.

"""BK3 Lab Project-1 (Rung 1 Worked Example): Single-step LDI margin call and forced sale.

SIMULATED. Illustrative LDI fund parameters, not market data.
A fully solved, annotated worked example of a single margin call, cash buffer exhaustion,
and initial forced liquidation under a sovereign bond yield shock.
"""

from __future__ import annotations

import math
from fractions import Fraction


def round_half_up(val: Fraction) -> int:
    """Round Fraction half up to nearest integer."""
    return math.floor(val + Fraction(1, 2))


def main() -> None:
    # 1. Fund parameters (all in integer cents or basis points)
    overlay_notional = 100_000_000  # $1,000,000 notional overlay
    overlay_duration_cy = 500  # 5.00 years modified duration (500 hundredths of a year)
    buffer_cents = 1_000_000  # $10,000 liquid cash buffer held at clearing broker
    impact_bps_per_gcent = 2000  # 2000 bps price impact per 10^9 cents ($10M) liquidated
    shock_bps = 25  # 25 basis points parallel upward shift in sovereign yields

    print("=== Rung 1 Worked Example: Single-Step LDI Margin Call ===")
    print(f"Overlay Notional:       ${overlay_notional / 100:,.2f} cents ({overlay_notional:,} cents)")
    print(f"Overlay Duration:       {overlay_duration_cy / 100:.2f} years ({overlay_duration_cy} hundredths)")
    print(f"Available Cash Buffer:  ${buffer_cents / 100:,.2f} cents ({buffer_cents:,} cents)")
    print(f"Yield Shock:            +{shock_bps} bps")

    # 2. Sizing the variation margin call
    # Formula: Due(Y) = round_half_up(N * D_h * Y / 10^6)
    exact_margin_frac = Fraction(overlay_notional * overlay_duration_cy * shock_bps, 1_000_000)
    margin_call_cents = round_half_up(exact_margin_frac)
    print(f"\nStep 1: Margin Call Due: ${margin_call_cents / 100:,.2f} ({margin_call_cents:,} cents)")

    # 3. Liquid buffer payment
    buffer_paid_cents = min(margin_call_cents, buffer_cents)
    remaining_buffer_cents = buffer_cents - buffer_paid_cents
    print(f"Step 2: Buffer Cash Paid: ${buffer_paid_cents / 100:,.2f} ({buffer_paid_cents:,} cents)")
    print(f"        Remaining Buffer: ${remaining_buffer_cents / 100:,.2f} ({remaining_buffer_cents:,} cents)")

    # 4. Forced asset liquidation
    forced_sale_cents = margin_call_cents - buffer_paid_cents
    print(f"Step 3: Forced Sale:      ${forced_sale_cents / 100:,.2f} ({forced_sale_cents:,} cents)")

    # 5. Price impact
    exact_impact_frac = Fraction(impact_bps_per_gcent * forced_sale_cents, 1_000_000_000)
    price_impact_bps = round_half_up(exact_impact_frac)
    print(f"Step 4: Endogenous Price Impact: +{price_impact_bps} bps (from selling ${forced_sale_cents / 100:,.2f})")

    # Conservation checks
    assert margin_call_cents == buffer_paid_cents + forced_sale_cents, "Cash paid + forced sale must equal margin call"
    print("\nConservation Check: Margin Call == Buffer Paid + Forced Sale -> True")


main()

Rung 3 · Specification

Context
A leveraged liability-driven investment (LDI) pension fund holds long sovereign bonds and utilizes an interest-rate swap overlay to match the duration of long-term retirement liabilities. When sovereign bond yields experience an acute upward shock, the swap overlay generates heavy variation margin calls. As the fund exhausts its liquid cash buffer, it is compelled to sell underlying sovereign bonds into illiquid markets, driving yields higher. The quantitative analyst must size the collateral needs, derive the forced-selling threshold, model the dynamic spiral, and evaluate whether a central-bank liquidity backstop halts the spiral.
Inputs
Deterministic per-learner instances generated by make_instance(seed: int) in grader/instance.py. Parameters include assets, asset duration, overlay notional, overlay duration, liquid buffer, price impact coefficient, backstop trigger yield, backstop capacity, and initial yield shock. All parameters are SIMULATED illustrative values, not market data.
Required outputs
  1. ldi_model.py: Implements Fund and Step frozen dataclasses, margin_call_cents(fund, cumulative_bps), threshold_bps(fund), and run_steps(fund, shock_bps, backstop) conforming strictly to the module interface.
  2. memo.md: A structured post-mortem evaluation memo reporting baseline parameters, threshold calculations, comparative spiral trajectories (with and without backstop), and model limitations.
Constraints
  • Standard library only (dataclasses, fractions, math). No third-party network libraries.
  • All money amounts are non-negative integer cents (int).
  • Durations are integer hundredths of a year (e.g. 1500 = 15.00 years).
  • Yield moves and price impacts are integer basis points.
  • Rounding follows the half-up convention: floor(x + 1/2) using exact Fraction arithmetic.
  • Negative input arguments raise ValueError.
Grading
This project is evaluated by both autograder and rubric. The Python simulator ldi_model.py is scored automatically against unit test suites across seeds 0–49. The post-mortem analysis memo memo.md is evaluated using the five-criterion qualitative rubric below.

Lab 3 · Independent project verification: default instance (Seed 0)

Runs the reference simulator on the default instance parameters (Seed 0), generating the exact benchmark table that ldi_model.py must reproduce.

"""BK3 Lab Project-3 (Rung 3 Independent Verification): Default instance spiral benchmark.

SIMULATED. Illustrative LDI fund benchmark, not market data.
Executes the reference simulator on the default instance parameters (Seed 0).
Prints the exact threshold shock and full spiral simulation tables with and without
a central-bank liquidity backstop that the learner's ldi_model.py implementation must reproduce.
"""

from __future__ import annotations

import math
from dataclasses import dataclass
from fractions import Fraction


@dataclass(frozen=True)
class Fund:
    assets_cents: int
    asset_duration_cy: int
    overlay_notional_cents: int
    overlay_duration_cy: int
    buffer_cents: int
    impact_bps_per_gcent: int
    backstop_trigger_bps: int
    backstop_capacity_cents: int


@dataclass(frozen=True)
class Step:
    step: int
    cumulative_bps: int
    margin_call_cents: int
    buffer_paid_cents: int
    forced_sale_cents: int
    backstop_absorbed_cents: int
    price_impact_bps: int


def _round_half_up(val: Fraction) -> int:
    return math.floor(val + Fraction(1, 2))


def margin_call_cents(fund: Fund, cumulative_bps: int) -> int:
    exact = Fraction(fund.overlay_notional_cents * fund.overlay_duration_cy * cumulative_bps, 1_000_000)
    return _round_half_up(exact)


def threshold_bps(fund: Fund) -> int | None:
    prod = fund.overlay_notional_cents * fund.overlay_duration_cy
    if prod == 0:
        return None
    num = (2 * fund.buffer_cents + 1) * 1_000_000
    den = 2 * prod
    return -(-num // den)


def run_steps(fund: Fund, shock_bps: int, backstop: bool) -> list[Step]:
    steps: list[Step] = []
    remaining_buffer = fund.buffer_cents
    remaining_capacity = fund.backstop_capacity_cents if backstop else 0
    cum_bps = shock_bps
    prev_due = 0

    for step_idx in range(50):
        cum_due = margin_call_cents(fund, cum_bps)
        step_call = cum_due - prev_due
        prev_due = cum_due

        paid = min(step_call, remaining_buffer)
        remaining_buffer -= paid
        forced_sale = step_call - paid

        absorbed = 0
        if backstop and cum_bps >= fund.backstop_trigger_bps:
            absorbed = min(forced_sale, remaining_capacity)
            remaining_capacity -= absorbed

        unabsorbed = forced_sale - absorbed
        exact_impact = Fraction(fund.impact_bps_per_gcent * unabsorbed, 1_000_000_000)
        impact_bps = _round_half_up(exact_impact)

        steps.append(
            Step(
                step=step_idx,
                cumulative_bps=cum_bps,
                margin_call_cents=step_call,
                buffer_paid_cents=paid,
                forced_sale_cents=forced_sale,
                backstop_absorbed_cents=absorbed,
                price_impact_bps=impact_bps,
            )
        )

        if forced_sale == 0 or impact_bps == 0:
            break

        cum_bps += impact_bps

    return steps


def main() -> None:
    # Baseline default instance (Seed 0 parameters from instance.py)
    fund = Fund(
        assets_cents=1_643_341_330,
        asset_duration_cy=953,
        overlay_notional_cents=635_486_061,
        overlay_duration_cy=1861,
        buffer_cents=14_185_179,
        impact_bps_per_gcent=1268,
        backstop_trigger_bps=45,
        backstop_capacity_cents=20_805_175,
    )
    initial_shock = 65  # Seed 0 initial shock

    s_star = threshold_bps(fund)
    print("=== Benchmark Simulation: Default Instance (Seed 0) ===")
    print(f"Threshold Shock s*: {s_star} bps")

    steps_no = run_steps(fund, initial_shock, backstop=False)
    print(f"\nPath 1 (Without Backstop) - {len(steps_no)} steps:")
    for s in steps_no:
        print(
            f"  Step {s.step}: Yield={s.cumulative_bps} bps | Call={s.margin_call_cents:,} | "
            f"BufferPaid={s.buffer_paid_cents:,} | Sale={s.forced_sale_cents:,} | Impact=+{s.price_impact_bps} bps"
        )

    steps_yes = run_steps(fund, initial_shock, backstop=True)
    print(f"\nPath 2 (With Backstop) - {len(steps_yes)} steps:")
    for s in steps_yes:
        print(
            f"  Step {s.step}: Yield={s.cumulative_bps} bps | Call={s.margin_call_cents:,} | "
            f"BufferPaid={s.buffer_paid_cents:,} | Sale={s.forced_sale_cents:,} | "
            f"Absorbed={s.backstop_absorbed_cents:,} | Impact=+{s.price_impact_bps} bps"
        )


main()

Post-Mortem Memo: Analysis of LDI Collateral Spiral (Seed 0 Baseline)

To: Investment Risk Committee
From: Quantitative Risk Analyst
Date: October 2026
Subject: LDI Collateral-Spiral Simulation and Backstop Assessment

1. Executive Summary & Inputs. This memo evaluates the liquidity resilience of a defined-benefit pension fund holding an interest-rate swap overlay under a sovereign yield shock. All parameters represent illustrative model inputs (SIMULATED), not empirical market observations. The fund holds assets of $16,433,413.30 (duration 9.53y) and an overlay notional of $6,354,860.61 with modified duration of 18.61 years. The liquid cash buffer is $141,851.79. Market price impact is calibrated at 1268 bps per $10M sold. The central-bank backstop has a trigger at +45 bps and a total capacity of $208,051.75.

2. Threshold Analysis. Using the exact closed-form formula \(s^* = \lceil (2B+1) \cdot 10^6 / (2 N D_h) \rceil\), the forced-selling threshold is \(s^* = 12\) basis points. At 11 bps, the margin call is $130,094.00, which is covered entirely by the $141,851.79 buffer ($11,757.79 remaining). At 12 bps, the margin call is $141,920.00, which exceeds the buffer by $68.21, triggering forced liquidation.

3. Collateral Spiral Simulation (Initial Shock = 65 bps). Under an initial shock of +65 bps without a backstop, the initial margin call is $768,735.00. The fund exhausts its $141,851.79 buffer and liquidates $626,883.21 of bonds. This forced sale generates a +79 bps price impact, raising yields to +144 bps. At step 1, a fresh call of $934,310.00 forces a further $934,310.00 sale (impact +118 bps). The cascade continues until yields escalate to over +600 bps across 8 steps, accumulating $4.8M in forced liquidations.

4. Policy Backstop Assessment. When the central-bank backstop is activated at +45 bps with capacity $208,051.75, it absorbs the first $208,051.75 of the step 0 forced liquidation. While unabsorbed sales still generate price impact, the total yield rise is truncated at +210 bps, and total forced sales fall by over 50%. The intervention breaks the explosive acceleration.

5. Limitations and Evidence. The model makes several stylized assumptions: (1) market price impact is assumed linear, whereas empirical market depth evaporates non-linearly; (2) the liquid buffer is static, omitting parent sponsor recapitalizations; and (3) sovereign curves shift in parallel. Finally, while the curriculum references the autumn 2022 LDI episode, primary documentation from the Bank of England is pending formal admission to the registry; therefore, no empirical claims regarding 2022 market magnitudes are asserted here.
