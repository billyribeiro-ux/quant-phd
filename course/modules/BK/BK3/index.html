Insurance & Pensions

Insurance companies and defined-benefit pension funds are the dominant long-duration institutional investors in global capital markets. Unlike commercial banks funded by short-term deposits, insurers and pensions manage long-dated, life-contingent liabilities that span multiple decades. This module develops the quantitative foundations of insurance and pension balance sheets: the actuarial present value of survival-contingent liabilities, statutory capital and solvency regulation under interest-rate shocks, defined-benefit funding ratios under alternative discount-rate choices, liability-driven investment (LDI) immunization via interest-rate swap overlays, and the endogenous collateral spiral that threatens leveraged LDI structures.

  • Prerequisites: BK1 Commercial Banking and FI1 Fixed Income Fundamentals
  • Project: Leveraged LDI collateral-spiral simulator and post-mortem evaluation memo (grading: both autograder and rubric)
  • Data: SIMULATED illustrative parameters. No empirical series are embedded.

48 h lessons · 12 h project (20% of the 60 module hours)

Objectives

By the end of BK3 you can:

  1. Formulate the actuarial present value of life annuities and death benefits, and derive exact geometric closed forms for level survival-contingent cash flows (Lesson 1).
  2. Model the insurer balance sheet, trace policy reserve roll-forwards, and verify the double-entry accounting identity \(A = L + K_c\) across operational periods (Lesson 2).
  3. Calculate the interest-rate sensitivity of statutory capital surplus, and derive the exact closed-form yield shock that causes an insurer to breach regulatory solvency (Lesson 3).
  4. Evaluate defined-benefit pension liabilities across competing discount-rate conventions, and prove the strict monotonicity of the pension funding ratio (Lesson 4).
  5. Construct liability-driven investment portfolios using swap overlays, derive the required overlay notional for first-order DV01 matching, and quantify the second-order convexity residual (Lesson 5).
  6. Simulate the multi-step LDI collateral spiral, derive the closed-form forced-selling threshold, and evaluate the stabilization capacity of central-bank liquidity backstops (Lesson 6 and Capstone Project).

Placement diagnostic

Eight questions testing prerequisite knowledge and the core skills taught in rungs 1 and 2 of the project ladder. The mastery bar is 80% (7 of 8 correct). To skip rungs 1 and 2, you must achieve 80% and correctly answer both marked skill checks.

  1. What is the present value at 5% annual interest of a $1,000 cash flow received in one year?

    Options

    \(1000 / 1.05 = 952.38\) (M1, present value).

  2. An insurer has $90,000 in assets and $80,000 in technical liabilities. What is its policyholders' surplus capital?

    \(K_c = A - L = 90\,000 - 80\,000 = 10\,000\) (AC1, balance sheet identity).

  3. A bond portfolio has a market value of $10,000,000 and a modified duration of 2.0 years. What is its DV01 in dollars per basis point?

    \(\mathrm{DV01} = 10\,000\,000 \times 2.0 / 10\,000 = 200\) dollars per basis point (FI1, DV01).

  4. What is the derivative of \(f(y) = (1 + y)^{-1}\) with respect to \(y\)?

    Options

    \(\frac{d}{dy}(1+y)^{-1} = -(1+y)^{-2}\) (M2, derivative).

  5. In commercial banking under Basel III, what constitutes the denominator of the Tier 1 capital ratio?

    Options

    Basel III capital adequacy ratios measure regulatory capital divided by risk-weighted assets (BK1, Basel III capital).

  6. What does zero-coupon spot rate curve bootstrapping produce from par yields?

    Options

    Bootstrapping iteratively solves for zero-coupon discount factors from coupon bond prices (FI1, curve bootstrapping).

  7. Skill check (rung 1). An LDI swap overlay has notional $1,000,000 (100,000,000 cents) and duration 5.00 years (500 hundredths of a year). Under a +25 bps yield shock, what is the variation margin call due in dollars? (Round half up to nearest cent.)

    \(N \times D_h \times Y / 10^6 = 100\,000\,000 \times 500 \times 25 / 10^6 = 1\,250\,000\) cents = $12,500.00. This is the core margin calculation of Rung 1.

  8. Skill check (rung 2). If the margin call due is $12,500 and the fund holds a liquid cash buffer of $10,000, how many dollars of forced bond sales must be executed?

    The buffer pays $\min(12\,500, 10\,000) = \$10\,000$. The forced sale shortfall is \(12\,500 - 10\,000 = \$2\,500\). This is the completion calculation of Rung 2.

Lessons

Six lessons of eight study hours each, totaling 48 hours of instruction. Each lesson integrates mathematical formulations, rigorous proofs, a runnable Python lab, and retrieval quizzes:

L01 · 8 h Actuarial Present Value and Mortality Discounting Survival probabilities, actuarial discounting, geometric series closed forms for life annuities, and comparative statics. L02 · 8 h The Insurer Balance Sheet: Reserves, Assets, and Surpluses Technical provisions, unearned premium reserves, claims roll-forward, and institutional contrast with commercial banks. L03 · 8 h Insurance Capital Regulation: Solvency and Balance-Sheet Shocks Risk-based solvency ratios, asset-liability duration mismatch, closed-form threshold shocks, and Koijen & Yogo (2015). L04 · 8 h Pension Funding and Choice of Liability Discount Rate Defined-benefit funding ratios, risk-free versus expected-return curves, monotonicity proof, and Novy-Marx & Rauh (2011). L05 · 8 h Liability-Driven Investment: Duration Matching and Convexity Residuals First-order DV01 immunization with swap overlays, closed-form notional sizing, and the second-order Taylor convexity residual. L06 · 8 h The LDI Collateral Spiral and Policy Backstops Derivative variation margin calls, buffer exhaustion, closed-form forced-sale thresholds, price impact, and central-bank backstops.

The Project Ladder

The module concludes with the LDI Collateral-Spiral Model and Post-Mortem Memo capstone project (12 hours). The project ladder provides four scaffolded rungs:

  1. Worked example

    Annotated line-by-line solution of single-step margin calls, buffer cash payments, forced bond liquidations, and price impact.

  2. Completion problem

    Interactive lab completing the core margin call and forced sale expressions, verified instantly in browser.

  3. Independent project

    Implement the complete discrete simulator ldi_model.py evaluated by the autograder, and author the post-mortem evaluation memo memo.md evaluated by the rubric. Project specifications.

  4. Stretch

    Advanced non-linear market impact modeling, multi-tenor curve twists, and procyclical haircuts for doctoral portfolios.
