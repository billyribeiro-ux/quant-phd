Household Finance & Wealth Management

Household finance models the balance sheet, consumption-smoothing choices, and asset allocations of individual households over their full economic lifecycle. You will value non-tradable human capital as an implicit bond asset, solve the tax-efficient asset location problem across taxable and tax-sheltered accounts, evaluate the empirical evidence on retail investment mistakes and advice fee drag, implement moving-block bootstrap resampling to preserve time-series dependence, analyze sequence-of-returns risk during decumulation, and size sustainable retirement withdrawal rates.

  • Prerequisites: FC3 Market Efficiency: The Evidence Primer and CF1 Time Value & Capital Budgeting.
  • Project: Retirement plan with Monte Carlo and tax-aware asset location, plus client letter. Graded by autograder and rubric.
  • Data: Generated simulations (labelled SIMULATED). Embedded market series available in repository data.

35 h lessons · 15 h project (30% of the 50 module hours)

Objectives

By the end of BK5 you can:

  1. Construct an economic household balance sheet integrating non-tradable human capital, housing equity, and liabilities, and derive the Merton financial equity glidepath across the working lifecycle (Lesson 1).
  2. Analyze the terminal wealth equivalence between Traditional tax-deferred and Roth tax-exempt accounts under invariant tax rates, and optimize asset location across taxable, TDA, and Roth capacities (Lesson 2).
  3. Evaluate the empirical evidence on household investment mistakes, financial illiteracy, and AUM fee erosion using the arithmetic of active management (Lesson 3).
  4. Implement a moving-block bootstrap algorithm that preserves return autocorrelation, and determine safe withdrawal rates under multi-year retirement horizons (Lesson 4).
  5. Quantify sequence-of-returns risk during decumulation, and prove why return permutation symmetry breaks in the presence of interim cash withdrawals (Lesson 5).
  6. Build a complete, automated household retirement engine and compose a professional client letter communicating decumulation risks (Project).

Prerequisites

BK5 builds directly on the foundational modules FC3 and CF1, drawing on these established concepts without redefining them:

  • From FC3: arithmetic of active management, efficient-markets hypothesis, luck vs. skill, and behavioral explanations.
  • From CF1: net present value, the Fisher relation, real vs. nominal cash flows, and Monte Carlo NPV simulation.
  • From earlier foundations: annuity (M1), balance sheet (AC1), and bootstrap resampling (S1).

Placement diagnostic

Eight questions: six on prerequisite concepts from M1, AC1, CF1, FC3, and S1, plus two skill checks testing the implementation core of Rungs 1 and 2. Mastery bar: 80% (at least 7 correct) including both skill checks to skip Rungs 1 and 2.

  1. Under the classical accounting identity, what fundamental equation governs the corporate balance sheet?

    Options

    Assets equal Liabilities plus Equity (AC1, balance sheet).

  2. A household receives an annual cash flow of $10 at the end of each year for 20 years. If the discount rate is 8%, what is the approximate present value in whole dollars?

    The annuity factor for \(N=20, r=0.08\) is \((1 - (1.08)^{-20})/0.08 \approx 9.818\), giving \(\$10 \times 9.818 = \$98.18 \approx \$100\) (M1, annuity).

  3. According to the Fisher relation, if the nominal rate is 6.08% and annual inflation is 2.0%, what is the exact real interest rate in percent?

    \((1 + R_{\text{nom}}) = (1 + r_{\text{real}})(1 + \pi)\). Here \(1.0608 / 1.0200 = 1.0400\), or 4.0% real (CF1, Fisher relation).

  4. Under Sharpe's (1991) arithmetic of active management, what is the average return of active investors before fees relative to the market index?

    Options

    Because passive investors earn the market return by definition, the aggregate dollar-weighted active portfolio must also earn the market return before fees (FC3, arithmetic of active management).

  5. In an independent and identically distributed (i.i.d.) non-parametric bootstrap, how are observations drawn from the empirical sample?

    Options

    The standard Efron bootstrap samples observations uniformly at random with replacement (S1, bootstrap).

  6. Why does an i.i.d. bootstrap fail when applied directly to multi-year returns of assets exhibiting volatility clustering or mean reversion?

    Options

    Independent sampling breaks the temporal ordering, eliminating auto-covariance and persistent drawdown clusters.

  7. Skill check (Rung 1). A household holds corporate bonds generating ordinary taxable interest and equities generating qualified dividends and capital gains. If both Taxable and Traditional IRA (TDA) accounts are available, which account should hold the bonds first to minimize tax drag?

    Options

    Bonds produce interest taxed annually at high ordinary income rates without capital gains deferral; locating them in TDA eliminates annual tax drag.

  8. Skill check (Rung 2). You have a historical time series of \(N = 50\) annual returns. How many distinct overlapping moving blocks of length \(b = 5\) can be extracted from this series?

    The number of overlapping blocks of length \(b\) in a sample of size \(N\) is \(N - b + 1 = 50 - 5 + 1 = 46\).

Lessons

Five lessons of seven study hours each (35 hours total). Each lesson includes formal definitions, theorems, empirical claims with registry citations, runnable inline Python labs, four problem tiers with complete solutions, and a retrieval quiz.

The project ladder

The module concludes with the comprehensive Retirement plan with Monte Carlo project (15 hours). Rungs 1 and 2 scaffold the key computational competencies.

  1. Worked example

    A fully solved case study calculating human capital present value, executing tax-aware asset location, and running a moving-block bootstrap withdrawal simulation on a 30-year horizon.

  2. Completion problem

    An interactive code completion task requiring implementation of the asset location priority allocation and moving-block index slicing, validated instantly against reference twins.

  3. Independent project

    Build retirement_plan.py implementing the full household wealth management engine, pass 50 deterministic autograder seeds, and author a professional client communication letter evaluated against a five-criterion rubric. Project specification.

  4. Stretch

    Incorporate dynamic withdrawal guardrails (Guyton-Klinger rules) and Gompertz-Makeham stochastic mortality survival modeling into the decumulation engine.
