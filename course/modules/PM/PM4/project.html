MBS Pricer: Monte Carlo Valuation, OAS & Effective Risk Analytics

A fixed income trading desk requires an institutional pricing and risk engine for agency mortgage-backed securities (agency-mbs-pass-through). You implement a production pricer in reference.py to simulate risk-neutral interest rate paths under mean-reverting short-rate dynamics, generate pathwise mortgage cash flow waterfalls governed by state-dependent prepayment and burnout (prepayment-model), calibrate Option-Adjusted Spread (option-adjusted-spread) to market quotes, and compute effective duration and negative convexity (negative-convexity, effective-duration) via finite-difference curve perturbations.

Specification

Context
A mortgage trading desk requires an institutional-grade Monte Carlo valuation and risk engine for agency pass-through securities. The pricer must simulate risk-neutral interest rate diffusions, compute path-dependent cash flow waterfalls under state-dependent prepayment and burnout, calibrate the Option-Adjusted Spread (OAS) matching market prices, and evaluate effective duration and negative convexity under parallel curve perturbations.
Inputs
A configuration dictionary generated by instance.py containing:
  • pool: notional ($), collateral wac, investor net pass_through_rate, remaining maturity wam (months), and actual delay_days.
  • rates: 1-factor Vasicek short-rate parameters (r0, kappa, theta, sigma).
  • prepayment: base CPR (base_cpr), maximum CPR (max_cpr), refinancing elasticity (beta), midpoint incentive (mu), mortgage rate spread (margin), and burnout speed (gamma_burnout).
  • pricing: n_paths (500), target_market_price ($), curve shift dy (0.0025 = 25 bps), and sim_seed.
Required outputs
A production module reference.py exposing:
  • simulate_interest_rates(r0, kappa, theta, sigma, wam, n_paths, seed) -> np.ndarray: array of shape (n_paths, wam) with exact Vasicek transitions.
  • generate_mbs_cash_flows(pool, prep, rates) -> np.ndarray: array of shape (n_paths, wam) of total monthly cash flows.
  • price_mbs(cash_flows, rates, spread, delay_days) -> float: average discounted cash flow value.
  • solve_oas(rates, cash_flows, target_price, delay_days) -> float: calibrated OAS in basis points ($S \times 10^4$) within $1 tolerance of target market price.
  • compute_effective_greeks(instance, oas_bps) -> dict[str, float]: dictionary with price_base, price_up, price_down, effective_duration, and effective_convexity.
  • solve_mbs_pricer(instance) -> dict[str, float]: master pipeline returning all pricing and risk metrics.
Constraints
Pure Python 3.14+ utilizing NumPy and SciPy. Execution time across 50 consecutive seeds must complete in under 60 seconds total (<2 seconds per seed). All cash flows strictly positive, exact collateral balance conservation, and verified negative convexity in the refinancing window.
Grading
Autograded by test_project.py: the automated test suite validates all short-rate simulations, cash flow waterfalls, OAS bisection tolerances, and effective duration/convexity bounds across 50 independent seeds (0 through 49).
