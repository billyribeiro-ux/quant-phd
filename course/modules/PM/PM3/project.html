An institutional investment committee requires an econometric audit of a prospective private credit fund. Reported quarterly returns suggest an annualized volatility of under 3.5%, a Sharpe ratio exceeding 2.0, and negligible correlation with public debt markets. You will build an institutional de-smoothing engine in reference.py to invert the first-order appraisal-smoothing filter, recover unobserved true quarterly return distributions, compute de-smoothed systematic beta against a public syndicated leveraged loan proxy, and write an LP investment due diligence memo evaluated by rubric against annotated exemplars.

Econometric Setup & Methodology

We specify the classic partial-adjustment model of appraisal smoothing: \(R_t^{\text{obs}} = \phi R_{t-1}^{\text{obs}} + (1 - \phi) R_t^{\text{true}}\). When true economic returns \(R_t^{\text{true}}\) are serially uncorrelated, the sample first-order autocorrelation of reported marks \(\hat{\rho}_1 = \operatorname{Cov}(R_t^{\text{obs}}, R_{t-1}^{\text{obs}}) / \operatorname{Var}(R_{t-1}^{\text{obs}})\) consistently estimates \(\phi\). Inverting the linear filter recovers unobserved quarterly returns: \(R_t^{\text{true}} = \frac{R_t^{\text{obs}} - \phi R_{t-1}^{\text{obs}}}{1 - \phi}\). All benchmark comparisons utilize a public syndicated leveraged loan proxy.

  • Meets Level 4 for C1: complete mathematical equations, rigorous identification justification, exact filter inversion.
Volatility Unmasking & Systematic Loan Beta

By Theorem 4.1, observed variance is suppressed by \(\frac{1-\phi}{1+\phi} = \frac{1-0.5922}{1+0.5922} = \frac{0.4078}{1.5922} \approx 0.2561\), predicting a theoretical volatility expansion factor of \(\sqrt{1 / 0.2561} \approx 1.976\), which matches our computed empirical expansion of 1.9741x. By Theorem 4.2, contemporaneous covariance is attenuated by \((1 - \phi)\), compressing observed beta. Unsmoothing expands loan market beta from 0.1918 to 0.4029. As established empirically by Couts, Gonçalves & Rossi (2024, Table 3), private credit does not provide uncorrelated diversification; its apparent stability is an accounting illusion. When public syndicated debt sells off, private credit collateral experiences matched credit deterioration with a multi-quarter reporting lag.

  • Meets Level 4 for C2 and C3: exact theoretical derivations, empirical congruence, verified registry citation with locator.
Limitations & LP Recommendations

This audit relies on SIMULATED 20-quarter return series; no claim of real-market asset performance is made. In live markets, evaluating true credit risk requires inspecting underlying borrower EBITDA leverage (Chernenko et al. 2022) and public BDC price-to-NAV discounts (Davydiuk et al. 2024). Methodologically, the constant AR(1) model assumes invariant appraisal behavior; during liquidity panics, managers become more reluctant to write down marks, causing \(\phi\) to rise endogenously and understating peak drawdown severity. We recommend: (1) treat private debt in the LP asset allocation model with an imputed volatility of 8–10% and loan beta of 1.0; (2) cap total private credit commitment at 5% of plan assets; and (3) require quarterly loan-level non-accrual reporting to monitor underlying distress.

  • Meets Level 4 for C4 and C5: prominent SIMULATED labeling, rigorous examination of time-varying \(\phi\), and actionable governance recommendations.
