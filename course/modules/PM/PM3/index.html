Private Credit & Infrastructure

Private credit and infrastructure have expanded into multi-trillion dollar asset classes following bank regulatory retreat post-2008. This module provides a rigorous, empirical, and econometric examination of direct middle-market lending, business development companies, unitranche priority waterfalls, infrastructure concession cash flows, and the pervasive distortions of appraisal return smoothing.

  • Prerequisites: PM1 Private Equity and CR1 Structural & Reduced-Form Credit Models
  • Unlocks: Capstone Asset Management & Alternative Allocation Desks
  • Project: Unsmoothing private credit, autograder and LP review memo

Objectives

  1. Model middle-market direct lending floating-rate contracts, including benchmark SOFR rate floors, original issue discounts (OID), and financial maintenance covenant structures.
  2. Decompose Business Development Company (BDC) capital structures, regulatory asset coverage constraints under the 1940 Act, and Level 3 fair-value mark-to-market mechanics under ASC 820.
  3. Structure unitranche facilities and derive cash-flow and liquidation recovery waterfalls under the Agreement Among Lenders (AAL) for First-Out and Last-Out tranches.
  4. Formulate project finance infrastructure cash-flow models, evaluating debt sizing, CFADS, DSCR covenants, and LLCR ratios across availability and merchant concession regimes.
  5. Quantify the impact of appraisal smoothing on reported private debt volatility, market beta, and Sharpe ratios using AR(1) and moving-average econometric models.
  6. Invert appraisal smoothing filters to recover unobserved true private credit return series and evaluate true systematic correlation against public leveraged loan proxies.

Prerequisites

This module builds directly upon the structural credit frameworks and private fund mechanics developed in earlier units:

  • From PM1 Private Equity: private-equity fund structure, management fees and carried interest, and public market equivalent (KS-PME).
  • From CR1 Structural & Reduced-Form Credit Models: Merton structural credit model, default intensity (hazard rate), and reduced-form credit models.

Placement diagnostic

Score 80% or higher and correctly answer both practical skill items to validate prerequisite mastery and proceed directly to Rung 3 of the project ladder.

  1. Under a first-order autoregressive AR(1) appraisal smoothing model \(R_t^{\text{obs}} = \phi R_{t-1}^{\text{obs}} + (1 - \phi) R_t^{\text{true}}\) with smoothing coefficient \(\phi = 0.50\), calculate the theoretical ratio of true return standard deviation to observed return standard deviation, \(\sigma_{\text{true}} / \sigma_{\text{obs}}\).

    By the AR(1) variance relation, \(\Var(R_t^{\text{obs}}) = \frac{1-\phi}{1+\phi} \Var(R_t^{\text{true}})\). For \(\phi = 0.50\), \(\frac{1-0.5}{1+0.5} = \frac{1}{3}\). Therefore, \(\sigma_{\text{true}} / \sigma_{\text{obs}} = \sqrt{3} \approx 1.732\).

  2. A reported private credit index exhibits an estimated beta of \(\beta_{\text{obs}} = 0.40\) against the public leveraged loan index. The estimated first-order serial correlation of reported returns is \(\phi = 0.50\). Assuming the underlying market proxy is serially uncorrelated, compute the de-smoothed true market beta \(\beta_{\text{true}}\) if true beta is \(\beta_{\text{true}} = \beta_{\text{obs}} / (1 - \phi)\), and state the beta adjustment multiplier \(\beta_{\text{true}} / \beta_{\text{obs}}\).

    \(\beta_{\text{true}} = 0.40 / (1 - 0.50) = 0.80\). The ratio of de-smoothed beta to observed beta is \(0.80 / 0.40 = 2.000\).

  3. Why do mid-market corporate borrowers accept private credit direct lending spreads that are 150 to 250 basis points wider than traditional commercial syndicated loans?

    Options

    Chernenko, Erel & Prilmeier (2022) show that borrowers pay a direct lending premium in exchange for leverage capacity exceeding regulatory bank guidelines, execution speed, and bespoke covenant terms.

  4. Under an Agreement Among Lenders (AAL) for a unitranche loan, what is the economic priority between the First-Out (FO) and Last-Out (LO) tranche holders upon borrower liquidation?

    Options

    The Agreement Among Lenders creates synthetic subordination: although the borrower sees a single first-lien facility, the AAL stipulates that FO lenders have absolute liquidation priority over LO lenders.

  5. In project finance concession modeling, what distinguishes an Availability Payment concession from a Merchant / Volume concession?

    Options

    Availability concessions eliminate demand and traffic volume risk, replacing it with public sponsor performance and operational availability risk.
