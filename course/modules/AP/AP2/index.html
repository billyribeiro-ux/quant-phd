Mean-Variance, CAPM, ICAPM & APT

The foundational architecture of modern asset pricing: Markowitz mean-variance geometry, Tobin's two-fund separation theorem, the Capital Asset Pricing Model of Sharpe and Lintner, Roll's critique of empirical testability, Merton's intertemporal hedging demands, and Ross's arbitrage pricing theory.

  • Prerequisites: M7 Constrained Optimization, S1 Statistical Inference & Hypothesis Testing, E3 Expected Utility & Choice Under Uncertainty
  • Unlocks: AP1 No-Arbitrage & the Stochastic Discount Factor, FC3 Equity & Fixed Income Valuation, CF2 Corporate Financial Policy
  • Project: CAPM on trial: automated test suite in capm_tests.py and analytical evaluation memo in report.md

70 h lessons · 20 h project (22%)

Objectives

By the end of AP2 you can:

  1. Derive the analytical global minimum-variance portfolio and the mean-variance efficient frontier using Lagrange multipliers, and compute exact frontier weights for arbitrary target returns.
  2. Prove the two-fund separation theorem and the existence of the tangency portfolio under riskless lending and borrowing, and construct the capital market line.
  3. Formulate market clearing in mean-variance equilibrium to derive the CAPM pricing equation and express market beta as the systematic covariance ratio.
  4. Decompose asset return variance into systematic and idiosyncratic components, and estimate Jensen's alpha with its exact sampling distribution under standard linear regression assumptions.
  5. State and prove the Roll critique, showing why testing the CAPM is mathematically equivalent to testing the mean-variance efficiency of the market proxy.
  6. Formulate Merton's intertemporal CAPM (ICAPM) with multiple state-variable hedging factors, and derive Ross's Arbitrage Pricing Theory (APT) under exact factor structure.

Placement diagnostic

Thirteen questions covering the prerequisite closure and two skill checks testing the implementation skills taught in rungs 1 and 2 of the project ladder. The mastery bar is 80% (at least 11 of 13 correct including both skill checks).

  1. Skill check (rung 1). For two uncorrelated assets with equal variances \(\sigma_1^2 = \sigma_2^2 = 0.04\), what is the weight \(w_1\) of the global minimum-variance portfolio?

    By symmetry, minimizing \(w_1^2 \sigma_1^2 + (1-w_1)^2 \sigma_2^2\) gives \(w_1 = \sigma_2^2 / (\sigma_1^2 + \sigma_2^2) = 0.5\).

  2. Skill check (rung 2). In a sample of returns, the sample covariance between an asset and the market is 0.0036 and the market variance is 0.0030. What is the sample market beta \(\hat{\beta}\)?

    \(\hat{\beta} = \Cov(r_i, r_m) / \Var(r_m) = 0.0036 / 0.0030 = 1.2\).

  3. From M7: What condition on the objective function and constraint set guarantees a unique global minimizer in quadratic programming?

    Options

    Strict convexity (positive definite Hessian) over a convex set ensures that any local minimum is the unique global minimum (M7).

  4. From S1: In an ordinary least squares regression with \(T\) observations and 2 estimated parameters (intercept and slope), how many degrees of freedom does the residual variance estimate possess?

    Options

    Estimating two parameters (intercept and slope) imposes two linear restrictions on the residuals, leaving \(T - 2\) degrees of freedom (S1).

  5. From E3: An expected-utility maximizer is risk-averse if and only if their Bernoulli utility function \(u(w)\) is…

    Options

    By Jensen's inequality, \(\E[u(w)] < u(\E[w])\) for all non-degenerate lotteries if and only if \(u\) is strictly concave (E3).

  6. From M4: If two random variables \(X\) and \(Y\) have \(\Cov(X, Y) = 0\), which of the following is necessarily true?

    Options

    \(\Var(X+Y) = \Var(X) + \Var(Y) + 2\Cov(X, Y)\); zero covariance eliminates the cross-term. Zero covariance does not imply independence in general (M4).

  7. From M3: A symmetric matrix \(\Sigma \in \R^{n \times n}\) is positive definite if and only if for all non-zero vectors \(x \in \R^n\)…

    Options

    By definition, strict positive definiteness requires the quadratic form \(x^\top \Sigma x\) to be strictly positive for every non-zero vector \(x\) (M3).

  8. From M2: For a quadratic function \(f(w) = \frac{1}{2} w^\top \Sigma w - \mu^\top w\) with symmetric \(\Sigma\), the gradient \(\nabla f(w)\) is…

    Options

    Differentiating \(\frac{1}{2} w^\top \Sigma w\) gives \(\Sigma w\), and differentiating \(\mu^\top w\) gives \(\mu\) (M2).

  9. From M1: A periodic discount factor \(v = \frac{1}{1 + r}\) applied over \(k\) compounding periods evaluates a cash flow \(C\) at present value…

    Options

    Under discrete compounding, the present value is \(C (1 + r)^{-k}\) (M1).

  10. From E1: In a competitive pure-exchange economy, an equilibrium price vector clears markets when…

    Options

    Competitive equilibrium requires market clearing: aggregate demand equals aggregate endowment across all goods (E1).

  11. From CS1: If an asset price moves from \(P_0 = 100\) to \(P_1 = 110\), the simple return \(R\) and log return \(r\) are…

    Options

    Simple return is \((110-100)/100 = 0.10\); log return is \(\ln(110/100) \approx 0.09531\) (CS1).

  12. From S0: Anscombe's quartet demonstrates that summary statistics (mean, variance, correlation) can be…

    Options

    Anscombe's quartet proves that summary statistics alone are insufficient to describe bivariate distributions without diagnostic plots (S0).

  13. From P0.1: In Python, floating-point arithmetic following IEEE 754 can cause expressions like 0.1 + 0.2 == 0.3 to evaluate to…

    Options

    Binary representation of tenths results in rounding error, so 0.1 + 0.2 == 0.30000000000000004 != 0.3 (P0.1, M8).

Lessons

Ten lessons of seven study hours each (70 hours total). Each lesson includes formal definitions, complete proofs with assumptions, numerical checks in runnable Python labs, problem sets across four tiers, and retrieval quiz questions.

L01 · 7 h Mean-Variance Portfolio Choice The Markowitz optimization framework, analytical GMV portfolio derivation, and geometry of the mean-variance efficient frontier. L02 · 7 h The Tangency Portfolio and Two-Fund Separation The riskless asset, Sharpe ratio maximization, the unique tangency portfolio, and Tobin's two-fund separation theorem. L03 · 7 h The Capital Market Line Linear efficient combinations of the riskless asset and tangency portfolio; the CML slope as the maximal Sharpe ratio. L04 · 7 h The Capital Asset Pricing Model Competitive equilibrium with homogeneous expectations, market clearing, CAPM pricing derivation, and market beta. L05 · 7 h The Security Market Line Individual asset pricing in beta-expected return space, pricing errors, and the curriculum SML lab on simulated asset cross-sections. L06 · 7 h Equity Risk Premium and Jensen's Alpha Time-series regressions, Jensen's alpha, OLS orthogonal projection, standard errors, and statistical inference for pricing errors. L07 · 7 h The Roll Critique Mathematical equivalence of the SML and proxy efficiency; why testing the CAPM is impossible without the unobservable true market portfolio. L08 · 7 h The Empirically Flat Security Market Line Fama-French empirical findings, errors-in-variables attenuation bias, and the economic mechanisms flattening the empirical SML. L09 · 7 h The Intertemporal CAPM Merton's dynamic consumption-investment framework, multi-beta pricing, and state-variable hedging premia. L10 · 7 h Linear Factor Models and Arbitrage Pricing Theory Ross's no-arbitrage factor model, zero-cost zero-exposure arbitrage portfolios, and multi-factor pricing equations.

The project ladder

The module concludes with CAPM on Trial, an end-to-end algorithmic implementation and analytical evaluation project.

  1. Worked example

    Full implementation and step-by-step execution of GMV weights, Jensen regression, and SML evaluation on a 3-asset simulated panel.

  2. Completion problem

    Fill in the mathematical formulas for the OLS beta estimator and t-statistic in the browser runtime.

  3. Independent project

    Implement capm_tests.py across 50 autograder test seeds and author an executive evaluation memo report.md.

  4. Stretch

    Download open-access historical French portfolio data and test for non-linearities and conditional beta dynamics client-side.
