Research Writing & Presentation

Writing and presenting research in quantitative finance requires rigorous discipline: papers must convey findings, empirical identification strategies, and institutional mechanisms rapidly and unambiguously to expert referees and seminar audiences. This module formalizes the canonical six-section paper architecture, the five-element results-first introduction standard, standalone regression table design, evidence-grounded figure redesign, and expert presentation slot budgeting. The capstone project requires refactoring three weak paper introductions to the results-first standard and annotating every structural change against an observable rubric.

  • Prerequisites: P0.1 Foundations of Quantitative Research and S0 Empirical Data Foundations. Their prerequisite closure includes CS1, M1, M2, and M4.
  • Project: Rewrite a Weak Paper Introduction, evaluated against a 5-criterion observable rubric with annotated exemplars.
  • Data: No real dataset is required. All exercises, outlines, and paper texts are SIMULATED models of empirical corporate finance, sovereign debt, and market microstructure studies.

24 h lessons · 6 h project (20%)

Objectives

By the end of PC1 you can:

  1. Partition an empirical finance manuscript into the canonical six-section sequence, and audit section word budgets against the 10-15-20-30-20-5 allocation rule (Lesson 1).
  2. Formulate a compliant results-first opening sentence specifying mechanism, signed direction, point estimate with explicit units, standardized shock, and sample boundaries in sentence 1 or 2 (Lesson 2).
  3. Audit a regression exhibit against the six-point standalone table checklist, ensuring complete documentation of standard error clustering, observation counts, and variable acronyms (Lesson 3).
  4. Quantify visual distortion ratios in truncated-axis bar charts, and redesign graphical exhibits to align with the Cleveland-McGill graphical perception hierarchy (Lesson 3).
  5. Construct an expert presentation slot budget, calculate slide pacing bounds within the 1.5 to 3.0 minutes-per-slide window, and execute the three-step adversarial Q&A defense protocol (Lesson 4).
  6. Refactor flawed empirical research introductions into compliant results-first prose, and defend every revision in a structured annotation log evaluated against rubric criteria (Project).

Prerequisites

PC1 requires P0.1 and S0. It uses these concepts from the prerequisite closure without redefining them:

  • From P0.1: paper anatomy, evidence grade, identification, robustness check, replication, steelmanning, and project ladder.
  • From S0: misleading chart, graphical perception, Anscombe's quartet, and empirical distribution.

The concepts introduced by PC1 are defined in their respective lessons: research paper structure (Lesson 1), results-first introduction (Lesson 2), standalone table and figure redesign (Lesson 3), and expert presentation (Lesson 4).

Placement diagnostic

Ten questions: eight on foundational prerequisites (P0.1 and S0), and two skill checks on what Rungs 1 and 2 of the project teach. The mastery threshold is 80 percent (8 of 10 items). To skip Rungs 1 and 2, you must achieve 80% and correctly answer both skill checks.

  1. What is the central function of the identification section in an empirical paper?

    Options

    Identification isolates the exogenous variation in the treatment variable from omitted confounders and reverse causality (P0.1 identification).

  2. In Anscombe's quartet, which property is shared by all four bivariate datasets?

    Options

    Anscombe's quartet demonstrates that four datasets with virtually identical numerical summary statistics possess completely distinct visual distributions (S0 Anscombe's quartet).

  3. According to Cleveland-McGill graphical perception experiments, which visual task is performed with the lowest error?

    Options

    Position along a common scale sits at Tier 1 of the graphical perception hierarchy, offering the highest decoding accuracy (S0 graphical perception).

  4. What does steelmanning an opposing empirical claim entail?

    Options

    Steelmanning requires constructing the strongest possible intellectual version of a critique before responding (P0.1 steelmanning).

  5. Why is a bar chart with a truncated non-zero vertical baseline classified as a misleading chart?

    Options

    Bars communicate through visual length; truncating the baseline exaggerates physical length ratios relative to true numerical ratios (S0 misleading chart).

  6. What distinguishes a replication study from a novel contribution in research craft?

    Options

    Scientific replication independently reproduces the data construction, econometric specification, and tables of a published study (P0.1 replication).

  7. Under the evidence grading system, what constitutes a Grade T claim?

    Options

    Grade T denotes an exact mathematical definition, identity, or theorem whose conditions and proof are established on the page (P0.1 evidence grade).

  8. What is the primary role of a robustness check in empirical economics?

    Options

    Robustness checks evaluate whether empirical conclusions are sensitive to discretionary modelling choices or sample cutoffs (P0.1 robustness check).

  9. Skill check (Rung 1): Under the results-first introduction standard scaffolded in Rung 1, what is the required sentence index (an integer: 1, 2, or 3) in the introduction where the primary quantitative empirical estimate must appear?

    The results-first rule mandates that the primary empirical finding appear in Sentence 1 (with an allowable threshold of sentence 2).

  10. Skill check (Rung 2): In the Rung 2 completion problem, which of the following provides the compliant results-first sentence structure for the sovereign debt haircut study?

    Options

    The compliant sentence contains all five required elements: mechanism, direction (reduce), magnitude with units (185 basis points), standardized shock (>30% haircuts), and sample boundaries (48 episodes, 1980–2018).

Lessons

Four lessons of six study hours each, summing to 24 lesson hours. Each page declares its study hours in its data-hours attribute. Together, the lessons establish the craft foundations for scientific writing, exhibit construction, and expert defense.

L01 · 6 h Structure of a Finance Paper The canonical six-section architecture, functional section allocations, the 10-15-20-30-20-5 word budget, and the first-number position rule. L02 · 6 h An Introduction That States the Result The results-first mandate, the five-element sentence anatomy, unrounded estimation arithmetic, and bounded empirical claim scope. L03 · 6 h Tables That Stand Alone and Figure Redesign The six-point standalone table checklist, Cleveland-McGill graphical perception hierarchies, and truncated-axis distortion arithmetic. L04 · 6 h Presenting to an Expert Audience Conference presentation slot budgeting, slide pacing bounds, results-first slide decks, and the three-step adversarial Q&A defense protocol.

The project ladder

The module concludes with the Rewrite a Weak Paper Introduction capstone project. Rungs 1 and 2 scaffold the core rewrite and annotation skills; to skip them, you must achieve 80% on the placement diagnostic and answer both skill checks.

  1. Worked example

    Study the fully solved reference rewrite of hypothetical introduction H1, annotated line by line with research craft justifications.

  2. Completion problem

    Complete the headline result and roadmap sentences of introduction H2 and verify passing status in the instant browser runner.

  3. Independent project

    Rewrite all three hypothetical introductions (H1, H2, H3), log 12 structured annotations in annotations.md, verify compliance via the structural self-check lab, and score your submission against the rubric.

  4. Stretch

    Design a 6-slide expert conference presentation deck for H1 or construct a publication-grade standalone regression table for H2.

Hours budget

The module is budgeted for 30 total hours: 24 hours of lessons (4 lessons × 6 hours) and 6 hours for the capstone project (20.0% of the module). The project hours split into five sequential activities: Rung 1 worked example (1 h), Rung 2 completion problem (1 h), drafting three independent rewrites (2 h), compiling the annotation log (1 h), and self-assessment against annotated exemplars (1 h). The components sum to \(1 + 1 + 2 + 1 + 1 = 6\) hours.

Data status

No real financial dataset is required. All numerical exercises, paper outlines, and regression exhibits in this module compute from mathematical definitions or operate on SIMULATED models of empirical finance studies. No external data files are embedded, and no network requests are issued.
