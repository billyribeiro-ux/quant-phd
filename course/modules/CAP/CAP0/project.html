Rungs 1 and 2

Lab 1 · Worked example: End-to-end model articulation and hedge derivation

This worked example demonstrates the fundamental accounting and risk mechanics: verifying exact balance sheet articulation where changes in assets match changes in liabilities plus equity, and computing the optimal minimum-variance hedge ratio.

"""Lab Project 1 (Worked Example): End-to-end corporate finance model and valuation."""

from __future__ import annotations

import numpy as np


def main() -> None:
    # 1. Three-statement articulation check on sample base
    rev = 1000.0
    cogs = 500.0
    opex = 250.0
    ebitda = rev - cogs - opex
    depr = 60.0
    ebit = ebitda - depr
    interest = 15.0
    ebt = ebit - interest
    tax = ebt * 0.25
    net_income = ebt - tax
    dividends = net_income * 0.30

    capex = 60.0
    delta_nwc = 20.0
    cfo = net_income + depr - delta_nwc
    cfi = -capex
    cff = -dividends
    delta_cash = cfo + cfi + cff

    delta_assets = delta_cash + delta_nwc + (capex - depr)
    delta_liab_eq = net_income - dividends
    gap = delta_assets - delta_liab_eq

    print(f"Net Income: {net_income:.2f}")
    print(f"CFO: {cfo:.2f}, CFI: {cfi:.2f}, CFF: {cff:.2f}")
    print(f"Delta Cash: {delta_cash:.2f}")
    print(f"Articulation Gap: {gap:.10f}")

    # 2. Minimum-variance hedge ratio
    r_spot = np.array([0.02, -0.01, 0.04, -0.03, 0.01])
    r_futures = np.array([0.018, -0.009, 0.038, -0.028, 0.011])
    cov_sf = float(np.cov(r_spot, r_futures)[0, 1])
    var_f = float(np.var(r_futures, ddof=1))
    h_star = cov_sf / var_f
    print(f"Optimal Hedge Ratio h*: {h_star:.4f}")


main()

Observe that balance sheet articulation holds with an exact zero gap when cash is dynamically tracked as the sum of operating, investing, and financing cash flows. Simultaneously, the minimum-variance hedge ratio dampens currency cash-flow volatility in accordance with empirical corporate risk management theory.

Rung 3 · Specification

Context
A multinational industrial and technology enterprise is reviewing its five-year corporate financial policy, dividend payout strategy, capital expenditure program, and foreign exchange exposure prior to a comprehensive Board of Directors and Investment Committee review. As the chief corporate finance officer and valuation advisor, you must construct an integrated three-statement financial model, audit historical accounting quality using forensic ratios, calculate dynamic enterprise and equity valuations, optimize capital structure against financial distress and covenant constraints, design an ASC 815 qualifying hedging program, and defend your strategic recommendations against hostile committee interrogation.
Inputs
A calibrated corporate financial dataset provided by make_instance(seed) in grader/instance.py, containing baseline operational figures (revenues, operating margins, working capital cycle days DSO/DIO/DPO, capex and depreciation schedules), market risk parameters (risk-free rate, equity risk premium, unlevered asset beta, pre-tax borrowing cost, shares outstanding), two consecutive years of audited balance sheet and income statement items for forensic auditing, capital structure distress parameters (logistic distress function, covenant limits, fixed overhead requirements), and 36 monthly return shocks for spot currency exposures and derivative hedging instruments.
Required outputs
Two primary unified deliverables:
  1. An autograded computational Python module capstone_engine.py implementing:
    • forecast_three_statements: 5-year integrated forecast with dynamic cash roll-forward and verified zero balance sheet articulation gap.
    • audit_forensic_quality: Computation of Sloan accruals, 8 Beneish indices, and overall M-Score with manipulation risk classification.
    • compute_dynamic_dcf: Unlevered free cash flow (FCFF) calculation, dynamic path-dependent WACC discounting using mid-year convention, Gordon growth terminal value, and equity value per share bridge.
    • optimize_capital_and_liquidity: Trade-off theory leverage optimization, debt covenant headroom audit (ICR and debt/EBITDA), and minimum precautionary liquidity buffer calculation.
    • design_hedging_program: Minimum-variance hedge ratio determination, ASC 815 / IFRS 9 regression effectiveness testing, and 95% parametric Value-at-Risk reduction calculation.
    • simulate_board_scenarios: Multi-scenario sensitivity analysis across Base, Bull, and Bear cases, evaluating scenario-weighted price targets and breakeven margin of safety.
  2. A formal 8-to-12 page Board & Investment Committee Report Pack and Presentation Brief comprising:
    • Executive Summary: Strategic investment thesis, scenario-weighted target share price, capital allocation recommendations, and covenant risk posture.
    • Section 1: Equity Initiation & Industry Landscape (FC4 Five Forces, competitive positioning, and long-term earnings drivers).
    • Section 2: Forensic Accounting & Virtual Data Room Audit (Sloan accruals analysis, Beneish M-Score interpretation, off-balance-sheet commitments, and working capital quality).
    • Section 3: Valuation Architecture (Three-statement projection tables, dynamic WACC schedule, DCF sensitivity heatmaps, and multiple cross-checks).
    • Section 4: Capital Structure & Liquidity Policy (Trade-off optimization curve, interest tax shields vs distress costs, covenant compliance stress tests, and dividend payout recommendations).
    • Section 5: Corporate Hedging Program (ASC 815 hedge effectiveness audit, VaR reduction, and operational FX risk management).
    • Section 6: Adversarial Board Q&A Defense (Written rebuttals responding to the 16 standard attack angles from the project question bank).
Constraints
Deterministic execution under Python 3.14 with NumPy and SciPy; strict zero balance sheet articulation gap across all forecast periods (\(|\Delta\text{Assets} - \Delta(\text{Liabilities} + \text{Equity})| < 10^{-6}\)); mid-year discounting convention applied to explicit FCFF cash flows; terminal growth rate strictly bounded below terminal WACC; all substantive assertions in the board presentation pack must cite verified literature registry keys, exact equations, and data-room exhibits.
Grading mode
The curriculum grades this project by both autograder and rubric: the computational module is graded automatically against deterministic pytest properties across 50 random seeds; the written board presentation pack and defense memo are evaluated by rubric across 5 doctoral-level criteria and four performance levels, calibrated against annotated exemplars.

Exception & Boundary Conditions

In corporate financial engineering and valuation, standard textbook formulas break down under severe economic stress. Your model and presentation must handle the following institutional edge cases explicitly:

Boundary Condition Economic Failure Mode Model & Presentation Resolution
Negative Operating Profit (\(\text{EBIT} < 0\)) Interest tax shield fails because current taxable income is zero; NOPAT formula produces distorted tax credits. Carry forward net operating losses (NOL) subject to statutory limitations (80% of future taxable income under TCJA); set instantaneous tax shield to zero until taxable earnings recover.
Insolvency / Negative Book Equity Hamada formula produces negative levered beta or division by zero when debt-to-equity ratio approaches singularity. Cap leverage at structural reorganization threshold; switch to Adjusted Present Value (APV) or Merton structural model where equity is modeled as a call option on firm assets.
Terminal Growth Exceeding WACC (\(g \ge WACC\)) Gordon growth denominator \(WACC - g \le 0\) produces negative or infinite terminal enterprise value. Impose macroeconomic convergence constraint: terminal growth rate \(g\) cannot exceed long-run nominal GDP growth (2.0%–2.5%); enforce model assertion failure if \(WACC - g \le 0.01\).
Covenant Breach Drawdown Operating shock drops EBITDA below minimum interest coverage covenant (\(ICR < ICR_{\min}\)), triggering technical default. Model liquidity acceleration: activate emergency liquidity credit line, suspend discretionary share repurchases and dividends, and incorporate covenant waiver restructuring fees.
Hedge Ineffectiveness Discontinuity Basis risk causes regression \(R^2 < 0.80\) or slope \(\beta \notin [0.80, 1.25]\), disqualifying derivative from ASC 815 hedge accounting. Deregister hedge accounting; record all derivative mark-to-market fluctuations directly through earnings statement (P&L) rather than Other Comprehensive Income (OCI), highlighting earnings volatility in board notes.

Exemplar · Level 4 (Exemplary Board Presentation & Defense)

Part 1: Executive Memorandum to the Board of Directors

To: Board of Directors & Investment Committee
From: Head of Strategic Finance & Valuation
Date: November 10, 2026
Subject: Integrated Capital Policy, Dynamic Valuation, and FX Hedging Recommendation for Titan Industrial Dynamics (NYSE: TID)

Executive Recommendation: We initiate coverage on Titan Industrial Dynamics with a BUY recommendation and a 12-month scenario-weighted price target of $44.80 per share, representing a 24.4% upside over the current market quote of $36.00. Simultaneously, we advise the Board to: (1) maintain the targeted net leverage at \(2.2\times\) EBITDA, capturing an estimated $42.5M in net interest tax shields while preserving 140 bps of Interest Coverage Ratio covenant headroom; (2) execute an ASC 815 compliant minimum-variance FX forward hedging program for our €250M European commercial exposure, which reduces cash-flow volatility by 88.4% and saves $14.2M in 95% monthly Value-at-Risk; and (3) sustain a minimum precautionary cash buffer of $85.0M to ensure six months of operational overhead coverage under severe recessionary stress.

Part 2: Board Defense & Adversarial Pre-Mortem

Adversarial Pre-Mortem Scenario: It is November 2028. Titan Industrial's stock has plunged 45% to $19.80, and the company has received a covenant breach warning letter from its syndicate banks. What caused this failure? Our pre-mortem audit reveals that European automotive revenues declined 25% amidst stagflation, while fixed working capital requirements (DSO expanding from 45 to 68 days) drained $75M in liquidity. Furthermore, because management failed to hedge input aluminum costs, operating margins contracted 450 bps, cutting EBITDA from $280M to $155M and driving the Interest Coverage Ratio down to \(2.1\times\) (breaching the \(2.5\times\) covenant threshold).

Defense & Risk Mitigation Strategy:

  1. Forensic Quality Assurance: We audited TID's reported earnings per Beneish (1999) and Sloan (1996). The 8-variable Beneish M-Score of TID is \(-2.42\), comfortably below the \(-1.78\) red-flag threshold. Days Sales in Receivables Index (\(DSRI = 1.04\)) and Gross Margin Index (\(GMI = 1.02\)) confirm that reported top-line expansion represents authentic cash collections rather than aggressive revenue acceleration.
  2. Capital Structure & Trade-off Theory: Following Graham & Harvey (2001) and Bates, Kahle & Stulz (2009), corporate debt capacity is constrained by cash flow volatility. Our empirical trade-off curve shows that levering TID beyond \(3.0\times\) EBITDA increases cumulative default probability from 1.2% to 11.8%, eroding $38M in expected distress costs. The recommended \(2.2\times\) leverage optimizes firm value at $1,280M.
  3. ASC 815 Qualifying Hedging Architecture: Following Froot, Scharfstein & Stein (1993) and Smith & Stulz (1985), hedging is valuable because it prevents costly underinvestment during liquidity shocks. Linear regression of spot EUR/USD returns against forward contracts yields a slope \(\beta = 1.048\) and \(R^2 = 0.884\), satisfying FASB ASC Topic 815 effectiveness standards and permitting cash-flow hedge accounting through OCI.

Adversarial Question Bank (16 Attack Angles)

When presenting an integrated corporate finance and equity case before an institutional Board of Directors or Investment Committee, expect aggressive interrogation across these 16 vetted attack angles:

  1. Working Capital Timing Articulation: How exactly does your model handle the balance sheet cash plug when operating cash flows turn negative during rapid inventory buildup? Demonstrate that no phantom balancing entries exist.
  2. Accrual Reversal & Earnings Quality: If operating accruals are positive, per Sloan (1996), by what quantitative factor will return on assets mean-revert in year \(t+1\)?
  3. Beneish Manipulation Exposure: Which specific sub-index of the Beneish M-Score is most sensitive to the company's capitalized development costs, and how does capitalizing rather than expensing R&D affect the Asset Quality Index (AQI)?
  4. Dynamic vs Static WACC: If the company executes a debt paydown schedule over the five-year forecast, why is discounting explicit cash flows at a single constant WACC mathematically invalid?
  5. Mid-Year Cash Flow Discounting Convention: Why does the end-of-year discounting convention systematically understate enterprise value for industrial firms with continuous revenue generation?
  6. Terminal Growth vs GDP Bound: Your model assumes a perpetual terminal growth rate \(g\). How does your target price change if \(g\) is constrained to the risk-free rate or long-run nominal GDP growth?
  7. Net Debt Bridge Integrity: In bridging from Enterprise Value to Equity Value, how did you treat restricted cash, operating lease liabilities under ASC 842, and unfunded pension obligations?
  8. Trade-Off Theory Concavity: At what specific debt-to-EBITDA ratio does the marginal present value of additional interest tax shields become strictly outweighed by the marginal increase in expected bankruptcy costs?
  9. Debt Covenant Headroom Sensitivity: Under a 200 bps macroeconomic rate hike combined with a 15% revenue decline, what is the exact probability that the Interest Coverage Ratio breaches the bank covenant?
  10. Precautionary Cash Holdings: Citing Bates, Kahle & Stulz (2009), why should a cyclical firm hold substantially more cash than implied by the Baumol-Tobin inventory model?
  11. Minimum-Variance Hedge Effectiveness: In your currency hedging regression \(R_{\text{spot}} = \alpha + \beta R_{\text{hedge}} + \epsilon\), what specific statistical thresholds must be satisfied for the hedge to qualify under ASC 815 hedge accounting?
  12. Hedge Accounting P&L Volatility: What occurs to reported GAAP earnings if your derivative program fails the 80%–125% effectiveness corridor and mark-to-market changes flow directly through P&L rather than OCI?
  13. Value-at-Risk Quantification: What is the parametric 95% one-month Value-at-Risk of the unhedged foreign currency cash flow, and how many dollars of VaR are preserved by your hedging architecture?
  14. Dividend Payout vs Share Repurchases: In light of the Lintner model and agency costs of free cash flow (Jensen 1986), why do you recommend dividends rather than opportunistic share buybacks?
  15. Project Pre-Mortem Catalysts: In your project pre-mortem, what is the single primary operational or macro catalyst that would cause your investment thesis to fail completely?
  16. Breakeven Margin of Safety: By what percentage can baseline EBITDA margin compress before the DCF target share price drops below the current market price?
