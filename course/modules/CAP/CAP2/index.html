The Great Debates

Quantitative finance advances not through dogma, but through disciplined, adversarial contestation of core paradigms. In this doctoral capstone, you master twelve canonical debates spanning market efficiency, macro-finance risk premia, empirical factor discovery, quantitative easing transmission, machine learning complexity, trend persistence, dynamic volatility scaling, secular real rates, fiscal inflation mechanics, and high-frequency market quality.

  • Prerequisites: AP4, AP8, F5, F6, E7, GM4, PR2, MS2, DV5
  • Unlocks: CAP4
  • Project: Debate tournament, autograder and rubric

Objectives

  1. Formulate formal, mathematical steelman arguments for both affirmative and negative sides of twelve canonical debate resolutions across neoclassical and behavioral paradigms.
  2. Construct structured evidence maps grading empirical claims (T, A, B, C, D, X) using verified registry literature, precise locators, and methodological classification.
  3. Define precise, falsifiable criteria for what evidence would settle a debate, specifying statistical test hurdles, required sample sizes, and out-of-sample identification conditions.
  4. Implement multiple testing hurdle adjustments per Harvey, Liu & Zhu (2016) and Empirical Bayes shrinkage algorithms to distinguish genuine anomalies from data-snooping artifacts.
  5. Evaluate post-discovery decay across quantitative anomalies and systematic strategies using Welch structural break tests and variance ratios.
  6. Simulate counterfactual policy trajectories under alternative structural macro-finance regimes (QE portfolio balance, FTPL inflation, HFT latency floors) to quantify macroeconomic impulse responses.

Prerequisites

This doctoral capstone synthesizes advanced empirical methodologies, macro-finance equilibrium models, and market microstructure dynamics. Students are expected to apply concepts established in prerequisite units without foundational review:

  • From AP4 & AP8: consumption-based stochastic discount factors, habit formation, rare disasters, and joint hypothesis testing.
  • From F5 & F6: the factor zoo, publication bias, anomaly decay, and machine learning asset pricing.
  • From E7: vector autoregressions, local projections, and structural macroeconomic identification.
  • From GM4: uncovered interest parity, foreign exchange carry trades, and currency risk premia.
  • From PR2: systematic trend following, time-series momentum, and volatility scaling.
  • From MS2: limit order books, high-frequency quote dynamics, and Hasbrouck price discovery.
  • From DV5: state-space models and Kalman filter estimation.

Placement diagnostic

Test your mastery of foundational quantitative debate concepts and project mechanics. Learners meeting the 80% mastery threshold may proceed directly to the capstone project specification.

  1. Under the Bonferroni multiple testing framework of Harvey, Liu & Zhu (2016), if an empirical researcher evaluates \(M = 316\) candidate factor models at nominal significance \(\alpha = 0.05\), what critical two-sided t-statistic hurdle is required?

    Options

    Harvey, Liu & Zhu (2016) show that with \(M = 316\) candidate factors, the adjusted significance level is \(\alpha / M = 0.05 / 316 \approx 0.000158\), yielding a two-sided normal quantile of \(\Phi^{-1}(1 - \alpha / (2M)) \approx 3.78\), with the empirical t-statistic hurdle establishing \(|t| \ge 3.00\) as the minimum robust cutoff.

  2. In Empirical Bayes shrinkage of cross-sectional anomaly return estimates \(\hat{\mu}_i\) toward zero, what determines the shrinkage weight \(w_i = \tau^2 / (\tau^2 + \sigma_i^2)\)?

    Options

    Empirical Bayes precision weighting shrinks noisy estimates with high standard errors \(\sigma_i\) heavily toward the prior mean (0), while estimates with low sampling variance retain weights close to 1.0.

  3. Why does Fama's (1970) Joint Hypothesis Problem imply that an empirical test can never reject market efficiency in isolation?

    Options

    Fama (1970, 1991) proved that to determine whether an asset price deviates from fundamental value, one must specify what fundamental value is using an asset pricing model. A rejection tests the joint null of market efficiency and the chosen asset pricing model.

  4. Under the Mehra & Prescott (1985) power utility equity premium calibration, what level of relative risk aversion \(\gamma\) is required to reconcile the historical US equity premium (~6%) with smooth consumption growth (~1.5% vol)?

    Options

    Because consumption growth covariance with equity returns is small, standard time-separable power utility requires \(\gamma > 30\) to match the historical equity premium, giving rise to the equity premium puzzle.

Lessons

  1. L01 Capital Markets Foundations: Market Efficiency & The Equity Risk Premium Resolutions 1 & 2: The Joint Hypothesis Problem, Shiller excess volatility, disaster risks, and consumption macro-finance. (14 hours)
  2. L02 Cross-Sectional Equities: Value Persistence & The Factor Zoo Resolutions 3 & 4: Value spread drawdowns, multiple hypothesis hurdles, publication bias, and Empirical Bayes shrinkage. (14 hours)
  3. L03 International & Central Banking: Quantitative Easing & The UIP Puzzle Resolutions 5 & 6: QE preferred habitat transmission, signaling channels, Fama forward discount regressions, and currency carry crashes. (14 hours)
  4. L04 Statistical Learning & Systematic Trends: Algorithmic Complexity & Trend Survived 2009 Resolutions 7 & 8: High-dimensional double descent, non-linear machine learning, time-series momentum, and post-GFC regime shifts. (14 hours)
  5. L05 Dynamic Sizing & Macro Equilibrium: Volatility Management & The Secular Fall in r* Resolutions 9 & 10: Moreira-Muir volatility timing net of transaction costs, and state-space Kalman filter estimation of natural interest rates. (14 hours)
  6. L06 Fiscal Macroeconomics & Microstructure: FTPL Inflation & High-Frequency Market Quality Resolutions 11 & 12: The Fiscal Theory of the Price Level during 2021–2023, and HFT liquidity provision vs latency arbitrage. (14 hours)

Project ladder

  1. Rung 1 · Worked example

    Step-by-step pipeline implementing multiple testing hurdles, Empirical Bayes shrinkage, and out-of-sample decay diagnostics.

  2. Rung 2 · Completion problem

    Complete precision-weighted shrinkage calculations and Welch structural decay statistics.

  3. Rung 3 · Independent project

    Debate tournament: Author eight quantitative briefs and build debate_engine.py passing 50 autograder seeds.

  4. Rung 4 · Stretch

    Advanced non-Gaussian mixture shrinkage priors and non-linear counterfactual policy impulse response engines.
