Trading Monetary Policy

Central bank policy decisions govern the global cost of capital, asset valuation multiples, currency parities, and market liquidity. GM3 develops the rigorous high-frequency identification toolkits and systematic trading architectures required to trade monetary policy announcements across global asset classes. You will master Fed funds futures pricing mechanics, the Kuttner scaling formula, the pre-FOMC announcement drift puzzle, Nakamura-Steinsson information effect decompositions, currency carry trade dynamics under central bank divergence, and cross-asset execution during volatility-crush windows.

  • Prerequisites: E7 Monetary Economics and S6 Causal Inference & Event Studies.
  • Project: Pre-FOMC Drift: Out-of-Sample Check, graded by autograder and rubric (20 hours).
  • Hours: 50 study hours (30 h across 5 lessons, 20 h capstone project).

Learning objectives

  1. Derive the Kuttner month-scaling formula for Fed funds futures jumps and formulate boundary condition adjustments for month-end announcements.
  2. Estimate asset class sensitivities to unanticipated policy actions using Bernanke-Kuttner regression frameworks and high-frequency 30-minute windows.
  3. Quantify the Lucca-Moench pre-FOMC announcement equity drift and test its statistical significance against non-parametric placebo null distributions.
  4. Decompose high-frequency interest rate surprises into pure monetary policy shocks versus Nakamura-Steinsson Delphic information effects using term structure PCA and sign restrictions.
  5. Analyze foreign exchange carry trade returns, central bank policy divergence, and asymmetric currency crash risks under uncovered interest parity failures.
  6. Formulate quantitative event execution strategies, accounting for pre-event implied volatility expansion, post-announcement volatility crush, and order-book liquidity depletion.

Placement diagnostic

Ten questions: eight on prerequisite E7 and S6 concepts, and two skill checks testing what rungs 1 and 2 of the project teach. The mastery threshold is 80 percent (at least eight of ten correct). To place out of rungs 1 and 2, you must achieve the mastery threshold and answer both skill checks correctly.

  1. In a 30-day calendar month, an FOMC rate announcement occurs on day \(d = 10\). Under Kuttner (2001) futures settlement mechanics, what is the exact scaling factor applied to the spot-month Fed funds futures price jump \(\Delta f_t\) to extract the discrete target surprise \(\Delta i_t^u\)?

    Options

    Because Fed funds futures settle on the monthly average effective rate, a rate change occurring on day \(d\) affects only the remaining \(M - d\) days of the month. The spot futures jump is \(\Delta f = \frac{M - d}{M} \Delta i^u\), so inverting yields the scaling factor \(\frac{M}{M - d} = 1.50\). Rung 1 skill check.

  2. When evaluating whether an empirical anomaly such as the pre-FOMC drift has decayed out-of-sample, why is Welch's two-sample t-test strictly required over Student's pooled t-test?

    Options

    In empirical finance, sample splits typically have unequal sample sizes (\(N_1 \ne N_2\)) and heterogeneous return volatilities (\(\sigma_1^2 \ne \sigma_2^2\)). Imposing pooled equal-variance assumptions severely distorts test size and inflates Type I error rates. Rung 2 skill check.

  3. In event study methodology (S6), why do researchers prefer tight intraday event windows (e.g., 30 minutes) over daily event windows when measuring monetary policy shocks?

    Options

    High-frequency identification relies on the assumption that in a narrow 30-minute window around the announcement, policy news dominates all other macro information, ensuring exogenous variation. S6 prerequisite.

  4. According to Bernanke & Kuttner (2005), what is the primary channel through which unanticipated Federal Reserve monetary policy rate hikes reduce broad equity prices?

    Options

    Campbell-Shiller variance decompositions in Bernanke & Kuttner (2005) reveal that unanticipated policy tightening primarily increases future expected excess equity returns (equity risk premia), accounting for the majority of the contemporaneous stock decline. E7 prerequisite.

  5. Under the Uncovered Interest Rate Parity (UIP) hypothesis (E1), what should happen to the exchange rate of a high-interest-rate currency relative to a low-interest-rate currency?

    Options

    UIP posits that expected currency depreciation offsets the nominal yield advantage, eliminating systematic excess returns from holding high-yielding currencies. E1 prerequisite.

  6. What empirical anomaly, originally formalized by Fama (1984), directly contradicts the UIP hypothesis and forms the foundation of FX carry trading?

    Options

    The forward premium puzzle shows that high-yielding currencies tend to appreciate or depreciate far less than predicted by interest differentials, creating persistent carry trade risk premia. E1 prerequisite.

  7. In modern causal inference (S6), how does a non-parametric placebo permutation test establish the statistical validity of an event-study effect?

    Options

    Placebo tests evaluate whether pseudo-events drawn on non-announcement days generate effects as large as the observed event, directly testing whether calendar clustering or heavy tails account for the finding. S6 prerequisite.

  8. What structural econometric issue arises when estimating Jordà local projections at horizons \(h > 0\) for multi-period cumulative responses?

    Options

    Cumulative multi-step dependent variables \(y_{t+h} - y_{t-1}\) induce MA(\(h\)) error correlation across successive observations, requiring heteroskedasticity-and-autocorrelation-consistent (HAC) covariance estimators. E7 prerequisite.

  9. In the Taylor rule formulation (E7), how does a central bank adjusting policy rates under the 'Taylor principle' ensure macroeconomic stability?

    Options

    The Taylor principle requires \(\phi_\pi > 1\); otherwise, higher inflation lowers real rates, further stimulating demand and causing indeterminacy or explosive inflation spirals. E7 prerequisite.

  10. What distinguishes central bank 'Delphic' forward guidance from 'Odyssean' forward guidance?

    Options

    Coined by Campbell et al. (2012), Delphic guidance forecasts future conditions like the Oracle of Delphi (often conveying information effects), while Odyssean guidance ties the central bank to the mast like Odysseus. E7 prerequisite.

Lesson sequence

L01 · 6 h High-Frequency Identification & FOMC Event Studies Fed funds futures mechanics, Kuttner scaling formula, narrow 30-minute event windows, and Bernanke-Kuttner equity response regressions. L02 · 6 h The Pre-FOMC Announcement Drift Lucca & Moench (2015) equity returns prior to scheduled FOMC meetings, international transmission, and non-parametric placebo permutation testing. L03 · 6 h The Fed Information Effect & Central Bank Communication Nakamura & Steinsson (2018) policy news shocks, Delphic information vs. pure policy shock identification via term-structure PCA and sign restrictions. L04 · 6 h Central Bank Policy Divergence & FX Carry Dynamics Asynchronous central bank cycles, UIP forward premium puzzles, FX carry trade returns, and asymmetric crash risk using daily market data. L05 · 6 h Macro Cross-Asset Execution & Event Trading Systems Options volatility crush, ATM straddle decay, order-book liquidity depletion, execution slippage, and institutional risk budgeting around announcements.

Capstone project ladder

The 20-hour capstone project, Pre-FOMC Drift: Out-of-Sample Check, leads from analytical worked examples to production-grade out-of-sample event study testing:

  1. Worked example: Trace the Kuttner futures scaling mathematics, pre-FOMC drift extraction, and placebo permutation testing on a deterministic fixture.
  2. Completion problem: Fill in the missing out-of-sample drift estimation, Welch-Satterthwaite test, and statistical survival criteria.
  3. Independent project: Build the complete event study engine in reference.py, passing all 7 statistical invariant tests across 50 seeds, and author an executive research note.
  4. Stretch problem: Deconstruct intraday statement vs. press conference market dynamics and volatility surface arbitrage.
