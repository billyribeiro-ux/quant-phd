Macro Data & Nowcasting

Global macro strategies live or die by the fidelity of their real-time economic signals. Quantitative backtests conditioned on ex-post revised macroeconomic data suffer from catastrophic look-ahead bias, presenting algorithms with future census benchmarks and multi-year revisions unknown at historical trade dates. GM1 establishes the rigorous econometric architecture required to track, nowcast, and trade macroeconomic data in real time. You will formalize bitemporal vintage matrices under the Croushore-Stark framework, estimate dynamic factor models via principal components across high-dimensional panels, solve the asynchronous publication dilemma using Kalman filtering with time-varying observation selection on ragged-edge datasets, decompose nowcast updates into release news versus backward revisions via the Giannone-Reichlin-Small identity, and construct stationary economic surprise indices. The module culminates in building Atlas A18, an end-to-end real-time nowcasting and macroeconomic regime monitoring dashboard.

  • Prerequisites: S3 Time Series & State-Space Models, E5 Macroeconomic Regimes & Asset Pricing, QR1 Research Data Engineering.
  • Project: Atlas A18: Nowcasting & Regime Dashboard, autograded deliverable.
  • Hours: 70 study hours (50 h across 5 lessons, 20 h capstone project).

Learning objectives

  1. Analyze real-time macroeconomic data vintages using bitemporal matrices and evaluate revision properties under Mankiw-Runkle-Shapiro news versus noise hypotheses.
  2. Estimate static dynamic factor models via principal components on high-dimensional panels and fit state-space VAR(1) factor transition dynamics.
  3. Formulate the Kalman filter on ragged-edge macroeconomic panels using time-varying observation selection matrices, proving the information monotonicity of posterior error covariances.
  4. Decompose real-time nowcast updates across consecutive data vintages into additive contributions from headline release news versus backward revisions.
  5. Construct exponentially smoothed macroeconomic surprise indices from standardized announcement errors and quantify empirical cross-asset market sensitivities.
  6. Classify macroeconomic cycle regimes and implement systematic multi-asset risk allocation in Atlas milestone A18.

Placement diagnostic

Ten questions: eight on prerequisite S3, E5, and QR1 foundations, and two skill checks testing what rungs 1 and 2 of the project teach. The mastery threshold is 80 percent (at least eight of ten correct). To place out of rungs 1 and 2, you must achieve the mastery threshold and answer both skill checks correctly.

  1. In a dynamic factor model estimated via static principal components on a standardized panel \(X\), how are consistent factor loadings \(\hat{\Lambda}\) obtained?

    Options

    Stock & Watson (2002) prove that the eigenvectors of the standardized sample covariance matrix consistently span the true factor loading space as \(N, T \to \infty\). Rung 1–2 skill check.

  2. How does the Kalman filter handle missing entries in a ragged-edge observation vector \(X_t\) during the measurement update?

    Options

    The selection matrix \(M_t\) reduces the measurement equation to observed dimensions only, enabling exact Bayesian state updating without heuristic imputation. Rung 1–2 skill check.

  3. Under the discrete-time linear Kalman filter from S3, what is the prior error covariance prediction equation given transition matrix \(A\), prior covariance \(P_{t-1|t-1}\), and state noise covariance \(Q\)?

    Options

    Linear state prediction propagates covariance through the quadratic form \(A P A'\) and adds process noise covariance \(Q\).

  4. In macroeconomic regime analysis from E5, what does an inverted Treasury yield curve (10Y yield minus 2Y yield < 0) historically signal?

    Options

    Yield curve inversions reflect monetary tightening and market expectations of subsequent economic contraction, serving as a reliable leading indicator of recessions.

  5. Under the QR1 bitemporal data model, what distinction separates valid time from publication transaction time?

    Options

    Bitemporal data architecture separates what period the observation describes (valid time) from when that estimate became known to the world (publication time).

  6. In a standardized macro panel, Factor 1 explains 35% of total variance, Factor 2 explains 25%, and Factor 3 explains 10%. What is the cumulative variance share (as a decimal) explained by the first two factors?

    The cumulative share is \(0.35 + 0.25 = 0.60\) (60%).

  7. Under the News Hypothesis of data revisions from Croushore & Stark (2001), what is the expected correlation between the initial data release and the subsequent historical revision?

    Options

    Under rational expectations, revision forecast errors are orthogonal to prior information sets, yielding zero covariance with initial announcements.

  8. What econometric problem arises when an unrestricted Vector Autoregression (VAR) is fitted to 100 macroeconomic indicators?

    Options

    Unrestricted VAR systems suffer parameter proliferation quadratic in the number of variables, necessitating factor models for dimension reduction.

  9. A single-factor Kalman filter has prior variance \(P = 0.50\), factor loading \(\lambda = 1.0\), and idiosyncratic noise \(R = 0.125\). Compute the scalar Kalman gain \(K = P \lambda / (\lambda^2 P + R)\).

    Innovation covariance is \(1.0^2 \times 0.50 + 0.125 = 0.625\). The Kalman gain is \(0.50 / 0.625 = 0.80\).

  10. Why does forward-filling missing entries in a macroeconomic panel distort time-series properties?

    Options

    Carrying forward last observations artificially sets \(\Delta X = 0\), distorting serial correlation and generating false jump innovations when actuals print.

Lessons

L01 · 10 h Real-Time Macro Vintages & Revision Risk Bitemporal vintage matrices, Croushore-Stark real-time foundations, Mankiw-Runkle-Shapiro news vs noise revision tests, and revision look-ahead bias in macro backtests. L02 · 10 h Dynamic Factor Models in State Space Static factor representation, Stock-Watson principal component extraction consistency, rotation indeterminacy, VAR(1) latent state transition, and covariance stationarity. L03 · 10 h Kalman Filter Nowcasting on Ragged Edges Asynchronous release calendars, ragged-edge observation masks, selection matrix Kalman filtering, information monotonicity of error covariance, and Mariano-Murasawa aggregation. L04 · 10 h News and Revision News Decomposition The anatomy of a nowcast update, Giannone-Reichlin-Small news attribution identity, separating headline release news from historical revisions, and exact mathematical additivity. L05 · 10 h Economic Surprise Index Construction Standardized announcement surprises, rolling volatility scaling, exponential decay half-life, empirical covariance stationarity, and cross-asset market sensitivities.
