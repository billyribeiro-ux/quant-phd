Atlas A18: Nowcasting & Regime Dashboard

Macroeconomic policy decisions and asset prices react continuously to asynchronous economic data releases. In this milestone project, you engineer Atlas A18 (atlas/macro.py): a real-time macroeconomic nowcasting, news decomposition, and regime monitoring engine. Your engine extracts static principal component factors from high-dimensional macroeconomic panels, tracks latent state dynamics via a VAR(1) state-space model, updates current-quarter GDP nowcasts across ragged-edge vintages using time-varying Kalman selection matrices, attributes nowcast revisions to release news versus backward revisions, constructs an exponentially weighted economic surprise index, and classifies macroeconomic cycle regimes to drive systematic cross-asset risk allocation.

Project Specification

Context
Institutional macro trading desks cannot wait for delayed quarterly GDP publications to rebalance risk. Atlas A18 integrates high-frequency asynchronous indicators (surveys, labor, industrial activity, inflation) into a point-in-time state-space nowcasting engine, insulating portfolio backtests from revision look-ahead bias and providing continuous probability updates across economic regimes.
Inputs
Historical balanced macroeconomic training panel \(X_{\text{balanced}} \in \mathbb{R}^{T_{\text{hist}} \times N}\), target series \(y \in \mathbb{R}^{T_{\text{hist}}}\), real-time ragged-edge test panel \(X_{\text{ragged}} \in \mathbb{R}^{T_{\text{rag}} \times N}\) with missing entries (NaN), consecutive vintage matrices \((X_{\text{old}}, X_{\text{new}})\), daily announcement surprise shocks \(S \in \mathbb{R}^{T_{\text{days}} \times N_{\text{ind}}}\), and importance weights \(w \in \mathbb{R}^{N_{\text{ind}}}\).
Required Outputs
A compliant Python module atlas/macro.py implementing the core contract classes and functions: extract_dynamic_factors, kalman_nowcast_ragged, decompose_nowcast_news, compute_economic_surprise_index, and classify_macro_regime.
Constraints
Zero look-ahead leakage across publication vintages; exact mathematical additivity of the news attribution decomposition; numerical stability and positive semi-definiteness of Kalman covariance matrices; strict adherence to PEP 8 standards with full type annotations passing mypy --strict and ruff.
Grading
Autograded by automated test harness suite (grader/test_project.py) across 50 independent pseudorandom seeds (0–49), accompanied by mutation testing killing 5 targeted implementation mutants.

Rung 1 · Worked example

Before implementing the complete Atlas A18 engine, review the architectural flow of a dynamic factor nowcaster:

  1. Standardization & Spectral Factor Extraction: Standardize the balanced panel \(X\) and compute the sample covariance matrix \(\Sigma = X' X / T\). Extract the \(r\) leading eigenvectors \(\Lambda\) and compute latent factors \(\hat{F} = X \Lambda\).
  2. VAR(1) Transition Estimation: Fit \(\hat{F}_t = A \hat{F}_{t-1} + u_t\) via ordinary least squares to estimate transition matrix \(A\) and residual covariance \(Q\).
  3. Selection Matrix Kalman Recursion: For each period in the ragged panel, construct boolean mask \(M_t\) selecting observed rows. Update predicted state \(\hat{F}_{t|t-1}\) with innovation \(\nu_t = X_t^* - \Lambda_t^* \hat{F}_{t|t-1}\) and gain \(K_t\).
  4. Target Nowcast & Variance: Project the terminal latent factor state \(\hat{F}_{T|T}\) through target regression weights \(\beta\) to generate \(\hat{y} = \beta' \hat{F}_{T|T}\) and confidence bounds \(\sqrt{\beta' P_{T|T} \beta}\).
