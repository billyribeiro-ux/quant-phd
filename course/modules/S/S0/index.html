Exploratory Data Analysis & Visualization

Before estimating a model or running an optimization, a quantitative researcher must interrogate raw market feeds. Financial datasets arrive contaminated by missing quotes, stale exchange feeds, unadjusted corporate splits, fat tails, and recording outliers. This module provides the mathematical and computational toolkit to profile distributions, detect anomalies robustly, understand missingness, and visualize quantitative data without self-deception.

  • Prerequisites: CS1 (Programming & Software Engineering), M4 (Probability & Distributions)
  • Unlocks: S1 (Statistical Inference), S2 (Linear Regression)
  • Project: Atlas A3: automated data-quality profiler (autograder)

35 h lessons · 15 h project (30%)

Objectives

  1. Compute empirical distribution functions and select optimal histogram bin widths under the Freedman–Diaconis and Scott rules.
  2. Evaluate the asymptotic breakdown point and influence functions of sample location and scale estimators, contrasting the mean and variance against the median and Median Absolute Deviation (MAD).
  3. Diagnose heavy tails, skewness, and multi-modality using Quantile–Quantile (Q-Q) plots against the theoretical normal distribution.
  4. Detect multivariate and univariate outliers while avoiding classical masking and swamping failures.
  5. Classify missing data into MCAR, MAR, and MNAR mechanisms and derive the estimation bias induced by complete-case truncation in financial panels.
  6. Identify stale pricing runs and unadjusted corporate action breaks in historical price series, deriving the split-adjusted continuous return.
  7. Ship Atlas milestone A3: an automated, typed data-quality profiler that audits any financial price dataset for gaps, stale prints, corporate breaks, and statistical outliers.

Prerequisites

S0 assumes fluency in Python, algorithmic thinking, and core probability theory from CS1 and M4. The following concepts are used directly without re-definition:

  • From CS1: simple returns, log returns, exception handling, and unit tests.
  • From M4: random variables, expectation, variance, normal distribution, quantile function, skewness, kurtosis, and heavy-tailed distributions.

Placement diagnostic

Ten questions evaluating prerequisites from CS1 and M4, plus two skill-check items on what rungs 1–2 of the project teach. Skip rungs 1–2 only if you reach the 80% mastery threshold and answer both skill checks correctly.

  1. If a stock trades at \(P_{t-1} = 100\) and closes at \(P_t = 105\), what is the continuous log return \(r_t\)?

    Options

    The continuously compounded log return is defined as \(r_t = \ln(P_t / P_{t-1})\).

  2. For a continuous random variable \(X\) with cumulative distribution function \(F(x)\), the theoretical quantile function \(Q(p)\) is:

    Options

    The quantile function is the generalized inverse of the CDF.

  3. What is the theoretical kurtosis \(\mathbb{E}[(X - \mu)^4] / \sigma^4\) of a standard normal random variable?

    The standard normal distribution has a kurtosis of 3.0 (excess kurtosis of 0.0).

  4. Under the Glivenko–Cantelli theorem, the empirical distribution function \(F_n(x)\) converges to the true CDF \(F(x)\):

    Options

    The fundamental theorem of statistics states \(\sup_{x \in \mathbb{R}} |F_n(x) - F(x)| \xrightarrow{\text{a.s.}} 0\).

  5. What is the asymptotic breakdown point of the sample mean in the presence of arbitrary contamination?

    Options

    The sample mean has a breakdown point of 0; a single corrupted observation can change it arbitrarily.

  6. To make the Median Absolute Deviation (MAD) an asymptotically unbiased estimator of standard deviation \(\sigma\) for normal data, by what factor must MAD be multiplied?

    For a standard normal distribution, \(\Phi^{-1}(0.75) \approx 0.67449\), so the consistency scaling factor is \(1 / \Phi^{-1}(0.75) \approx 1.4826\).

  7. In a Quantile–Quantile (Q-Q) plot against a normal reference, an S-shaped pattern with empirical quantiles exceeding theoretical quantiles in both tails signifies:

    Options

    When extreme sample quantiles are more extreme than normal quantiles, the distribution has heavier tails than the normal reference.

  8. Under Missing at Random (MAR), the probability of missingness:

    Options

    MAR means \(\mathbb{P}(M = 1 \mid Y_{\text{obs}}, Y_{\text{mis}}) = \mathbb{P}(M = 1 \mid Y_{\text{obs}})\).

  9. Skill Check 1: A stock undergoes an unadjusted 2-for-1 stock split, closing at \$100.00 before the split and \$50.00 after. What is the raw, unadjusted log return \(r_t^{\text{raw}}\) to 4 decimal places?

    \(r_t^{\text{raw}} = \ln(50.0 / 100.0) = \ln(0.5) \approx -0.69315\). The split-adjusted return adds \(\ln(2.0)\), restoring \(r_t = 0.0\).

  10. Skill Check 2: A financial price series contains 7 consecutive rows with price 84.50, followed by a missing print (NaN), followed by 6 rows with price 84.50. Under a stale run threshold of 5, what stale runs must be reported?

    Options

    Under Atlas A3 contract rules, a gap row ends a stale run; two distinct maximal runs of lengths 7 and 6 are reported.

Lessons

L01 · 8 h Distributions & Robust Summaries Empirical cumulative distribution functions, optimal histogram binning, breakdown points, median, MAD, and trimmed means. L02 · 7 h Quantile Diagnostics & Outliers Q-Q plots against theoretical distributions, tail diagnostic curves, masking and swamping phenomena, and Hampel outlier filtering. L03 · 6 h Missing Data Mechanisms Rubin's taxonomy (MCAR, MAR, MNAR), complete-case selection bias, delisting bias in equity panels, and inverse propensity weighting. L04 · 8 h Prices as Data: Stale Prints & Splits Non-synchronous trading, stale price runs, corporate action adjustments, and distinguishing breaks from statistical outliers. L05 · 6 h Graphical Perception & Misleading Charts Anscombe's quartet, Cleveland–McGill elementary perceptual tasks, dual axes, truncated baselines, and dishonest visualization devices.

The project ladder

  1. Worked example

    Walk through an annotated, complete data-quality profiler on a small SIMULATED price trajectory, observing gap tracking, stale run intervals, split adjustments, and MAD scaling step by step.

  2. Completion problem

    Implement missing detection routines for gap identification, run-length tracking, and split-adjusted return validation with instant inline testing.

  3. Independent project

    Build Atlas milestone A3: an automated data-quality profiler strictly adhering to atlas/A3.contract.md, tested across 50 seeded test cases by the autograder. Project specification.

  4. Stretch

    Apply your profiler to real historical market data (Federal Reserve H.10 FX rates and H.15 Treasury yields), examining real-world stale prints, COVID March 2020 low-yield spikes, and calendar limitations.
