Pod Allocation Simulator

Simulate a multi-manager quantitative platform with drawdown stops, calibrate stop-loss thresholds that truncate toxic alpha decay without prematurely liquidating skilled managers, and evaluate dynamic cushion capital allocation against Equal Weight and Risk Parity benchmarks.

  • Deliverables: Platform simulation engine (simulator.py) and executive risk memo (memo.md)
  • Evaluation: Dual mode (autograder for simulation code; rubric for executive memo)
  • Time budget: 12 hours (30.0% of module hours)

The project ladder

The project develops through a four-rung ladder from concrete worked examples to independent implementation and open-ended research extension.

  1. Worked example

    A concrete, annotated implementation verifying Magdon-Ismail expected drawdown, Grossman-Zhou floor protection, and discrete path drawdown monitoring.

  2. Completion

    An interactive completion lab implementing distance-to-stop cushion calculation and multi-pod capital allocation under inverse-volatility and cushion weighting.

  3. Independent project

    Full implementation of simulator.py simulating a 10-pod hedge fund platform with soft and hard drawdown stops, evaluated across 50 random market seeds against the browser pytest suite, accompanied by an executive allocation memo memo.md.

  4. Stretch

    Open-ended research extension exploring jump-diffusion returns, endogenous fire-sale price impact upon pod liquidation, and Bayesian probation recovery rules.

Lab 1 · Drawdown Analytics & Cushion Policy Worked Example

Under zero drift with sigma = 0.20 and T = 1.0 year, the continuous Magdon-Ismail theoretical expectation is 0.2507 (25.07%). Under Grossman-Zhou with floor alpha = 0.85, an investor with peak M = 110 and current wealth W = 100 has floor F = 93.5, cushion C = 6.5, and risky allocation 16.25.

"""Lab PR5-project-1: Project Rung 1 Worked Example Twin.

SIMULATED: Analytical checks for expected MDD, Grossman-Zhou cushion, and pod drawdown tracking.
"""

from __future__ import annotations

import math

import numpy as np


def main() -> None:
    print("Project Rung 1 · Worked Example: Drawdown Analytics & Cushion Policy")
    print("=" * 68)

    # 1. Magdon-Ismail zero-drift expected MDD
    sigma = 0.20
    t_years = 1.0
    theo_mdd = sigma * math.sqrt(math.pi * t_years / 2.0)
    print(f"1. Magdon-Ismail Zero-Drift MDD: sigma={sigma:.2f}, T={t_years:.1f} yr")
    print(f"   Theoretical E[MDD(T)] = sigma * sqrt(pi*T/2) = {theo_mdd:.4f} (25.07%)")

    # 2. Grossman-Zhou cushion allocation
    wealth = 100.0
    peak = 110.0
    alpha = 0.85
    m_multiplier = 2.5
    floor = alpha * peak
    cushion = max(0.0, wealth - floor)
    dollar_alloc = m_multiplier * cushion
    print("\n2. Grossman-Zhou Floor Protection:")
    print(f"   Wealth W = {wealth:.1f}, Peak M = {peak:.1f}, Floor alpha = {alpha:.2f}")
    print(f"   Floor F = alpha * M = {floor:.2f}")
    print(f"   Cushion C = max(0, W - F) = {cushion:.2f}")
    print(f"   Risky Dollar Allocation = m* * C = {dollar_alloc:.2f}")

    # 3. Pod drawdown tracking
    w_series = [100.0, 108.0, 102.0, 96.0, 105.0]
    peaks = [float(p) for p in np.maximum.accumulate(w_series)]
    dds = [float((p - w) / p) for p, w in zip(peaks, w_series, strict=False)]
    mdd = max(dds)
    print("\n3. Discrete Wealth Path Drawdown Tracking:")
    print(f"   Wealth path:  {w_series}")
    print(f"   Peak path:    {peaks}")
    print(f"   Drawdown:     {[round(d, 4) for d in dds]}")
    print(f"   Maximum DD:   {mdd:.4f} (11.11%)")


main()

Rung 3 · Independent project (Graded Deliverable)

Project specification

Context
A multi-manager quantitative hedge fund platform operates a pod structure across 10 trading teams with heterogeneous skill, volatility, and correlation. The Chief Risk Officer mandates the design and implementation of an institutional platform simulator to evaluate pod-level stop-loss rules (soft and hard stops) and dynamic capital allocation policies. The objective is to maximize platform Calmar ratio and Sharpe ratio while containing platform maximum drawdown within institutional budget limits.
Inputs
The problem instance generated by make_instance(seed: int) contains:
  • pod_params: List of 10 pod dictionaries, specifying mu, sigma, skill (bool), soft_stop, and hard_stop.
  • corr_rho: Average pairwise return correlation across pods \(\rho \in [0.08, 0.24]\).
  • n_days: 252 trading days (1-year horizon).
  • risk_free_rate: Risk-free rate \(r \in [0.02, 0.04]\).
  • platform_budget_mdd: Maximum tolerable platform drawdown \(D_{\mathrm{max}} \in [0.08, 0.12]\).
Required outputs
Two deliverables submitted to the grading engine:
  1. simulator.py: Fully typed Python 3.14 module implementing:
    • expected_mdd_brownian: Brownian expected maximum drawdown.
    • grossman_zhou_cushion_allocation: Grossman-Zhou floor protection.
    • compute_pod_drawdowns: Running peaks, drawdowns, and peak-to-trough MDD.
    • compute_allocation_weights: Capital weights across active pods.
    • simulate_platform: Full platform simulation under stops.
  2. memo.md: Executive Risk Committee Memorandum (1,500–2,500 words) presenting empirical simulation findings, analyzing the stop-loss paradox, comparing allocation policies, and formulating institutional governance recommendations.
Constraints
  • Python 3.14 standard library, NumPy, and SciPy only. No external machine learning or simulation packages.
  • Full type hints (compatible with mypy --strict).
  • Runtime: under 2.0 seconds per seed, under 30 seconds across 50 seeds.
  • Strict deterministic reproducibility using seeded NumPy random generators.
Grading
The curriculum grades this project by both autograder and rubric. The Python simulator is autograded across 50 random seeds (0–49) against mathematical property tests. The policy memo is evaluated by rubric across five performance criteria.

"""Lab PR5-project-3: Project Rung 3 Full Platform Simulator Twin.

SIMULATED: Full multi-manager platform execution and policy comparison on seed 0.
"""

from __future__ import annotations

import math
import random
from typing import Any

import numpy as np


def make_instance(seed: int) -> dict[str, Any]:
    """Generate deterministic parameters for a multi-manager pod platform."""
    rng = random.Random(seed)

    n_pods = 10
    n_skilled = 6
    n_unskilled = 4

    mus: list[float] = []
    sigmas: list[float] = []
    skills: list[int] = []
    soft_stops: list[float] = []
    hard_stops: list[float] = []

    for _ in range(n_skilled):
        mu = round(0.08 + 0.10 * rng.random(), 4)
        sigma = round(0.12 + 0.10 * rng.random(), 4)
        soft_stop = round(0.04 + 0.02 * rng.random(), 4)
        hard_stop = round(soft_stop + 0.03 + 0.03 * rng.random(), 4)
        mus.append(mu)
        sigmas.append(sigma)
        skills.append(1)
        soft_stops.append(soft_stop)
        hard_stops.append(hard_stop)

    for _ in range(n_unskilled):
        mu = round(-0.04 + 0.07 * rng.random(), 4)
        sigma = round(0.14 + 0.11 * rng.random(), 4)
        soft_stop = round(0.04 + 0.02 * rng.random(), 4)
        hard_stop = round(soft_stop + 0.03 + 0.03 * rng.random(), 4)
        mus.append(mu)
        sigmas.append(sigma)
        skills.append(0)
        soft_stops.append(soft_stop)
        hard_stops.append(hard_stop)

    corr_rho = round(0.08 + 0.16 * rng.random(), 4)
    risk_free_rate = round(0.02 + 0.02 * rng.random(), 4)
    platform_budget_mdd = round(0.08 + 0.04 * rng.random(), 4)

    return {
        "seed": seed,
        "n_pods": n_pods,
        "mus": mus,
        "sigmas": sigmas,
        "skills": skills,
        "soft_stops": soft_stops,
        "hard_stops": hard_stops,
        "corr_rho": corr_rho,
        "n_days": 252,
        "t_years": 1.0,
        "risk_free_rate": risk_free_rate,
        "platform_budget_mdd": platform_budget_mdd,
    }


def compute_allocation_weights(
    policy: str,
    active_mask: np.ndarray,
    current_drawdowns: np.ndarray,
    hard_stops: np.ndarray,
    sigmas: np.ndarray,
) -> np.ndarray:
    """Compute platform portfolio weights across pods for a given risk policy."""
    n_pods = len(active_mask)
    weights = np.zeros(n_pods, dtype=float)
    n_active = int(np.sum(active_mask))

    if n_active == 0:
        return weights

    if policy == "equal":
        weights[active_mask] = 1.0 / float(n_active)
        return weights

    if policy == "risk_parity":
        inv_vols = 1.0 / np.maximum(sigmas[active_mask], 1e-4)
        total_inv_vol = float(np.sum(inv_vols))
        if total_inv_vol > 0.0:
            weights[active_mask] = inv_vols / total_inv_vol
        else:
            weights[active_mask] = 1.0 / float(n_active)
        return weights

    if policy == "cushion":
        cushions = np.maximum(0.0, hard_stops[active_mask] - current_drawdowns[active_mask])
        vols = np.maximum(sigmas[active_mask], 1e-4)
        raw_scores = cushions / vols
        total_score = float(np.sum(raw_scores))
        if total_score > 1e-7:
            weights[active_mask] = raw_scores / total_score
        else:
            weights[active_mask] = 1.0 / float(n_active)
        return weights

    msg = f"Unknown allocation policy: {policy!r}."
    raise ValueError(msg)


def simulate_platform(
    instance: dict[str, Any],
    policy: str = "cushion",
    seed: int | None = None,
) -> dict[str, Any]:
    """Execute dynamic multi-pod simulation under drawdown stops and capital allocation."""
    use_seed = instance["seed"] if seed is None else seed
    rng = np.random.default_rng(use_seed)

    n_pods = instance["n_pods"]
    n_days = instance.get("n_days", 252)
    t_years = instance.get("t_years", 1.0)
    dt = t_years / float(n_days)
    sqrt_dt = math.sqrt(dt)

    mus = np.array(instance["mus"], dtype=float)
    sigmas = np.array(instance["sigmas"], dtype=float)
    soft_stops = np.array(instance["soft_stops"], dtype=float)
    hard_stops = np.array(instance["hard_stops"], dtype=float)
    skill_flags = np.array(instance["skills"], dtype=bool)

    rho = float(instance.get("corr_rho", 0.15))
    corr = np.full((n_pods, n_pods), rho)
    np.fill_diagonal(corr, 1.0)
    l_chol = np.linalg.cholesky(corr)

    z_uncorr = rng.standard_normal((n_days, n_pods))
    z_corr = z_uncorr @ l_chol.T

    daily_returns_raw = (mus - 0.5 * sigmas**2) * dt + sigmas * sqrt_dt * z_corr

    pod_wealth = np.ones(n_pods, dtype=float)
    pod_peaks = np.ones(n_pods, dtype=float)
    pod_active = np.ones(n_pods, dtype=bool)

    platform_wealth = np.ones(n_days + 1, dtype=float)
    platform_peaks = np.ones(n_days + 1, dtype=float)
    platform_drawdowns = np.zeros(n_days + 1, dtype=float)
    platform_daily_returns: list[float] = []

    stopped_out = np.zeros(n_pods, dtype=bool)

    for t in range(n_days):
        current_dds = (pod_peaks - pod_wealth) / pod_peaks

        new_hard_stops = (current_dds >= hard_stops) & pod_active
        pod_active[new_hard_stops] = False
        stopped_out[new_hard_stops] = True

        if not np.any(pod_active):
            platform_daily_returns.append(0.0)
            platform_wealth[t + 1] = platform_wealth[t]
            platform_peaks[t + 1] = platform_peaks[t]
            platform_drawdowns[t + 1] = (platform_peaks[t + 1] - platform_wealth[t + 1]) / platform_peaks[t + 1]
            continue

        leverage_multipliers = np.ones(n_pods, dtype=float)
        in_soft_zone = (current_dds >= soft_stops) & (current_dds < hard_stops) & pod_active
        leverage_multipliers[in_soft_zone] = 0.5

        weights = compute_allocation_weights(
            policy=policy,
            active_mask=pod_active,
            current_drawdowns=current_dds,
            hard_stops=hard_stops,
            sigmas=sigmas,
        )

        scaled_pod_returns = leverage_multipliers * daily_returns_raw[t]
        r_plat = float(np.sum(weights * scaled_pod_returns))
        platform_daily_returns.append(r_plat)

        pod_wealth[pod_active] *= 1.0 + scaled_pod_returns[pod_active]
        pod_peaks = np.maximum(pod_peaks, pod_wealth)

        platform_wealth[t + 1] = platform_wealth[t] * (1.0 + r_plat)
        platform_peaks[t + 1] = max(platform_peaks[t], platform_wealth[t + 1])
        platform_drawdowns[t + 1] = (platform_peaks[t + 1] - platform_wealth[t + 1]) / platform_peaks[t + 1]

    plat_rets_arr = np.array(platform_daily_returns)
    mean_daily = float(np.mean(plat_rets_arr)) if len(plat_rets_arr) > 0 else 0.0
    std_daily = float(np.std(plat_rets_arr, ddof=1)) if len(plat_rets_arr) > 1 else 0.0

    ann_return = mean_daily * 252.0
    ann_vol = std_daily * math.sqrt(252.0)
    sharpe = ann_return / ann_vol if ann_vol > 1e-6 else 0.0

    platform_mdd = float(np.max(platform_drawdowns))
    calmar = ann_return / platform_mdd if platform_mdd > 1e-6 else 0.0

    n_skilled = int(np.sum(skill_flags))
    n_unskilled = int(np.sum(~skill_flags))

    skilled_stopped = int(np.sum(stopped_out & skill_flags))
    unskilled_stopped = int(np.sum(stopped_out & (~skill_flags)))

    skilled_churn_rate = float(skilled_stopped / n_skilled) if n_skilled > 0 else 0.0
    unskilled_stopped_rate = float(unskilled_stopped / n_unskilled) if n_unskilled > 0 else 0.0
    overall_survival_rate = float(np.sum(pod_active) / n_pods)

    return {
        "platform_annualized_return": ann_return,
        "platform_annualized_volatility": ann_vol,
        "platform_sharpe": sharpe,
        "platform_mdd": platform_mdd,
        "calmar_ratio": calmar,
        "skilled_churn_rate": skilled_churn_rate,
        "unskilled_stopped_rate": unskilled_stopped_rate,
        "overall_survival_rate": overall_survival_rate,
        "platform_wealth_path": platform_wealth,
        "platform_drawdown_path": platform_drawdowns,
    }


def main() -> None:
    seed = 0
    inst = make_instance(seed)

    print("Project Rung 3 · Full Platform Simulation (Seed 0)")
    print("=" * 72)
    print(f"Platform Setup: N={inst['n_pods']} pods (6 skilled, 4 unskilled), T=252 days")
    print(f"Average Pod Correlation rho={inst['corr_rho']:.4f}, Risk-free rate={inst['risk_free_rate']:.4f}")
    print("-" * 72)

    policies = ["equal", "risk_parity", "cushion"]

    print(f"{'Metric':<30} {'Equal Weight':<15} {'Risk Parity':<15} {'Drawdown Cushion':<15}")
    print("-" * 72)

    results = {p: simulate_platform(inst, policy=p, seed=seed) for p in policies}

    def fmt_pct(val: float) -> str:
        return f"{val * 100:.2f}%"

    def fmt_pct1(val: float) -> str:
        return f"{val * 100:.1f}%"

    def fmt_num(val: float) -> str:
        return f"{val:.4f}"

    rows = [
        ("Annualized Return", [fmt_pct(float(results[p]["platform_annualized_return"])) for p in policies]),
        ("Annualized Volatility", [fmt_pct(float(results[p]["platform_annualized_volatility"])) for p in policies]),
        ("Platform Sharpe Ratio", [fmt_num(float(results[p]["platform_sharpe"])) for p in policies]),
        ("Platform Maximum Drawdown", [fmt_pct(float(results[p]["platform_mdd"])) for p in policies]),
        ("Calmar Ratio", [fmt_num(float(results[p]["calmar_ratio"])) for p in policies]),
        ("Skilled Pod Churn Rate", [fmt_pct1(float(results[p]["skilled_churn_rate"])) for p in policies]),
        ("Unskilled Stopped Rate", [fmt_pct1(float(results[p]["unskilled_stopped_rate"])) for p in policies]),
        ("Overall Pod Survival Rate", [fmt_pct1(float(results[p]["overall_survival_rate"])) for p in policies]),
    ]

    for label, vals in rows:
        print(f"{label:<30} {vals[0]:<15} {vals[1]:<15} {vals[2]:<15}")


main()

Exemplar A · Level 4 (Distinguished)

Context: Excerpt from an executive policy memorandum submitted to the Platform Risk Committee.

1. Theoretical Risk Framework: The platform models individual pod capital dynamics as drifted diffusion processes \(dW_{i,t} = \mu_i dt + \sigma_i dW_{i,t}\). Point-in-time Value at Risk fails to capture pathwise capital depletion. By Skorokhod's reflection representation (Theorem 1.1), the arithmetic drawdown \(D_t = M_t - W_t\) behaves as a continuous diffusion reflected at 0. As established by Magdon-Ismail et al. (2004), the expected maximum drawdown scales logarithmically as \(\frac{\sigma^2}{2\mu} \ln T\) for positive drift strategies, but expands at \(\sqrt{T}\) for zero drift and linearly as \(|\mu| T\) for decaying strategies.

2. The Stop-Loss Paradox and Calibration Trade-off: By Doob's Optional Stopping Theorem (Theorem 3.2), applying a trailing stop-loss to a stationary submartingale with true positive drift \(\mu > 0\) strictly reduces expected terminal wealth and Sharpe ratio by crystallizing losses at the trough. On our 10-pod calibrated universe, an aggressive 6% stop terminates 89.4% of skilled managers (\(\mathrm{SR} = 1.0\)) over 252 days. However, under unobserved Markovian regime switching where strategy drift can deteriorate to \(\mu_{\mathrm{bad}} = -10\%\) (alpha decay), the stop-loss provides essential Bayesian truncation: the likelihood ratio of drawdown exceedance provides conclusive evidence of alpha death. We recommend a calibrated two-tier stop rule: a soft stop at \(h_{\mathrm{soft}} = 5.0\%\) (cutting leverage by 50%) and a hard stop at \(h_{\mathrm{hard}} = 9.0\%\) (complete liquidation). This preserves 68.2% of skilled pods while terminating 96.5% of broken alphas.

3. Policy Benchmark: Over 1,000 platform simulations across 10 pods, Dynamic Drawdown Cushion Allocation (\(w_i \propto \frac{\max(0, h_i - D_{i,t})}{\sigma_i}\)) achieved a platform Sharpe ratio of 0.842 and Calmar ratio of 1.28, outperforming Equal Weight (Sharpe 0.761, Calmar 0.94) and Risk Parity (Sharpe 0.785, Calmar 1.01). Cushion allocation smoothly reduces capital to zero as \(D_{i,t} \to h_i\), mitigating cliff-edge liquidation drag.

Rubric mapping: Meets Level 4 on C1 (cites Magdon-Ismail with locator, specifies Skorokhod reflected diffusion and scaling regimes), C2 (defines two-tier soft/hard stops with explicit 50% leverage reduction), C3 (proves stop-loss paradox via Optional Stopping Theorem, calibrates 5%/9% thresholds against skilled false positives), C4 (documents Calmar and Sharpe outperformance across all three allocation policies), and C5 (addresses alpha decay and liquidation drag).
