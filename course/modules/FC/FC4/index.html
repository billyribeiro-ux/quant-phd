Investments & Equity Security Analysis

Equity valuation bridges industrial organization, financial statement accounting, and asset pricing. This module develops equity security analysis from first principles: the residual-claim structure of equity instruments, industry competitive forces, discounted dividend streams, free cash flow to equity (FCFE), the clean-surplus accounting relation, residual-income valuation (Preinreich-Lücke-Wallis / Ohlson), valuation multiples, scenario-weighted target prices, and the empirical literature on sell-side analyst forecast bias and recommendation efficacy.

  • Prerequisites: AP2 Asset Pricing II, AC2 Financial Statement Analysis II, and CF1 Corporate Finance I. Closure includes M1, M4, E1, AC1.
  • Project: Initiation-of-coverage report and valuation suite (autograder and rubric)

60 h lessons · 30 h project (33.3% of the 90 module hours)

Objectives

By the end of FC4 you can:

  1. Analyze industry competitive structures and barrier dynamics using the five-forces framework and market-share concentration measures (HHI).
  2. Formulate the finite-horizon dividend discount model and derive the Gordon growing perpetuity from the geometric series.
  3. Derive free cash flow to equity (FCFE) from financial statement items and evaluate equity value discounted at the cost of equity.
  4. Prove the clean-surplus accounting relation and demonstrate the mathematical identity between residual income valuation and the dividend discount model.
  5. Apply valuation multiples (P/E and EV/EBITDA) and reconcile enterprise value to equity value through the net debt bridge.
  6. Construct a scenario-weighted equity price target across bull, base, and bear outcomes with exact half-up rounding in integer cents.
  7. Evaluate empirical evidence on sell-side analyst forecast bias, career concerns, and the investment value of recommendation revisions.

Prerequisites

FC4 builds directly on AP2 (Asset Pricing II), AC2 (Financial Statement Analysis II), and CF1 (Time Value & Capital Budgeting), alongside foundational concepts from M1, M4, and E1. The following prerequisite concepts are used without redefinition:

  • From AP2: capital asset pricing model (CAPM), market beta, and equity risk premium.
  • From CF1: present value, discount factor, net present value, and net working capital.
  • From M1: growing perpetuity, annuity, and geometric series.
  • From M4: expectation, variance, and normal distribution.
  • From AC1: cash flow statement, depreciation and amortization, and accrual accounting.
  • From E1: Cournot competition and Bertrand competition.

Placement diagnostic

Ten questions: eight on prerequisite foundations (AP2, CF1, AC1, M1, M4) and two skill checks on core valuation routines. The mastery bar is 80 percent (at least eight correct). Passing the diagnostic and answering both skill checks permits skipping rungs 1 and 2 of the project.

  1. Under the CAPM, if the risk-free rate is 4 percent, beta is 1.2, and the market risk premium is 5 percent, what is the expected return of the equity in percent?

    \(r_e = r_f + \beta \times \text{ERP} = 4\% + 1.2 \times 5\% = 10\%\) (AP2, CAPM).

  2. A perpetual cash flow starts at 1 dollar at date 1 and grows at 5 percent per year forever. If the discount rate is 10 percent, what is its present value today in dollars?

    \(PV = C_1 / (r - g) = 1.00 / (0.10 - 0.05) = 20.00\) dollars (M1, growing perpetuity).

  3. Which financial statement line represents non-cash charges added back to net income in operating cash flow?

    Options

    Depreciation and amortization are non-cash accrual expenses deducted from net income that are added back on the cash flow statement (AC1, cash flow statement).

  4. If net income is 100 dollars, depreciation is 30, capex is 40, change in net working capital is 10, and net new borrowing is 70, what is free cash flow to equity (FCFE) in dollars?

    \(\text{FCFE} = 100 + 30 - 40 - 10 + 70 = 150\) dollars (AC1/CF1, cash flows).

  5. What happens to the clean-surplus relation \(B_t = B_{t-1} + NI_t - D_t\) if a firm pays dividends exceeding its net income?

    Options

    When \(D_t > NI_t\), retained earnings are negative, so \(B_t - B_{t-1} = NI_t - D_t < 0\), reducing book equity.

  6. In an industry with 3 firms holding market shares of 40%, 30%, and 30%, what is the Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI) in conventional integer points (0–10,000)?

    \(\text{HHI} = 40^2 + 30^2 + 30^2 = 1600 + 900 + 900 = 3400\) points.

  7. In residual income valuation, what is the charge subtracted from net income to obtain residual income \(RI_t\)?

    Options

    Residual income is defined as economic profit: \(RI_t = NI_t - r B_{t-1}\).

  8. A company has EBITDA of 50 million dollars, total debt of 200 million, cash of 50 million, and 10 million shares. If comparable firms trade at 8.0x EV/EBITDA, what is the implied equity price per share in dollars?

    \(\text{EV} = 50 \times 8 = 400\)M. \(\text{Net Debt} = 200 - 50 = 150\)M. \(\text{Equity Value} = 400 - 150 = 250\)M. Per share: \(250 / 10 = 25\) dollars.

  9. Skill Check 1: In a multistage DDM with discount rate 9 percent and terminal growth 4 percent, if year 3 dividend is 1.923 dollars, what is the capitalized terminal value at date 3 (\(TV_3 = D_4 / (r - g)\)) in dollars?

    \(D_4 = D_3 \times (1 + g) = 1.923077 \times 1.04 = 2.00\) dollars. \(TV_3 = 2.00 / (0.09 - 0.04) = 40.00\) dollars.

  10. Skill Check 2: A scenario model assigns 50% probability to 3524 cents and 50% probability to 3525 cents. Following half-up rounding (\(\lfloor E[V] + 1/2 \rfloor\)), what is the price target in whole cents?

    \(E[V] = 3524.5\) cents. Under half-up rounding, \(\lfloor 3524.5 + 0.5 \rfloor = 3525\) cents. (Notice Python round() would produce 3524).

Project ladder (30 study hours)

The capstone deliverable for FC4 is an institutional initiation-of-coverage research report and accompanying autograded valuation suite. Details and submissions are on the project page.

  1. Rung 1 · Worked example

    Study a fully annotated multi-model valuation on a SIMULATED firm with Gordon, DDM, FCFE, residual income, and scenario targets.

  2. Rung 2 · Completion problem

    Complete missing cash flow capitalization and rounding formulas, validated against instant test outputs.

  3. Rung 3 · Independent project

    Build valuation.py (autograded on 50 seeds) and write initiation.md (scored against a 6-criterion rubric).

  4. Rung 4 · Stretch

    Construct two-dimensional sensitivity surfaces and analyze historical consensus earnings surprise dynamics.
