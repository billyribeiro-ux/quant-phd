Inflation-Linked Markets

Mechanics of inflation-indexed sovereign debt, breakeven inflation dynamics, the Fisher relation, structural decomposition into expectations, inflation risk premia, and TIPS liquidity premia, and the limits to arbitrage behind the TIPS-Treasury puzzle.

Prerequisites: FI3
Unlocks: FI6, STUDIO-FI
Project: Inflation-expectations decomposition (both autograder and rubric)

28 h lessons across 4 lessons @ 7 h · 12 h project (30.0% of the 40 module hours)

Objectives

By the end of FI5 you can:

  1. Formulate Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities (TIPS) indexation mechanics, interpolating reference CPI under the 2.5-month statutory lag, calculating semiannual adjusted coupons, and pricing the embedded deflation put floor.
  2. Construct zero-coupon breakeven inflation curves from nominal and real yield term structures, quantifying the geometric Fisher compounding cross-product wedge and pricing zero-coupon inflation swaps (ZCIS).
  3. Attribute horizon carry on breakeven inflation positions across yield carry and inflation accrual, incorporating seasonal CPI adjustments.
  4. Decompose breakeven inflation into survey-anchored expected inflation, the inflation risk premium (IRP), and the TIPS liquidity premium (TLP) using affine term-structure models.
  5. Evaluate the economic determinants of positive versus negative inflation risk premia, analyzing nominal bonds as procyclical inflation bets versus deflation hedges.
  6. Replicate synthetic nominal Treasuries by packaging TIPS with zero-coupon inflation swaps and compute the Fleckenstein, Longstaff, and Lustig (2014) mispricing puzzle basis.
  7. Assess institutional limits to arbitrage, evaluating repo haircut financing, collateral specialness, and dealer balance-sheet constraints under the Supplementary Leverage Ratio (SLR).

Prerequisites

FI5 assumes FI3 (Bond Risk Premia & the Expectations Hypothesis) and its prerequisite closure {FI1, S3, S2, S1, S0, M4, M3, M2, M1, CS1, CF1, AC1}. It uses these concepts without redefining them:

  • From FI1: spot rate, forward rate, duration, convexity, DV01, and yield to maturity.
  • From FI3: expectations hypothesis, term premium, carry and roll-down, and model-based term premium.
  • From CF1: Fisher relation and real vs nominal cash flows.

Placement diagnostic

Ten questions: eight on prerequisite concepts from FI1, FI3, and CF1, and two skill checks on what rungs 1 and 2 of the project ladder teach. The mastery threshold is 0.80 (8 out of 10 items correct). Skip rungs 1 and 2 if you meet the threshold and correctly answer both skill checks.

  1. Under the classical linear Fisher relation, how is the nominal interest rate \(i\) related to the real interest rate \(r\) and expected inflation \(\pi^e\)?

    Options

    The linear Fisher relation decomposes nominal rates into the real rate plus expected inflation (CF1, Fisher relation).

  2. If the 10-year nominal Treasury yield is 4.15% and the 10-year TIPS real yield is 1.90%, what is the linear 10-year breakeven inflation rate in percent?

    \(4.15\% - 1.90\% = 2.25\%\) or 225 basis points (FI1, yield spread).

  3. What does the term premium on a long-term Treasury bond represent under modern asset pricing theory?

    Options

    The term premium is the compensation demanded by risk-averse investors for bearing interest rate volatility over the bond's holding period (FI3, term premium).

  4. For a zero-coupon bond with maturity 5 years and yield 5.0% compounded annually, what is its modified duration in years (\(D_{\text{mod}} = \tau / (1 + y)\))?

    \(D_{\text{mod}} = 5 / 1.05 \approx 4.76\) years (FI1, duration).

  5. Why does Jensen's inequality introduce a wedge between bond prices and bond yields in expectations models?

    Options

    Convexity of the pricing function ensures that expected price exceeds the price at expected yield (FI1, convexity).

  6. What does carry and roll-down measure for a bond portfolio held over an evaluation horizon assuming the yield curve remains unchanged?

    Options

    Carry and roll-down quantify total expected return under a static term structure (FI3, carry and roll-down).

  7. When real cash flows are discounted to find real present value, what discount rate must be applied?

    Options

    The fundamental valuation consistency rule requires real cash flows to be discounted at real rates (CF1, real vs nominal cash flows).

  8. If the 1-year nominal rate is 5.0% and the 1-year real rate is 2.0%, what is the exact Fisher cross-product wedge \((r \cdot \pi)\) in basis points under exact compounding, assuming geometric breakeven is 2.941%?

    The compounding wedge is \(r \cdot \pi = 0.02 \times 0.02941 = 0.000588\) or approximately 5.9 basis points.

  9. Skill check (Rung 1): In US Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities (TIPS), what happens to the final principal redemption payment at maturity if cumulative inflation over the bond's life is negative (severe deflation, index ratio < 1.0)?

    Options

    US TIPS indentures embed a non-detachable deflation floor guaranteeing redemption at not less than original face value.

  10. Skill check (Rung 2): In the tripartite decomposition of breakeven inflation \(\text{BEI} = \mathbb{E}[\pi] + \text{IRP} - \text{TLP}\), why does the TIPS liquidity premium enter with a negative sign?

    Options

    An illiquidity penalty increases TIPS yield \(y_R\). Since \(\text{BEI} = y_N - y_R\), a higher \(y_R\) mechanically reduces breakeven inflation.

Project ladder

The module capstone is Inflation-Expectations Decomposition (12 study hours), delivering an automated decomposition library in decomposition.py and an institutional research memo.

  1. Rung 1 (Worked example): Study and execute the end-to-end decomposition pipeline in Lab 1.
  2. Rung 2 (Completion problem): Implement deflation floor protection and the tripartite inflation risk premium formula in Lab 2.
  3. Rung 3 (Independent project): Code decomposition.py to satisfy the 50-seed autograder suite and author the institutional research memo.
  4. Rung 4 (Stretch): Model multi-factor affine term structure models with Blue Chip survey anchors or dynamic repo haircut spirals.
