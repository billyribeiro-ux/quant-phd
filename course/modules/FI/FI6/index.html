Interest-Rate Derivatives

The quantitative architecture of modern fixed income markets: multi-curve collateralized discounting, the benchmark transition from LIBOR to backward-looking compounded SOFR, Black-76 lognormal caplet stripping, European swaption valuation under the swap annuity measure, negative interest rates and the Bachelier normal model, the Hagan et al. (2002) SABR stochastic volatility framework, and full 3D swaption volatility cube construction.

  • Prerequisites: FI2 Term-Structure Models, DV1 Option Pricing & Dynamic Hedging
  • Concepts introduced: multi-curve-discounting, libor-sofr-transition, interest-rate-cap-floor, black-76-model, swaption, bachelier-model, sabr-model, swaption-cube
  • Project: Swaption-Cube Engine: Python pricing and risk engine (swaption_cube.py) calibrating SABR slices, interpolating surfaces, and enforcing Breeden-Litzenberger arbitrage bounds.

72 h lessons · 28 h project (28.0%)

Objectives

By the end of FI6 you can:

  1. Derive the dual-curve bootstrap methodology and price linear interest rate swaps and forward rate agreements under OIS/SOFR collateralized discounting.
  2. Formulate the backward-looking compounded SOFR in arrears payment mechanics and calculate the official ISDA 2020 5-year historical median spread adjustments.
  3. Strip forward caplet volatilities from quoted flat cap surfaces under the Black-76 lognormal model and prove exact cap-floor-swap put-call parity.
  4. Price European payer and receiver swaptions using the swap annuity numeraire under both Black-76 and the Bachelier normal model across negative rate regimes.
  5. Calibrate the Hagan et al. (2002) SABR stochastic volatility model \((\alpha, \rho, \nu)\) to empirical market smiles and resolve the dynamic smile paradox of local volatility.
  6. Construct an arbitrage-free 3D swaption volatility cube across expiry, tenor, and strike, verifying the Breeden-Litzenberger non-negative risk-neutral density condition.

Prerequisites

FI6 builds directly on the term-structure foundations of FI2 and the continuous-time option pricing framework of DV1, along with the foundational prerequisites from FI1 and FC2. Key concepts assumed established without redefinition include:

  • From FI2: short-rate models, Vasicek model, Cox–Ingersoll–Ross model, and Heath–Jarrow–Morton framework.
  • From DV1: Black–Scholes–Merton PDE, dynamic replication, delta hedging, and option Greeks.
  • From FI1: forward rate, spot rate, curve bootstrapping, and par yield.
  • From FC2: interest-rate swap, put–call parity, and arbitrage opportunity.

Placement diagnostic

Eight diagnostic questions covering prerequisite foundations and two implementation skill checks from project rungs 1 and 2. Mastery bar is 80% (at least 7 of 8 correct, including both skill checks).

  1. Skill check (rung 1): In a Bachelier normal model with forward swap rate \(S = 0.0400\), strike \(K = 0.0400\) (ATM), annuity \(A = 4.520\), expiry \(T = 1.0\), and normal volatility \(\sigma_N = 0.0080\) (80 bps/year), what is the analytical price of a European payer swaption, given by \(A \sigma_N \sqrt{T} / \sqrt{2\pi}\)?

    \(P = 4.520 \times 0.0080 \times 1.0 \times \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} = 4.520 \times 0.0080 \times 0.398942 = 0.014426\).

  2. Skill check (rung 2): What is the exact official ISDA 2020 Fallback Protocol spread adjustment in basis points for 3-month USD LIBOR against compounded SOFR?

    The benchmark 5-year historical median spread fixed by Bloomberg/ISDA on March 5, 2021 is exactly 26.161 basis points (0.26161%).

  3. From FC2: In a standard single-currency fixed-for-floating interest rate swap, when is the floating reference rate determined relative to payment?

    Standard floating rate loans and legacy swaps reset in advance (at \(T_{k-1}\)) and pay in arrears (at \(T_k\)).

  4. From FI1: If the 1-year zero-coupon bond price is \(P(0, 1) = 0.9600\) and the 2-year price is \(P(0, 2) = 0.9150\), what is the simple 1-year forward rate \(F(0; 1, 2)\)?

    \(F(0; 1, 2) = \frac{P(0, 1)}{P(0, 2)} - 1 = \frac{0.9600}{0.9150} - 1 = 1.04918 - 1 = 4.918\%\).

  5. From DV1: What numeraire asset makes the discounted price of any tradable asset a martingale under the risk-neutral measure \(\mathbb{Q}\)?

    By definition of the risk-neutral measure \(\mathbb{Q}\), the money market account \(B(t)\) serves as the numeraire.

  6. From FI2: Which short-rate model guarantees strictly positive interest rates by incorporating a square-root diffusion term \(\sigma \sqrt{r_t} dW_t\)?

    The CIR model features state-dependent diffusion \(\sigma \sqrt{r_t}\), ensuring \(r_t \ge 0\) when the Feller condition \(2\kappa \theta \ge \sigma^2\) is met.

  7. From FI1: If a 2-year annual coupon swap has discount factors \(P(0, 1) = 0.9615\) and \(P(0, 2) = 0.9231\), what is the par swap rate \(S_2 = \frac{1 - P(0, 2)}{P(0, 1) + P(0, 2)}\)?

    \(S_2 = \frac{1 - 0.9231}{0.9615 + 0.9231} = \frac{0.0769}{1.8846} = 0.04080\) (approx 4.08%).

  8. From DV1: What option Greek measures the second derivative of the option price with respect to the underlying asset price (\(\frac{\partial^2 V}{\partial S^2}\))?

    Gamma represents the rate of change of Delta with respect to the underlying asset price, measuring convexity.

Lessons

The module is structured into six 12-hour doctoral lessons totaling 72 study hours:

  1. L01 · Multi-Curve Framework & OIS Discounting 12 h

    Collateralized CSA discounting, the collapse of single-curve pricing post-2008, dual-curve bootstrapping, forward rate projection curves, tenor basis spreads, and linear swap valuation.

  2. L02 · The Reference Rate Revolution: LIBOR-to-SOFR Transition 12 h

    Structural failure of unsecured bank submissions, Secured Overnight Financing Rate mechanics, compounding in arrears, observation shift versus payment delay, and the ISDA 2020 5-year median fallback protocol.

  3. L03 · Caps, Floors, and the Black-76 Model 12 h

    Anatomy of caplets and floorlets, payment in arrears mechanics, derivation of Black-76 under the forward measure, cap-floor-swap put-call parity, and forward volatility stripping bootstrap.

  4. L04 · European Swaptions & the Bachelier Normal Model 12 h

    Payer and receiver swaptions, the swap annuity numeraire, breakdown of Black-76 under negative interest rates, the Bachelier (1900) normal model, and normal versus lognormal volatility dynamics.

  5. L05 · The SABR Stochastic Volatility Model 12 h

    The dynamic smile paradox of local volatility, the Hagan et al. (2002) SABR model SDEs, parameter interpretation (\(\alpha, \beta, \rho, \nu\)), singular perturbation asymptotic expansion, and empirical calibration.

  6. L06 · The Swaption Volatility Cube & Surface Engineering 12 h

    The 3D coordinate geometry (expiry \(\times\) tenor \(\times\) strike), slice-by-slice SABR calibration, 2D parameter interpolation, Breeden-Litzenberger butterfly arbitrage verification, and multi-curve risk management.

Project: Swaption-Cube Engine (28 hours)

The capstone project builds a production-grade 3D swaption volatility cube pricing and risk engine in Python.

  1. Rung 1 · Worked Example

    Calibrate SABR parameters to a single market smile slice (1Y into 5Y), evaluate Black and Bachelier pricing formulas, and verify put-call parity.

  2. Rung 2 · Completion Problem

    Complete the missing expressions for the SABR \(z\) variable and the second-derivative Breeden-Litzenberger risk-neutral density to detect strike arbitrage.

  3. Rung 3 · Independent Project

    Implement the complete SwaptionCubeEngine: multi-curve discounting, grid calibration, 2D surface parameter interpolation, no-arbitrage enforcement, and off-grid swaption pricing with analytic DV01 and Vega.

  4. Rung 4 · Stretch Extension

    Incorporate shifted SABR parameterizations to support negative interest rates and analyze dynamic delta-vega hedging performance.
