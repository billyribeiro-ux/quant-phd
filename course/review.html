Recall the answer before you reveal it, then rate how hard it was. The scheduler brings each card back just before you are likely to forget it, so material from your first module stays available in your last.

How the schedule works

Cards enter the deck when you finish a lesson's retrieval quiz. Each rating updates the card's memory state, and the next review is scheduled for when recall probability falls to 90%. "Again" brings a card back within the session. The scheduler is the FSRS-4.5 algorithm with its published default parameters. It implements the model family of .
