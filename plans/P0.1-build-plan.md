## Project ladder and grading (rubric)

Grading is **rubric**, per `01-foundations.yaml:38`. There is no grader, no `grader/` directory and no `instance.py`.
`check_projects.py` checks only the rubric and exemplar rules (`check_projects.py:58-72`).

Project: **Paper teardown**. Brief (`01-foundations.yaml:33-36`): one assigned classic paper, a one-page teardown:
question, mechanism, data, identification, main result with its locator, weakest assumption, evidence grade.

- **Rung 1 · Worked example.** A fully annotated teardown of one classic paper, with a "why" note at every step.
  Paper: OPEN QUESTION (see below). Skippable on a placement pass.
- **Rung 2 · Completion problem.** The same template with the identification and weakest-assumption fields blanked,
  graded instantly by a checklist (mark locator present, grade present). Skippable on a placement pass.
- **Rung 3 · Independent project.** The assigned paper, teardown to the spec above. Rubric-graded. Never skipped.
- **Rung 4 · Stretch.** Open-ended: grade-audit a result from the student's field against the registry, with a
  written verdict on its grade. Optional.

**Rubric (proposed, 6 criteria, four observable levels each; `check_projects.py` requires 4–7):**
1. Question and mechanism stated in one sentence each, checkable against the paper's introduction.
2. Identification: names the variation that pins down the estimate and says why it is credible.
3. Data: names the sample, period and market. Each quoted number carries a table, panel or page locator.
4. Main result: states the result with its exact locator. Generalizes no further than the paper's sample.
5. Weakest assumption: names it and says which robustness check, if any, addresses it.
6. Evidence grade: assigns T/A/B/C/D/X per BLUEPRINT §3, justifies it, and for B or C names one published counter-source if one exists.

Exemplars: write level 4 and level 2 (two levels minimum), each fully written, each annotated against the criteria.
Self-assessment form: the student scores themselves; the page shows divergence from the exemplar scoring.
Question bank: NOT required. BUILDER_RULES §5.4.4 applies to adversarial projects (IC defense, debate, referee
report); a teardown is not one. Confirm with the architect.

Estimate per component (Option B): worked example 0.5 h; completion 0.5 h; teardown 2.5 h; self-assessment and
exemplar calibration 0.5 h; total 4.0 h (BUILDER_RULES §4.3 requires each component's rationale in one line).
