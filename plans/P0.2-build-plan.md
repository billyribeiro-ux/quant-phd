## 0. BLOCKING: the curriculum hours cannot satisfy the module gates

The curriculum gives P0.2 **4 h** (`curriculum/01-foundations.yaml:42`; `curriculum.json:6259`). The gates require:

- each lesson declares **6-12 study hours** (`tools/check_pages.py:227-228`: `if declared and not 6 <= declared <= 12`);
- the project is **10-40%** of module hours (`tools/check_pages.py:339-340`), and lessons plus project equal the curriculum figure (`check_pages.py:337-338`).

At 4 h, one lesson alone (≥6 h) exceeds the module. No lesson split or project share can pass. The same defect applies to
P0.1 (6 h, `01-foundations.yaml:9`): one 6 h lesson plus a ≥0.6 h project is 6.6 h, which is more than 6 h.

Hours are an architect decision (BLUEPRINT §2: "design targets"; the generated tables update automatically). Options:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Keep curriculum | 4 | — | — | **No** | No lesson fits; `check_pages --unit P0.2` cannot pass |
| B. **Recommended** | **16** | 2 × 6 h = 12 | 4 h (25%) | Yes | Smallest comfortable split; project room for rung 3 |
| C. Minimum two-lesson | 14 | 2 × 6 h = 12 | 2 h (14%) | Yes, tight | Project at the 10% floor |

Consequence of B: F-tier total moves 1420 → 1432 h and program total 13715 → 13727 h (`curriculum.json` `stats`, starting at line 5726). BLUEPRINT §2 figures (4,345 / 11,155 / 12,515 / 13,715) and README quote them, so
`tests/test_docs_figures.py` fails until `render_curriculum.py` is re-run and the quoted figures are updated. P0.2 is in no
credential closure (`curriculum.json` credentials closures list P0.1 but not P0.2), so credential hours do not change.

**The builder does not change `curriculum/*.yaml`.** The plan below assumes option B. If the architect picks another
option, only the hub hours and the time-budget table change.

## 5. Project ladder and rubric (Builder A; project is `grading: rubric`, `01-foundations.yaml:68`)

Project title (curriculum): **Personal study plan**. Brief (curriculum): "From your placement results and the track hour
totals, build a dated study plan covering weekly hours, track choice, review cadence and milestones." Deliverable
(curriculum): "Exported plan JSON plus a half-page rationale."

Ladder (`project.html`, `ol.ladder` with four `rung-title` items in this order):
1. **Worked example:** one fully solved plan for a generated learner profile (SIMULATED), annotated at each step. Lab
   `lab-P0.2-project-1`.
2. **Completion problem:** the same plan with the week-by-week hour sums and the review-date step removed; `section.lab.completion`
   with `___` blanks (`check_projects.py` requires a completion lab or quiz for rung 2). Lab `lab-P0.2-project-2`; the twin is
   the completed solution.
3. **Independent project:** the student's own plan from their own placement results and `curriculum.json` track totals. The
   spec gives the JSON schema (`plan.schema` fields: weeks, weekly_hours, track, review_cadence, milestones) and the rationale
   length (half a page). Track totals are read from `curriculum.json` `tracks`, never typed in (BLUEPRINT §2; CLAUDE.md hard rule 5).
4. **Stretch:** open-ended: a re-plan after a simulated placement miss, with the mastery threshold justified. No ceiling.

Spec `dl.spec` must have dt entries for: context, inputs, required outputs, constraints, grading. The grading entry must say
"rubric" (check_projects `grading_problems`).

Rubric (`table.rubric`): 5 criteria (4-7 required), each with four observable levels (not "good", "adequate"). Proposed criteria:
1. Dated schedule: every week's hours sum to the stated weekly total, and the weeks cover the track's module hours.
2. Track choice: the track's total hours and full-time years are cited from the `tracks` field with the value copied from the
   generated output.
3. Review cadence: review dates follow the stated spacing rule and say which concept (spaced retrieval, interleaving) each rule uses.
4. Use of placement results: each module the student tests out of is named with its placement score and the mastery bar it cleared
   (`data-mastery` 0.8 on the hub).
5. Evidence and grades: every empirical rationale line cites a registry key with its grade.

Exemplars: `article.exemplar data-level="4"` and `data-level="2"` at minimum, each annotated with the criterion scores in
`data-scores`. Self-assessment `section.rubric-self` per `course/assets/js/rubric.js` header (lines 1-8).
Question bank: not required (not an adversarial project).

Reading list `ol.reading-list`, `li data-cite=<key>` for each key used in the project (section 5 keys only).
