## 1. Dynamic Factor Model & Ragged-Edge Kalman Nowcaster

Given an unbalanced macroeconomic panel $X \in \mathbb{R}^{T \times N}$ where missing observations due to asynchronous release schedules are represented by `np.nan`:

### 1.1 Balanced Factor Extraction (Stock & Watson 2002)
For a balanced historical subset $X_{\text{bal}} \in \mathbb{R}^{T_{\text{bal}} \times N}$:
1. Standardize each column: $\tilde{X}_{t,i} = \frac{X_{t,i} - \mu_i}{\sigma_i}$.
2. Compute the sample covariance matrix $\Sigma = \frac{1}{T_{\text{bal}}} \tilde{X}_{\text{bal}}' \tilde{X}_{\text{bal}}$.
3. Extract $r$ principal eigenvectors $V \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times r}$ with eigenvalues $D = \operatorname{diag}(d_1, \dots, d_r)$.
4. Factor loadings: $\Lambda = V \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times r}$.
5. Static factors: $\hat{F} = \tilde{X}_{\text{bal}} V \in \mathbb{R}^{T_{\text{bal}} \times r}$, normalized such that $\frac{1}{T_{\text{bal}}} \hat{F}' \hat{F} = D$.
6. Estimate VAR(1) transition matrix:
   $$F_t = A F_{t-1} + u_t, \quad u_t \sim \mathcal{N}(0, Q)$$
   $$A = \left(\sum_{t=2}^{T_{\text{bal}}} F_t F_{t-1}'\right) \left(\sum_{t=2}^{T_{\text{bal}}} F_{t-1} F_{t-1}'\right)^{-1}$$
   $$Q = \frac{1}{T_{\text{bal}} - 1} \sum_{t=2}^{T_{\text{bal}}} (F_t - A F_{t-1}) (F_t - A F_{t-1})'$$
7. Idiosyncratic measurement noise:
   $$R = \operatorname{diag}\left(\operatorname{Var}(\tilde{X}_{\text{bal}} - \hat{F} \Lambda')\right)$$

### 1.2 Ragged-Edge Filtering (Giannone, Reichlin & Small 2008)
For any vintage containing missing entries ($X_{t,i} = \text{NaN}$):
Let $W_t \in \mathbb{R}^{N_t \times N}$ be the row-selection matrix selecting the $N_t$ observed series at period $t$:
$$X_t^* = W_t \tilde{X}_t, \quad \Lambda_t^* = W_t \Lambda, \quad R_t^* = W_t R W_t'$$

- **Prediction:**
  $$\hat{F}_{t|t-1} = A \hat{F}_{t-1|t-1}$$
  $$P_{t|t-1} = A P_{t-1|t-1} A' + Q$$

- **Update:**
  - If $N_t = 0$:
    $$\hat{F}_{t|t} = \hat{F}_{t|t-1}, \quad P_{t|t} = P_{t|t-1}$$
  - If $N_t > 0$:
    $$v_t = X_t^* - \Lambda_t^* \hat{F}_{t|t-1}$$
    $$S_t = \Lambda_t^* P_{t|t-1} (\Lambda_t^*)' + R_t^*$$
    $$K_t = P_{t|t-1} (\Lambda_t^*)' S_t^{-1}$$
    $$\hat{F}_{t|t} = \hat{F}_{t|t-1} + K_t v_t$$
    $$P_{t|t} = (I - K_t \Lambda_t^*) P_{t|t-1}$$

## 2. News Decomposition

When transitioning from vintage $v_1$ to vintage $v_2$, new macroeconomic data is published and previously published observations are revised. The nowcast revision is:
$$\hat{y}_{T|v_2} - \hat{y}_{T|v_1} = \sum_{i=1}^N \omega_i \cdot \nu_{t_i, i}$$
where $\nu_{t_i, i} = X_{t_i, i}^{(v_2)} - \mathbb{E}[X_{t_i, i} | \Omega_{v_1}]$ is the news/innovation in series $i$, and $\omega_i$ is the model weight mapping innovation to target revision.

## 3. Economic Surprise Index

Given macroeconomic releases $A \in \mathbb{R}^{T \times N}$, market consensus expectations $E \in \mathbb{R}^{T \times N}$, and historical standard deviations $\sigma \in \mathbb{R}^N$:

1. Standardized Surprise:
   $$S_{t, i} = \frac{A_{t, i} - E_{t, i}}{\sigma_i}$$
2. Weighted Exponentially Decaying Surprise Index:
   $$ESI_t = \sum_{k=0}^{\min(t, M)} \lambda^k \sum_{i=1}^N w_i S_{t-k, i}$$
   where $\lambda = \exp(-\ln(2) / H)$ with half-life $H > 0$, weights $\sum w_i = 1$, and normalized to sample mean 0 and unit variance.

- **Quadrant 1 (Goldilocks / Expansion):** Growth $\ge \theta_g$, Inflation $< \theta_\pi$

- **Quadrant 3 (Stagflation):** Growth $< \theta_g$, Inflation $\ge \theta_\pi$
