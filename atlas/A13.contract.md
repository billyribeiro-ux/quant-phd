## 1. Units and Conventions

| Quantity | Unit and definition |
|---|---|
| Yields \(y(t, \tau)\) | `float` or `np.ndarray`, decimal per annum (e.g., `0.05` represents 5.0% per annum), continuously compounded zero-coupon rate. |
| Maturities \(\tau\) | `float` or `np.ndarray`, in years (\(\tau > 0\)). |
| Prices \(P(t, \tau)\) | Normalized zero-coupon bond price per unit face value: \(P(t, \tau) = \exp(-\tau y(t, \tau))\). |
| Log prices \(p(t, \tau)\) | \(p(t, \tau) = \ln P(t, \tau) = -\tau y(t, \tau)\). |
| Forward rates \(f(t, \tau_1, \tau_2)\) | Continuously compounded forward rate between \(\tau_1\) and \(\tau_2\): \(f(t, \tau_1, \tau_2) = \frac{\tau_2 y(t, \tau_2) - \tau_1 y(t, \tau_1)}{\tau_2 - \tau_1}\). |
| Excess bond returns \(rx_{t+1}^{(\tau)}\) | 1-year holding period excess log return: \(rx_{t+1}^{(\tau)} = p(t+1, \tau-1) - p(t, \tau) - y(t, 1)\). |
| Horizon \(dt\) | `float`, in years (\(dt > 0\)). |
| Weights \(w\) | `np.ndarray`, portfolio capital weights. |

## 2. Functions Specification

### 2.1 `fama_bliss_forward_rates(yields: np.ndarray, maturities: np.ndarray) -> np.ndarray`

Computes instantaneous or discrete 1-year forward rates from zero-coupon yields.

- **Inputs:**
  - `yields`: `np.ndarray` of shape `(T, K)` or `(K,)`, continuously compounded yields.
  - `maturities`: `np.ndarray` of shape `(K,)`, sorted strictly increasing positive maturities in years.
- **Formula:**
  For maturity index \(k = 0\), \(f_t(0, \tau_0) = y_t(\tau_0)\).  
  For \(k \ge 1\), \(f_t(\tau_{k-1}, \tau_k) = \frac{\tau_k y_t(\tau_k) - \tau_{k-1} y_t(\tau_{k-1})}{\tau_k - \tau_{k-1}}\).
- **Errors:**
  - `TypeError` if inputs are not numpy arrays or compatible numeric sequences.
  - `ValueError` if `maturities` is not 1D, has length \(< 2\), is not strictly increasing, or contains \(\le 0\).
  - `ValueError` if `yields.shape[-1] != len(maturities)`.

### 2.2 `excess_bond_returns(yields: np.ndarray, maturities: np.ndarray) -> np.ndarray`

Computes 1-year holding period excess log returns for bonds of annual maturities \(\tau \in \{2, \dots, K\}\).

- **Inputs:**
  - `yields`: 2D `np.ndarray` of shape `(T, K)` observed at annual frequency (\(T \ge 2\), \(K \ge 2\)).
  - `maturities`: 1D `np.ndarray` of shape `(K,)` containing integer maturities `[1, 2, ..., K]`.
- **Formula:**
  \[
  rx_{t+1}^{(n)} = -(n-1) y_{t+1}^{(n-1)} + n y_t^{(n)} - y_t^{(1)}
  \]
  for \(n = 2, \dots, K\) and \(t = 0, \dots, T-2\).
- **Outputs:**
  - `np.ndarray` of shape `(T-1, K-1)`.
- **Errors:**
  - `ValueError` if `yields.ndim != 2` or \(T < 2\) or \(K < 2\).
  - `ValueError` if `maturities` does not match integer sequence `1, 2, ..., K`.

### 2.3 `cochrane_piazzesi_factor(yields: np.ndarray, maturities: np.ndarray) -> dict[str, Any]`

Estimates the single-factor Cochrane–Piazzesi (2005) return predictability model.

- **Inputs:**
  - `yields`: 2D `np.ndarray` of shape `(T, K)` with \(K \ge 5\) and \(T > K + 1\).
  - `maturities`: 1D `np.ndarray` of shape `(K,)` starting with maturities 1 through 5.
- **Method:**
  1. Compute 1-year forward rates \(f_t = [y_t^{(1)}, f_t^{(1,2)}, f_t^{(2,3)}, f_t^{(3,4)}, f_t^{(4,5)}]\) for \(t = 0, \dots, T-2\).
  2. Compute average excess return \(\overline{rx}_{t+1} = \frac{1}{4} \sum_{n=2}^5 rx_{t+1}^{(n)}\).
  3. Run OLS regression: \(\overline{rx}_{t+1} = \gamma_0 + \sum_{i=1}^5 \gamma_i f_{t,i} + \bar{\varepsilon}_{t+1}\).
  4. Form the single factor: \(\text{cp}_t = \gamma_0 + \gamma^\top f_t\).
  5. Run individual regressions: \(rx_{t+1}^{(n)} = b_n \cdot \text{cp}_t + e_{t+1}^{(n)}\) for \(n = 2, 3, 4, 5\).
- **Outputs:**
  `dict` with:
  - `"gamma"`: `np.ndarray` of shape `(6,)` containing \([\gamma_0, \gamma_1, \dots, \gamma_5]\).
  - `"cp_factor"`: `np.ndarray` of shape `(T-1,)` containing fitted single factor values.
  - `"r2"`: `float`, coefficient of determination of the average excess return regression.
  - `"loadings"`: `np.ndarray` of shape `(4,)` containing \([b_2, b_3, b_4, b_5]\).

### 2.4 `acm_term_premium(yields: np.ndarray, maturities: np.ndarray, num_factors: int = 3) -> dict[str, Any]`

Implements the Adrian, Crump & Moench (2013) 3-step linear regression affine term-structure model.

- **Inputs:**
  - `yields`: 2D `np.ndarray` of shape `(T, K)` zero-coupon yields.
  - `maturities`: 1D `np.ndarray` of shape `(K,)`.
  - `num_factors`: `int`, number of pricing factors \(K_f \ge 1\) (default 3).
- **Method:**
  1. Extract \(K_f\) principal components from `yields` as factors \(X_t\).
  2. Estimate VAR(1) under \(\mathbb{P}\): \(X_{t+1} = \mu + \Phi X_t + v_{t+1}\), estimating \(\mu\), \(\Phi\), and \(\Sigma_v = \widehat{\text{Cov}}(v)\).
  3. Regress excess returns \(rx_{t+1}^{(n)} = \alpha_n + \beta_n^\top v_{t+1} + c_n^\top X_t + e_{t+1}^{(n)}\).
  4. Estimate market prices of risk \(\lambda_0, \lambda_1\) via cross-sectional regression:
     \(\lambda_1 = (\beta^\top \beta)^{-1} \beta^\top C\).
  5. Compute expected short rates under \(\mathbb{P}\):
     \(r_t = \delta_0 + \delta_1^\top X_t\),
     \(\mathbb{E}_t[r_{t+i}] = \delta_0 + \delta_1^\top \left[ (I - \Phi^i)(I - \Phi)^{-1} \mu + \Phi^i X_t \right]\),
     \(\bar{r}_t^{(n)} = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} \mathbb{E}_t[r_{t+i}]\).
  6. Compute term premium: \(TP_t^{(n)} = y_t^{(n)} - \bar{r}_t^{(n)}\).
- **Outputs:**
  `dict` with:
  - `"term_premium"`: `np.ndarray` of shape `(T, K)`.
  - `"expected_short_rate"`: `np.ndarray` of shape `(T, K)`.
  - `"fitted_yields"`: `np.ndarray` of shape `(T, K)`.
  - `"phi"`: `np.ndarray` of shape `(num_factors, num_factors)`.
  - `"lambda1"`: `np.ndarray` of shape `(num_factors, num_factors)`.
  - `"lambda0"`: `np.ndarray` of shape `(num_factors,)`.

### 2.5 `carry_and_roll_down(yield_curve: np.ndarray, maturities: np.ndarray, horizon: float = 1.0) -> dict[str, np.ndarray]`

Decomposes instantaneous and horizon expected bond returns into carry and roll-down.

- **Inputs:**
  - `yield_curve`: 1D `np.ndarray` of shape `(K,)`, spot zero-coupon yields.
  - `maturities`: 1D `np.ndarray` of shape `(K,)`, maturities in years.
  - `horizon`: `float`, holding horizon in years (default 1.0).
- **Definitions:**
  For maturity \(\tau_k\):
  - `carry`: \(\tau_k y(\tau_k) - (\tau_k - \Delta t) y(\tau_k) = \Delta t \cdot y(\tau_k)\).
  - `roll_down`: \((\tau_k - \Delta t) [y(\tau_k) - y(\tau_k - \Delta t)]\) if \(\tau_k > \Delta t\), else `0.0`.
  - `total_carry_roll`: `carry + roll_down`.
  - `annualized_carry_roll`: `total_carry_roll / horizon`.
- **Outputs:**
  `dict` containing:
  - `"carry"`: `np.ndarray` of shape `(K,)`.
  - `"roll_down"`: `np.ndarray` of shape `(K,)`.
  - `"total_carry_roll"`: `np.ndarray` of shape `(K,)`.
  - `"annualized_carry_roll"`: `np.ndarray` of shape `(K,)`.

### 2.6 `curve_trade_pnl_attribution(weights: np.ndarray, maturities: np.ndarray, yields_t0: np.ndarray, yields_t1: np.ndarray, dt: float, financing_rate: float) -> dict[str, float]`

Performs relative-value fixed-income P&L attribution for curve portfolios.

- **Inputs:**
  - `weights`: 1D `np.ndarray` of shape `(M,)`, leg portfolio weights.
  - `maturities`: 1D `np.ndarray` of shape `(M,)`, leg maturities in years.
  - `yields_t0`: 1D `np.ndarray` of shape `(M,)`, initial yields at \(t_0\).
  - `yields_t1`: 1D `np.ndarray` of shape `(M,)`, terminal yields at \(t_1 = t_0 + dt\).
  - `dt`: `float`, time step in years (\(dt > 0\)).
  - `financing_rate`: `float`, repo/financing rate over period.
- **Decomposition:**
  - `carry_pnl`: \(\sum_i w_i (y_{0,i} - \text{financing\_rate}) \cdot dt\)
  - `rolldown_pnl`: \(\sum_i w_i (\tau_i - dt) [y_{0,i} - y_0(\tau_i - dt)]\)
  - `level_pnl`: \(-\sum_i w_i \tau_i \cdot \Delta L\) where \(\Delta L = \frac{1}{M} \sum_i \Delta y_i\)
  - `slope_pnl`: \(-\sum_i w_i \tau_i \cdot (\Delta y_i - \Delta L)\)
  - `curvature_pnl`: 0.0 or butterfly-specific projection
  - `convexity_pnl`: \(\frac{1}{2} \sum_i w_i \tau_i^2 (\Delta y_i)^2\)
  - `total_pnl`: \(\sum_i w_i R_i\) where \(R_i = \exp(-\tau_i \Delta y_i + y_{0,i} dt) - 1\)
  - `residual_pnl`: `total_pnl - (carry_pnl + rolldown_pnl + level_pnl + slope_pnl + convexity_pnl)`
- **Outputs:**
  `dict` mapping each component string to `float`.
