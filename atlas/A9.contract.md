## 1. Mathematical Specifications

### 1.1 Discrete-Event Queue & Execution Lag

In an event-driven backtesting engine, the time index $t \in \{0, 1, \dots, T-1\}$ represents discrete simulation intervals (e.g. daily bars or intraday intervals). At bar $t$, the state of the backtester consists of:
- Cash balance $C_t \in \R$
- Asset position $h_t \in \R$ (shares or contracts)
- Portfolio equity:
  $$E_t = C_t + h_t \cdot P_t$$
  where $P_t > 0$ is the prevailing asset price.

A strategy generates a desired target position $h^*_{t}$ or order signal based strictly on information available up to bar $t$: $\mathcal{F}_t = \sigma(\{P_s, X_s : s \le t\})$.

To eliminate the "trade-on-close" lookahead fallacy, the engine enforces a strictly positive **signal-to-execution lag** $\tau_{\text{lag}} \in \N_{\ge 1}$ (default $\tau_{\text{lag}} = 1$ bar):
- An order generated at bar $t$ is registered with submission bar $t_{\text{sub}} = t$.
- The order cannot be executed before fill bar:
  $$t_{\text{exec}} \ge t_{\text{sub}} + \tau_{\text{lag}}$$
- If $\tau_{\text{lag}} < 1$ or if an order attempts execution at $t_{\text{exec}} \le t_{\text{sub}}$, the engine raises `ValueError` or rejects the execution in strict mode.

When order quantity $q_t = h^*_{t - \tau_{\text{lag}}} - h_{t-1}$ is executed at bar $t$ at price $P_t$, cash updates as:
$$C_t = C_{t-1} - q_t \cdot P_t - \text{Cost}(q_t, P_t)$$
and position updates as:
$$h_t = h_{t-1} + q_t$$

### 1.2 Transaction Cost & Market Impact Model

Execution friction decomposes into linear fees/spreads and non-linear market impact:
$$\text{Cost}(q, P) = \text{Commission}(q, P) + \text{SpreadCost}(q, P) + \text{ImpactCost}(q, P)$$

1. **Brokerage commission and exchange fees:**
   $$\text{Commission}(q, P) = c_{\text{bps}} \times 10^{-4} \cdot |q| \cdot P + c_{\text{fixed}}$$
2. **Bid-ask spread half-cost:**
   $$\text{SpreadCost}(q, P) = \frac{1}{2} s_{\text{bps}} \times 10^{-4} \cdot |q| \cdot P$$
3. **Square-root market impact:**
   Following the empirical square-root law (Toth et al. 2011; Almgren et al. 2005; López de Prado 2018):
   $$\Delta P_{\text{impact}} = \operatorname{sign}(q) \cdot Y \cdot \sigma_t \cdot P_t \cdot \sqrt{\frac{|q|}{V_t}}$$
   where $V_t > 0$ is average daily volume, $\sigma_t > 0$ is asset volatility, and $Y > 0$ is a dimensionless scaling constant (default $Y = 0.5$).
   The realized execution price for the order is:
   $$\tilde{P}_t = P_t + \Delta P_{\text{impact}}$$
   and total impact cost absorbed on the fill is:
   $$\text{ImpactCost}(q, P) = |q| \cdot |\Delta P_{\text{impact}}| = Y \cdot \sigma_t \cdot P_t \cdot \frac{|q|^{1.5}}{\sqrt{V_t}}$$

### 1.3 Walk-Forward Optimization Splits

Walk-forward validation partitions $T$ observations into $K$ contiguous rolling or expanding evaluation windows:
- Training window: $[t_{k,\text{train\_start}}, t_{k,\text{train\_end}}]$
- Testing window: $[t_{k,\text{test\_start}}, t_{k,\text{test\_end}}]$
Strict causality requires:
$$t_{k,\text{train\_end}} < t_{k,\text{test\_start}}$$
and testing windows across folds $k = 1, \dots, K$ are non-overlapping:
$$t_{k,\text{test\_end}} \le t_{k+1,\text{test\_start}}$$
The out-of-sample track record concatenates the realized test returns $r_{\text{OOS}} = [r_{\text{test},1}, r_{\text{test},2}, \dots, r_{\text{test},K}]$.

### 1.4 Purged and Embargoed Cross-Validation

Following López de Prado (2018, Ch. 7), when labels or returns span holding periods of $h_{\text{label}} \ge 1$ bars, standard $k$-fold cross-validation leaks future information because training observations near the fold boundaries overlap in information content with test observations.

Let test fold $k$ span sample indices $[j_{\text{start}}, j_{\text{end}}]$.
1. **Purging:** Any training observation $i$ whose label period $[i, i + h_{\text{label}} - 1]$ overlaps with test evaluation window $[j_{\text{start}}, j_{\text{end}} + h_{\text{label}} - 1]$ is purged (removed) from the training fold:
   $$\text{Purge if } i \le j_{\text{end}} + h_{\text{label}} - 1 \quad \text{and} \quad i + h_{\text{label}} - 1 \ge j_{\text{start}}$$
2. **Embargoing:** To eliminate auto-regressive feature leakage after the test fold, an embargo of duration $h_{\text{embargo}} = \lceil \text{embargo\_pct} \times T \rceil$ bars is applied immediately after $j_{\text{end}}$:
   $$\text{Embargo if } j_{\text{end}} < i \le j_{\text{end}} + h_{\text{embargo}}$$

### 1.5 Combinatorial Purged Cross-Validation (CPCV)

Following López de Prado (2018, Ch. 12), CPCV partitions $T$ observations into $N$ contiguous blocks ($N \ge 2$).
The number of possible test combinations of size $k$ is:
$$\binom{N}{k} = \frac{N!}{k!(N-k)!}$$
For each combination $c \in \binom{N}{k}$, the $k$ chosen blocks form the test set, while the remaining $N-k$ blocks form the training set, subject to pairwise purging and post-test embargoing against each test block.
Each observation is tested across $\varphi = \binom{N-1}{k-1}$ combinations, producing $\binom{N}{k} \frac{k}{N}$ out-of-sample backtest paths.

### 1.6 Planted-Lookahead Test of a Backtester

A planted-lookahead test is an adversarial verification routine that introduces an intentional future information leak into input signals:
$$s_t^{\text{planted}} = \operatorname{sign}(P_{t+\delta} - P_t), \quad \delta \ge 1$$
An honest and sound backtesting architecture must:
1. Block execution when $\tau_{\text{lag}} = 0$ is requested, or
2. Expose an anomalous predictive cross-correlation:
   $$\hat{\rho}(\delta) = \operatorname{Corr}(s_t, r_{t+\delta}) \gg 0$$
   with an inflated Sharpe ratio or $t$-statistic exceeding the statistical threshold under the null:
   $$\mathrm{SR}_{\text{planted}} \ge \mu_{\mathrm{SR}} + z_{1-\alpha} \cdot \sigma_{\mathrm{SR}}$$
If the backtester fails to detect or prevent zero-lag lookahead execution, it fails the verification suite.

### 1.7 Research Integrity Attachment (A8 Consumption)

Every backtest evaluation returning a candidate strategy must attach an `IntegrityReport` computed via Atlas A8 (`atlas.integrity.integrity_report`) from the candidate's trial return matrix $(T, N)$.

## 3. Acceptance Criteria

The QR2 autograder (`course/modules/QR/QR2/grader/test_project.py`) is the acceptance suite for A9. It verifies:
1. Strict lag invariant: any attempt to execute at lag 0 raises `ValueError`.
2. Conservation of cash, shares, and transaction friction on every fill.
3. Market impact scales as $|q|^{1.5} / \sqrt{V}$ under square-root cost model.
4. Walk-forward train/test splits are strictly disjoint with $t_{\text{train}} < t_{\text{test}}$.
5. Purged $k$-fold splits completely exclude overlapping label windows and respect embargo buffers.
6. CPCV partitions yield exactly $\binom{N}{k}$ combinations without index leakage.
7. Planted-lookahead audit flags anomalous Sharpe spikes and positive forward lead correlations.
8. Every returned `BacktestResult` contains an authentic `integrity` report satisfying A8.
