## Mathematical specifications

### 1. Square-Root Impact Law

Given metaorder size $Q$, average daily volume $V > 0$, daily price volatility $\sigma \ge 0$, and dimensionless constant $Y > 0$ (default $Y = 0.5$):
The expected price change induced by order execution is:
$$\Delta p = \operatorname{sign}(Q) \cdot Y \cdot \sigma \cdot \sqrt{\frac{|Q|}{V}}$$
For $Q = 0$, $\Delta p = 0.0$.

Temporary price impact of instantaneous trading rate $v$ with coefficient $\eta \ge 0$ and exponent $\alpha > 0$ (default $\alpha = 1.0$):
$$h(v) = \operatorname{sign}(v) \cdot \eta \cdot |v|^\alpha$$

Permanent price impact of executed volume $Q$ with coefficient $\gamma \ge 0$:
$$g(Q) = \gamma \cdot Q$$

### 2. Almgren–Chriss Optimal Execution Framework

In the discrete-time Almgren & Chriss (2001) model, a trader liquidates $X$ shares over horizon $T$ across $N \ge 1$ intervals of duration $\tau = T / N$ at discrete time steps $t_k = k \tau$, $k = 0, \dots, N$. The trajectory is represented as remaining holdings $x_k$ where $x_0 = X$ and $x_N = 0$.

The trade executed in interval $k$ is $n_k = x_{k-1} - x_k$. The objective minimizes expected cost plus variance penalty with risk aversion parameter $\lambda \ge 0$:
$$\min_{\{x_k\}} U(x) = E[x] + \lambda V[x]$$
where:
$$E[x] = \frac{1}{2} \gamma X^2 + \frac{\eta}{\tau} \sum_{k=1}^N (x_{k-1} - x_k)^2$$
$$V[x] = \sigma^2 \tau \sum_{k=1}^N x_k^2$$

- When $\lambda = 0$ (risk-neutral):
  $$x_k = X \left(1 - \frac{k}{N}\right), \quad k = 0, \dots, N$$
- When $\lambda > 0$:
  Let $\tilde{\kappa} = \frac{1}{\tau} \operatorname{arccosh}\left(1 + \frac{\lambda \sigma^2 \tau^2}{2 \eta}\right)$. The exact discrete solution is:
  $$x_k = X \frac{\sinh(\tilde{\kappa} (T - t_k))}{\sinh(\tilde{\kappa} T)}, \quad k = 0, \dots, N$$

### 3. Efficient Execution Frontier

The efficient execution frontier maps the locus of Pareto-optimal $(V[x], E[x])$ pairs parameterized by $\lambda \ge 0$. As $\lambda \to 0$, expected cost is minimized (linear TWAP) and variance is maximized. As $\lambda \to \infty$, variance is minimized and expected cost is maximized. The frontier is strictly convex: $\frac{d^2 E}{d V^2} > 0$.

### 4. Obizhaeva–Wang Limit Order Book Resilience

In Obizhaeva & Wang (2013), the limit order book has depth density $q$ and exponential resilience rate $\rho \ge 0$. The optimal continuous execution over $[0, T]$ decomposes into:
- Initial discrete block trade at $t = 0$: $x_0 = \frac{X}{2 + \rho T}$
- Steady continuous trading rate on $(0, T)$: $v^* = \frac{\rho X}{2 + \rho T}$
- Terminal discrete block trade at $t = T$: $x_T = \frac{X}{2 + \rho T}$
Conservation of shares guarantees $x_0 + v^* T + x_T = X$.

### 5. Implementation Shortfall Accounting

Following Perold (1988), implementation shortfall measures the difference between paper return and actual return relative to decision price $P_0$. For a target order of $X$ shares (positive for buy, negative for sell) with sign $s = \operatorname{sign}(X)$, arrival price $P_{\text{arr}}$, executed trades $(q_k, P_k)$ totaling $X_{\text{exec}} = \sum q_k$, terminal price $P_T$, and explicit fees:
- Delay cost: $s \cdot X_{\text{exec}} \cdot (P_{\text{arr}} - P_0)$
- Impact cost (realized trading cost): $s \cdot \sum_k q_k (P_k - P_{\text{arr}})$
- Opportunity cost (unexecuted shares): $s \cdot (X - X_{\text{exec}}) \cdot (P_T - P_0)$
- Fees: explicit cash commissions
- Total shortfall: Delay + Impact + Opportunity + Fees
- Shortfall in basis points: $\frac{\text{Total}}{|X| \cdot P_0} \times 10^4$
