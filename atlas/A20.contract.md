## 2. Mathematical Specifications

### 2.1 Duration-Neutral Relative-Value Hedge Ratio
For a two-legged curve or relative-value trade between leg 1 and leg 2 with respective dollar sensitivities \(\text{DV01}_1 > 0\) and \(\text{DV01}_2 > 0\):
$$\beta_{\text{DV01}} = \frac{\text{DV01}_1}{\text{DV01}_2}$$
Given notional or contract position \(N_1\) in leg 1, the duration-neutral hedge position in leg 2 is:
$$N_2 = -N_1 \cdot \beta_{\text{DV01}} = -N_1 \cdot \frac{\text{DV01}_1}{\text{DV01}_2}$$
such that the net DV01 of the spread is zero:
$$\text{DV01}_{\text{spread}} = N_1 \cdot \text{DV01}_1 + N_2 \cdot \text{DV01}_2 = 0$$

### 2.2 Carry, Roll-Down, and Financing Drag
For a fixed-income or cross-asset position held over horizon \(h\) (years):
1. **Gross Carry:**
   $$\text{Carry}_{\text{gross}} = y_{\text{spot}} \cdot h$$
2. **Roll-Down Gain:**
   $$\text{RollDown} = \Delta y_{\text{roll}} \cdot \text{Duration} \cdot h$$
   where \(\Delta y_{\text{roll}}\) is the yield slide along the upward-sloping term structure curve over horizon \(h\).
3. **Financing Drag:**
   $$\text{Drag}_{\text{repo}} = r_{\text{repo}} \cdot h$$
4. **Net Carry:**
   $$\text{Carry}_{\text{net}} = \text{Carry}_{\text{gross}} + \text{RollDown} - \text{Drag}_{\text{repo}}$$

### 2.3 Expression Payoff Convexity
For a portfolio of options and underlying delta-one assets with spot \(S\), strikes \(K_i\), volatilities \(\sigma_i\), time to expiry \(T\), risk-free rate \(r\), and position weights \(w_i\):
1. **Analytical Delta:**
   $$\Delta_{\text{net}} = \sum_{i} w_i \Delta_i$$
2. **Analytical Gamma (Convexity):**
   $$\Gamma_{\text{net}} = \sum_{i} w_i \Gamma_i = \sum_{i} w_i \frac{\phi(d_{1,i})}{S \sigma_i \sqrt{T}}$$
3. **Analytical Vega:**
   $$\mathcal{V}_{\text{net}} = \sum_{i} w_i \mathcal{V}_i = \sum_{i} w_i S \phi(d_{1,i}) \sqrt{T}$$
4. **Theta Decay Drag:**
   $$\Theta_{\text{net}} = \sum_{i} w_i \Theta_i$$

### 2.4 Risk–Reward Payoff Asymmetry
Given an empirical or simulated discrete scenario P&L distribution \(\Delta \Pi = (\Delta \Pi_1, \ldots, \Delta \Pi_M)\):
1. **Expected Upside:**
   $$\mu_+ = \frac{1}{M} \sum_{m=1}^M \max(0, \Delta \Pi_m)$$
2. **Expected Downside:**
   $$\mu_- = \frac{1}{M} \sum_{m=1}^M \max(0, -\Delta \Pi_m)$$
3. **Asymmetry Ratio:**
   $$\mathcal{A} = \begin{cases} \frac{\mu_+}{\mu_-} & \text{if } \mu_- > 0 \\ \infty & \text{if } \mu_- = 0 \text{ and } \mu_+ > 0 \\ 0.0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

### 2.5 Fractional Kelly Position Sizing
Given expected excess return \(\mu\), return variance \(\sigma^2 > 0\), fractional multiplier \(f \in (0, 1]\), and maximum gross leverage cap \(L_{\max} > 0\):
1. **Unconstrained Full Kelly Fraction:**
   $$k^* = \frac{\mu}{\sigma^2}$$
2. **Fractional Sizing with Leverage Cap:**
   $$w_{\text{Kelly}} = \operatorname{sign}(\mu) \cdot \min\left(L_{\max}, \; f \cdot \frac{|\mu|}{\sigma^2}\right)$$

### 2.6 Full Non-Linear Scenario Revaluation Grid
For a grid of spot price relative shocks \(\Delta S / S \in \mathcal{S}\) and volatility shifts \(\Delta \sigma \in \mathcal{V}\):
The scenario P&L matrix \(P \in \R^{|\mathcal{V}| \times |\mathcal{S}|}\) evaluates exact terminal or horizon instrument payoffs:
$$P_{j, k} = \sum_{i} w_i \left[ V_i\left(S(1 + \Delta S_k), \sigma_i + \Delta \sigma_j, T - h\right) - V_i(S, \sigma_i, T) \right]$$
recomputing full Black–Scholes–Merton values rather than linear approximations.
