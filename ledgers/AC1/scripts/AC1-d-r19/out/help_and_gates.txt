== run_graders
usage: run_graders.py [-h] (--unit UNIT | --all) [--seeds SEEDS]

Run a unit's autograder against its reference solution, seed by seed.

For a unit whose project is graded by ``autograder`` or ``both``:
== run_labs
usage: run_labs.py [-h] (--unit UNIT | --all) [--page PAGE]

Run every inline lab locally and hold it to its page.

For each ``<section class="lab" data-runtime="python">`` on a unit's pages:
== page_smoke
usage: page_smoke.py [-h] (--unit UNIT | --all) [--pages [PAGES ...]]
                     [--skip-labs] [--skip-sweep]

Load every page of a unit in Chromium and fail on anything a learner would hit.

== verify_refs
usage: verify_refs.py [-h] [--offline] [--workers WORKERS]
                      [--backfill-print-years] [--unit UNIT]

Re-verify every registry entry against the *current* record that established it.
