M2-c165 L12 None | Definition 1.1: what is the improper integral of f over [a, infinity)? | Hypothesis: f is integrable on [a, b] for every b >= a. The improper integral is the limit of the truncated integrals from a to b as b tends to infinity, when that limit exists and is a finite number. The integral is then convergent; otherwise it is divergent.

M2-c168 L12 T | Theorem 2.2 (power integrals): what is the integral of x^(-p) from 1 to b? | Hypotheses, assuming Assumption 2.1 of Lesson 6: p >= 2 is an integer and b > 1 (b = 1 gives 0 on both sides by convention).
