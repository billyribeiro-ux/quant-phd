Exemplar A
              P&amp;L decomposition
              For the submitted log, replay returns a cash flow of \(-9950\) cents, an ending inventory of 1 unit, an inventory mark of 9980 cents and a P&amp;L of 30 cents. The nine fills are at turns 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 13, 18 and 19 (the fill lines that Lab 1 prints). The purchases by the maker at the bid (turns 2, 3, 4, 8 and 19) cost 49910 cents in total, and the sales at the ask (turns 5, 7, 13 and 18) bring in 39960 cents, so the cash flow is \(39960 - 49910 = -9950\) cents. The inventory after turn 19 is 1 unit, and the mark is \(1 \times 9980 = 9980\) cents.
              Evidence from the log
              Turn 2 bought at the bid of 9990 cents, a fill at 9990. Turn 5 sold at the ask of 9980 cents, a fill at 9980 with inventory 2 after it. Turn 18 sold at the ask of 10000 cents, a fill at 10000 that leaves the inventory at 0.
              Counterfactual
              Lab 3 reruns seed 0 with the half-spread widened from 10 cents to 15 cents, and the arrivals are the same. The fills stay at 9, and the P&amp;L rises from 30 cents to 55 cents, a change of \(+25\) cents on this seed. One seed is one sample, so this is a difference on the game of seed 0, not an estimate for other seeds.
              Assumptions and limits
              The arrival probabilities of 1/4, 1/4 and 1/2 set the thresholds that turn each draw into an arrival. Lab 1 does not vary them, so the printed run does not measure their effect on the fills or the P&amp;L. The inventory limit of 10 units does not bind in this run, whose largest inventory is 3 units (Lab 1 prints it). The model leaves out the queue of resting orders and a trader who learns from the fills (both set aside in Lesson 4), latency, and more than one asset, so it does not describe a real market maker.
              Self-review
              The identity holds for the submitted log: \(30 = -9950 + 1 \times 9980\), and the identity line of Lab 1 prints True. The cash flow also equals the cash after the last turn, which Lab 1 checks on the same log.
