The Financial System, Markets & Instruments

A financial system moves money between savers and borrowers through banks, markets and intermediaries, and an instrument's terms are set either by a venue or by the parties to the trade. This module describes that system in the order a trade experiences it. You will state a stylised bank with its assumptions written out, classify instruments on three axes, separate exchange trading from over-the-counter trading, follow a dealer's spread and a limit order book, and trace one trade from order entry to settlement. The module ends in a cash-instrument engine, graded by an autograder.

Prerequisites: P0.1 How to Study This Program, and E1 Microeconomics. The module uses the geometric series of M1 and the Bertrand-competition concept of E1.

Project: Instrument cash-flow engine (autograded)

50 h lessons · 20 h project (28.6% of the 70 module hours)

Objectives

By the end of FC1 you can:

Compute the total deposits that a stylised bank produces from a reserve ratio, using the closed form, and check the sum in exact arithmetic.

Classify an instrument by exposure, form and venue, and check that each register entry has exactly one value on each axis.

Compute a dealer's spread capture in integer cents, and state the condition (zero inventory at one mid) under which its cash equals the spread times the volume.

Trace how a limit order book matches incoming market and limit orders at price-time priority, and check share conservation after each event.

Trace one trade from order entry to settlement, and state the business-day offset of each state.

Implement a cash-instrument engine to a published interface, and pass the autograder's tests on seeded instances.

Prerequisites

FC1 assumes P0.1 (How to Study This Program) and E1 (Microeconomics), and everything they rest on. It uses these concepts without redefining them:

From E1: Bertrand competition.

From M1, through E1 and P0.1: the geometric series, used in Lesson 1, and the present-value and discount-factor material that the project's pricing uses.

Lesson 1 defines financial intermediary and bank money creation. Lesson 2 defines exchange-traded and over-the-counter markets and the instrument taxonomy.

Placement diagnostic

Nine questions: seven on the prerequisites (M1, E1 and P0.1), and two skill checks, marked as such, on what rungs 1 and 2 of the project ladder below teach. The mastery bar is 80 percent, which for nine items means eight correct, since seven of nine is 78 percent. Skip rungs 1 and 2 when you reach the bar and answer both skill checks. Below the bar, revisit the prerequisites first.

What is the present value at 10 percent of 110 received at the end of one period?

Options
99.00
100.00
121.00

\(110/1.1 = 100\) (M1, present value).

What is the sum \(1 + 1/2 + 1/4\)?

Answer

The first three terms of a geometric series with ratio \(1/2\) sum to \((1-(1/2)^3)/(1/2) = 1.75\) (M1, geometric series).

Demand is \(Q = 100 - P\) and supply is \(Q = P - 20\). Which price clears the market?

Options
60
40
80

Setting \(100 - P = P - 20\) gives \(P = 60\) (E1, competitive equilibrium).

In the market of the previous question, what quantity is traded at the equilibrium price?

Answer

At \(P = 60\), \(Q = 100 - 60 = 40\) (E1, competitive equilibrium).

Which grade does a theorem proved on the page carry?

Options
T
A
C

Grade T is the theorem grade of the evidence standard (P0.1, evidence grade).

A deposit of 1,000 dollars earns 5 percent a year, compounded annually. What is its balance after two years, in dollars?

Answer

\(1{,}000 \times 1.05^2 = 1{,}102.50\) dollars (M1, compounding frequency).

Under the evidence standard, which grade does a finding from a single sample carry?

Options
A
C
T

Grade C is the grade for a working paper, practitioner research or single-sample evidence (P0.1, evidence grade).

Skill check (rung 1). A bond has a face value of 10,000 cents and a coupon of 500 basis points a period. What is the coupon paid at each period, in cents?

Answer

The coupon is \(10000 \times 500/10000 = 500\) cents (the project's coupon rule, in basis points of face value).

Skill check (rung 2). The same bond has three periods and pays its face value at the last period, together with that period's coupon. What is the cash flow at period 3, in cents?

Answer

The period-3 flow is the coupon \(500\) plus the face value \(10000\), which is \(10500\) cents.

Lessons

Five lessons of ten study hours each. Every lesson has graded claims, a runnable lab, a problem set with worked solutions and a retrieval quiz that feeds your review deck. The lessons follow one trade: the bank that lends, the instrument classified, the dealer who quotes, the order that rests in the book, and the trade that settles.

L01 · 10 hMoney, Banks and Financial IntermediariesA stylised bank with its assumptions written out, the deposit multiplier and its closed form, the effect of leakage, and an intermediary's loss buffer.
L02 · 10 hMarkets, Exchanges and the Instrument TaxonomyExchange-traded and over-the-counter trades defined by who fixes the terms, a three-axis instrument taxonomy, and a register check on its counts.
L03 · 10 hDealers, Market Makers and the Bid–Ask SpreadThe dealer's quote, the bid-ask spread, spread capture in integer cents, and the closed-sequence condition under which cash equals the spread times the volume.
L04 · 10 hOrder Types and the Limit Order BookMarket, limit and stop orders, the limit order book, and a seeded price-time-priority match with share conservation checked after each event.
L05 · 10 hClearing, Settlement and the Trade LifecycleOne seeded trade traced from order entry through matching, clearing and settlement, with each state and its business-day offset printed.

The project ladder

The module ends in the instrument cash-flow engine. Rungs 1 and 2 scaffold the skills the placement check tests. You may skip them when you reach the placement bar and answer both skill checks.

Worked example
Lab 1 on the project page: a fully solved set of SIMULATED cash instruments, with the reasoning printed beside each step, including the coupon cash flow of a bond.

Completion problem
Lab 2 on the project page: the discount exponent and the trade's cash flow, with its two blanks. Its output must match the expected output when the blanks are right.

Independent project
Implement the cash-instrument engine to the project specification. The autograder checks it on seeded SIMULATED instances, and your workspace seed generates your instances. Project page.

Stretch
An extension of your own design, with a stated hypothesis and a check on your own generated instances. It is not graded.

Data status

No dataset is used. No FC1 page embeds a dataset or reads a market data file. Each lab uses typed-in inputs or seeded generated inputs, and each generated input is labelled SIMULATED on the page where it appears. The bank in Lesson 1, the register in Lesson 2, and the examples of each lesson are typed-in models, not market observations. When a lesson quiz is finished, its page adds that lesson's cards from cards.json to your review deck.
