+          <li><strong>P6 · Cost model.</strong> Quotes are mid prices. A leg is one instrument that a finding buys or sells. The fee is the charge per contract that a leg pays to trade, in cents (the input <code>fee_cents_per_contract</code>). The half-spread is half of a leg's bid–ask spread, the cost of crossing from the mid to the market on that leg. A finding trades three legs, each leg pays the fee and the half-spread, and so a finding costs 3 × (fee + half-spread) cents. A parity finding trades the call, the put and the underlying. A convexity finding trades three calls.</li>
