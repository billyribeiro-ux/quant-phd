M3-c070 L04 T  
  F: What does the spectral theorem for symmetric matrices state? 
  B: Assuming F1 to F4, for a real symmetric n x n matrix A, R^n has an orthonormal basis q_1, …, q_n with A q_j = lambda_j q_j for real lambda_j. With Q the matrix of these columns and Lambda the diagonal matrix of the lambda_j, Q is orthogonal and A = Q Lambda Q^T. The hypotheses are A real and symmetric, F1 to F4, and the inner product of Definition 1.2 (Theorem 2.3).
