E5-c001 L01 None | F: What is gross domestic product (GDP)? | B: The market value of all final goods and services produced within the economy in the period. E5-c002 L01 None | F: What are the national accounts? | B: The system of GDP definitions, applied so that each transaction in the period is counted once (Definition 1.1). | BASIS: None

E5-c003 L01 None | F: How are net exports defined? | B: NX = X - M, where X is exports and M is imports, all in one period and one currency. Consumption, investment, government purchases, exports and imports are the expenditure components (Definition 1.2). | BASIS: None

The identity is a property of the definitions and says nothing about the size of any component. | BASIS: Theorem 1.4

E5-c005 L01 None | F: What is the operating surplus on the income side? | B: The balancing entry: expenditure-side GDP minus compensation of employees, taxes less subsidies on production, and consumption of fixed capital (Definition 1.5). The income identity therefore holds by construction and is a check on the arithmetic of a table, not an economic result. | BASIS: None

E5-c006 L01 None | F: What is nominal GDP in period t? | B: The sum over goods of p(i,t) times q(i,t), valued at the prices of that period (Definition 1.6). | BASIS: None

E5-c007 L01 None | F: What is real GDP at base period 0? | B: The sum over goods of p(i,0) times q(i,t), which holds prices at their base-period values (Definition 1.6). Proposition 2.6 uses the deflator form instead. | BASIS: None

E5-c008 L01 None | F: What is a deflator? | B: A price index D(t) > 0 with D(0) = 1. Real GDP can be defined as nominal GDP divided by the deflator (Definition 1.6). | BASIS: None

E5-c009 L01 T | F: Under what hypotheses does closed-economy saving equal investment? | B: (A0) Assumption 1.3 holds. (A1) The economy is closed, so X = M = 0 and NX = 0. E5-c024 L02 None | F: What is the Cobb-Douglas production function? | B: Y = A K^alpha L^(1 - alpha), where A is a positive productivity level, K and L are positive capital and labour inputs, and 0 < alpha < 1 is the exponent on capital (Definition 1.1). E5-c025 L02 None | F: What are log growth and simple growth of a positive series? | B: Log growth in period t is ln x(t) - ln x(t-1). Simple growth is x(t)/x(t-1) - 1 (Definition 1.2). The decomposition in Theorem 1.4 uses log growth. | BASIS: None

E5-c027 L02 T | F: What does constant returns to scale mean for the Cobb-Douglas function? | B: Under (A1) A > 0, K > 0, L > 0, 0 < alpha < 1 and lambda > 0, A(lambda K)^alpha (lambda L)^(1 - alpha) = lambda A K^alpha L^(1 - alpha).
