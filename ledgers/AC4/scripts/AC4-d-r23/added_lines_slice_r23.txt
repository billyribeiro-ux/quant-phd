+      <p>The term \(D(p_m - v)/q\) is the opportunity cost per internal unit, averaged over the \(q\) internal units. It is the outside contribution of the displaced units, spread over the internal units. When spare capacity absorbs the order (\(D = 0\)), the opportunity cost per internal unit is zero and \(t_{\min} = v\). When every internal unit displaces one outside unit (\(D = q\)), the opportunity cost per internal unit is \(p_m - v\) and \(t_{\min} = p_m\). With \(q > 0\) and \(q \le K\) (Lemma 7.2) and \(E \le K\) (§1), \(D \le q\), so with \(p_m \ge v\) the lowest price never exceeds \(p_m\) (P2).</p>
