### E3-c078 | L05 | grade=None | basis=None
F: What is the two-period Epstein-Zin value of a plan?
B: V0 = [(1 - beta) c0^a + beta (E V1^(1 - gamma))^(a/(1 - gamma))]^(1/a), with a = 1 - 1/psi and terminal value V1 = c1 (Definition 1.1). Assumptions: 0 < beta < 1, psi > 0 and psi not 1, gamma not 1, and positive consumption.
### E3-c079 | L05 | grade=None | basis=None
F: What is the period-1 certainty equivalent CE1?
B: CE1 = (E c1^(1 - gamma))^(1/(1 - gamma)), the CRRA certainty equivalent of the date-1 lottery. It satisfies CE1^(1 - gamma) = E c1^(1 - gamma) (Remark 1.2).
### E3-c080 | L05 | grade=T | basis=None
F: How does the date-1 lottery enter the Epstein-Zin value?
B: Only through CE1: V0 = [(1 - beta) c0^a + beta CE1^a]^(1/a). For fixed c0, beta, gamma and psi, V0 is strictly increasing in CE1 (Theorem 1.3, under A1 to A4).
### E3-c081 | L05 | grade=T | basis=None
F: What is the value of a risky date-1 plan?
B: Under A1 to A4, it equals the value of the sure plan whose date-1 consumption is CE1 (Corollary 1.4), not the value at its mean (section 5, third misconception).
### E3-c082 | L05 | grade=T | basis=None
F: Does the mean of the date-1 lottery enter the Epstein-Zin value?
B: No. Under A1 to A4 at gamma = 2, the value depends on the lottery through CE1, which depends on the whole distribution of c1, not only on its mean, so two lotteries with the same mean can have different values (Remark 1.5).
### E3-c083 | L05 | grade=None | basis=None
F: What is the elasticity of intertemporal substitution?
B: For deterministic plans, eps = d ln(c1/c0) / d ln MRS, where MRS = (dV0/dc0)/(dV0/dc1) is the marginal rate of substitution (Definition 2.1).
### E3-c084 | L05 | grade=T | basis=None
F: What is the EIS of the two-period Epstein-Zin form?
B: psi. Under A1 to A4 for deterministic plans, MRS = ((1 - beta)/beta)(c1/c0)^(1/psi), so the elasticity equals psi (Proposition 2.2).
### E3-c085 | L05 | grade=T | basis=None
F: Does risk aversion gamma change the EIS or the value of a deterministic plan?
B: No. Under A1 to A4 for deterministic plans, and for gamma not equal to 1 (the values A3 allows), CE1 = c1, so V0 depends on c0, c1, beta and psi only, and the EIS is psi for every such gamma (Remark 2.3, Proposition 2.2).
### E3-c086 | L05 | grade=T | basis=None
F: When is theta = (1 - gamma)/(1 - 1/psi) equal to 1?
B: Under A1 to A4, theta = 1 exactly when psi = 1/gamma (Remark 3.1).
### E3-c087 | L05 | grade=T | basis=None
F: When is the Epstein-Zin value a power transform of CRRA expected utility?
B: When psi = 1/gamma, with gamma not 0 (so gamma > 0 by A2), under A1 to A4: (1 - gamma) U = V0^(1 - gamma), where U = (1 - beta) u(c0) + beta E u(c1) and u(c) = c^(1 - gamma)/(1 - gamma) (Theorem 3.2).
### E3-c088 | L05 | grade=T | basis=None
F: Under psi = 1/gamma, do the two criteria rank plans the same way?
B: Under A1 to A4 and psi = 1/gamma with gamma not 0, V0 is a strictly increasing function of U, so V0(X) >= V0(Y) exactly when U(X) >= U(Y) (Corollary 3.3).
### E3-c089 | L05 | grade=T | basis=None
F: What are the MRS exponents of the two criteria off the restriction?
B: On deterministic plans under A1 to A4, CRRA expected utility has MRS = ((1 - beta)/beta)(c1/c0)^gamma, and Epstein-Zin has ((1 - beta)/beta)(c1/c0)^(1/psi). They agree at every deterministic plan exactly when 1/psi = gamma (Remark 3.4).
### E3-c090 | L05 | grade=T | basis=None
F: Does the disagreement between the criteria in Lab 1 contradict Corollary 3.3?
B: No. Corollary 3.3 needs psi = 1/gamma, with A1 to A4. Off that restriction the two criteria can assign different marginal rates of substitution (Remark 3.4).
### E3-c091 | L05 | grade=T | basis=None
F: On deterministic plans, what is the elasticity of the consumption ratio with respect to the MRS for CRRA expected utility?
B: Under A1 to A4, 1/gamma for gamma not 0 (Remark 3.4); at gamma = 0 it is undefined. Epstein-Zin gives psi instead (Proposition 2.2).
